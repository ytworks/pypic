## Product

**Goal** (owner request 2026-11-09; roadmap `docs/roadmap.md`): a GPU-accelerated, multi-GPU,
3-D special-relativistic electromagnetic PIC engine for plasma experiments.

- Two schemes, switchable: explicit (relativistic Buneman–Boris, Esirkepov, Yee; Gauss and
  div B to round-off) and implicit (Crank–Nicolson + implicit midpoint; total energy and
  charge conserved exactly). Cubic (CIC) macro-particles.
- Boundaries per face: periodic, open, reflecting. Species: electron, ion (mass ratio, Z),
  positron; e-i / e-p / e-i-p mixtures, charge neutral by construction.
- Experiments are set up from physics (linear theory → box, grid, dt, run length); first
  example: 3-D two-stream instability.
- Hardware: 8× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (SM120), single node, NCCL; CPU-only CI on GitHub.

### 2026-11-11 — [design] Absorbing sphere (PIC-D14): an insulating body that keeps the absorbed charge
- What: `body: {center, radius, relax}`: a particle that ends a step inside the sphere is removed after its path was deposited; its charge stays at the nodes of the body, which the Gauss check leaves out (`radius + (1 + reach)·|dx|`). Particles loaded inside are dropped by one collective migration. New history keys `absorbed_kinetic`, `absorbed_charge`, `n_absorbed`; kernel constants `BODY_*`. A dipole now needs a body that covers its core. Explicit only. Measured in the gate wind (3.9e8 particles, 8 GPUs, 5761 steps, criteria fixed before the runs): with the gate dipole the charge levels (last crossing 0.043 max|Q|, passed); without a dipole it does not (0.299, failed). `body.relax` (E damped on the edges inside the sphere), built by the rule fixed for that failure, fails in both configurations (0.386 without, 0.112 with the dipole). Gauss 3–4e-14 and upstream within 0.11 % in all four.
- Why: a charge-conserving deposit leaves the charge of a removed particle on the grid; keeping it and masking the nodes needs no Poisson correction and no work per step. The bow-shock example runs the insulating body (the dipole case passed); `body.relax` stays an option, off by default and documented as not for use with a dipole.
- Refs: PIC-D14, PIC-D12 (`docs/design/engine.md`); plan Phase 4 (`docs/plans/2026-11-10-bow-shock.md`); `pypic/body.py`, `pypic/kernels/particles.cu`, `pypic/simulation.py`; `tests/cpu/test_body.py`, `tests/gpu/test_engine.py`, `tests/gpu/mgpu_open_flow.py`; `.claude/rules/numerics-invariants.md` (checked region). Gates not yet run on this commit. Open: the charge of an absorber without a dipole; mechanism not identified.

### 2026-11-11 — [design] Filter with open faces and Marder zone (PIC-D15, PIC-D16); null test M2 passed
- What: the deposit is cut at every open face (a leaving particle leaves its charge on the face nodes), the face is the filter's wall, and `marder_zone` diffuses `div E − ρ` of the layer, the face node and `reach + 1` nodes inside to the conducting wall. The Gauss check retreats `passes + compensate` nodes at an open face (+1 with the zone). M2 (uniform magnetised wind, 240×320×320, 3.9e8 particles, 8 GPUs, 5760 steps, criteria fixed before the run) passed for two geometries: Gauss 3e-14, density within 1.25 %, velocity within 0.84 % of |v|, interior T_e ≤ 1.024 T0. Cost: filter + zone 40.0–41.2 ms/step against 31.5–32.6 with injection alone (one gate rank).
- Why: Phase 0 showed that an open flow with a filter is stationary only with all three (cut, face wall, zone); the CPML does not relax the static field of the charge left at a face. The zone changes E only outside the checked region, so charge conservation there still comes from the deposit; `.claude/rules/numerics-invariants.md` now states the checked region and this limit.
- Refs: PIC-D15, PIC-D16, PIC-D13 (`docs/design/engine.md`); plan Phase 3 (`docs/plans/2026-11-10-bow-shock.md`); `pypic/grid.py`, `pypic/kernels/{fields,particles}.cu`, `pypic/simulation.py`; `tests/gpu/test_engine.py`, `tests/gpu/mgpu_open_flow.py`. Bits of open-face runs change (the cut). Gates not yet run on this commit. Open: the oblique case's magnetic energy had not levelled after three crossings; zone depth not varied.
