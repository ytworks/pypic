## Product

**Goal** (owner request 2026-11-09; roadmap `docs/roadmap.md`): a GPU-accelerated, multi-GPU,
3-D special-relativistic electromagnetic PIC engine for plasma experiments.

- Two schemes, switchable: explicit (relativistic Buneman–Boris, Esirkepov, Yee; Gauss and
  div B to round-off) and implicit (Crank–Nicolson + implicit midpoint; total energy and
  charge conserved exactly). Cubic (CIC) macro-particles.
- Boundaries per face: periodic, open, reflecting. Species: electron, ion (mass ratio, Z),
  positron; e-i / e-p / e-i-p mixtures, charge neutral by construction.
- Experiments are set up from physics (linear theory → box, grid, dt, run length); first
  example: 3-D two-stream instability.
- Hardware: 8× RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (SM120), single node, NCCL; CPU-only CI on GitHub.

### 2026-11-11 — [design] Injection at open faces (PIC-D13): `flux` and `paired` faces (roadmap R1)
- What: every step the cell layer outside an injecting face is sampled like the loader (keys: seed, step, global cell, slot) and the samples that end inside the box enter. `flux`: each species on its own, path deposited. `paired` (wind inflow face): electrons are returned at the face instead of leaving, each ion enters with an electron born at the same point, and an ion that left is paid for by the next ions entering in its face column. `PAIRED_FACES` is a kernel constant. New history keys `lost_charge`, `injected_kinetic`, `injected_charge`, `n_injected`, `fill`; `capacity_factor`; planner memory estimate with 8 rows. Flux within 5σ of quadrature, bank identity to 1e-9, 1 GPU vs 2×2 ranks 1e-10. Step time without injection unchanged (23.05 vs 23.06, 25.62 vs 25.61 ms/step); with five injecting faces 29.3–29.5 ms/step at 4.9e7 particles, 2.0 ms of it the injection. M2 (null test of open flow) is judged at the end of Phase 3.
- Why: Phase 0 showed that a fast inflow face needs charge-neutral pairs (flux injection settles in a source sheath) and a thermal face the plain flux (the paired rule gains density); unfiltered open flow at the gate parameters does not stay stationary, so M2 needs the Phase 3 features.
- Refs: PIC-D13 (`docs/design/engine.md`), roadmap R1; `pypic/injection.py`, `pypic/kernels/particles.cu` (`inject_face`, `inject_partners`), `pypic/simulation.py`; `tests/cpu/test_injection.py`, `tests/gpu/test_engine.py`, `tests/gpu/mgpu_open_flow.py`; plan Phase 2

### 2026-11-11 — [design] External static fields (PIC-D12): the Yee arrays hold the difference field
- What: `external_fields` (uniform E, uniform B, regularised dipoles) are felt by the particles on top of the Yee fields; field solver, CPML, walls and deposition are unchanged. The dipole is the discrete curl of `m × r / (r² + a²)^(3/2)` from global indices (div B 8.9e-16, decomposition independent). Kernel `ext_sum` builds the gather arrays; outputs and tracks hold the total field (`run.json: field_convention`), the history energies stay those of the Yee fields. Explicit only; not together with `B0` / `E0`. Orbits equal bit for bit those in the same fields given as grid fields; 1 GPU vs 2×2 ranks 1e-10; step cost +0.5 to +0.9 % (FP64), +0.3 to +0.4 % (`mixed`) at 4.9e7 particles.
- Why: a dipole and the solar wind's B and −v×B cross every face of an open box; as Yee fields the wall behind the absorbing layer changes them and the layer absorbs them.
- Refs: PIC-D12 (`docs/design/engine.md`), `docs/design/output-analysis.md`; `pypic/external.py`, `pypic/simulation.py`, `pypic/kernels/particles.cu`; `tests/cpu/test_external.py`, `tests/gpu/test_engine.py`, `tests/gpu/test_output.py`; plan Phase 1
### 2026-11-11 — [design] Bow-shock plan: open-boundary design fixed by the Phase 0 measurements
- What: plan for `examples/bow_shock/` and its engine features (external static fields + dipole, injection at open faces, absorbing sphere, filter with open faces). A scratch probe on the unchanged engine (128×32×32, up to 6e4 steps) fixed three points: an open face needs the deposit cut at the face, the filter's wall at the box face and a Marder zone (layer + face + passes + 2 nodes) together; the wind inflow face injects charge-neutral pairs and the side faces a fixed flux; ions leaving through the paired face are paid for by the next entering ions.
- Why: a relaxation of E in the layer had no effect at any rate, and each of the three measures alone failed with a filter; flux injection at u / v_te = 2.5 settled in a source sheath (n 1.51 at the face, potential range 17 T0) while the paired face stayed stationary for 6e4 steps, and the reverse held for faces with a thermal normal velocity (paired: density +9 % in 2e4 steps).
- Refs: `docs/plans/2026-11-10-bow-shock.md` (run table); roadmap R1, R6; PIC-D12..D16 (not yet written)
