# 3-D bow shock example and the engine features it needs
Status: In progress — Phases 0 (measurement), 1 (PIC-D12) and 2 (PIC-D13) done; Phases 3–5 open
Refs: roadmap R1, R6; PIC-D12..D16 (each written to `docs/design/engine.md` by its phase)

## Context

The owner asked (2026-11-10) for an example that produces a 3-D bow shock and for the engine
features it needs: a sphere with a dipole magnetic field as the obstacle (a planet), a continuous
supply of solar-wind particles upstream, removal of particles downstream and damping of the fields
near the boundaries, with the design chosen after a survey of prior work.

The engine has: removal at `open` faces (full path deposited, then removed), CPML (explicit only),
the drifting Maxwell–Jüttner loader, the binomial filter, 3-D NCCL decomposition. It lacks:
injection (R1), external static fields, a body, an internal absorber. In the way: `E0` rejects
components tangential to a non-periodic face, `apply_bc_B` zeroes the normal B at walls, and the
filter rejects `open` faces.

Target: `examples/bow_shock/` with a gate configuration (minutes on 8 GPUs) and a production
configuration (about 2 hours), reproducing a stationary bow shock and magnetopause under the
existing contracts (Gauss ≤ 1e-10, div B ≤ 1e-12, 1 vs N GPUs ≤ 1e-10).

Prior work used: Omidi et al. (a shock needs Dp = magnetopause distance / d_i ≳ 1); Lavorenti et
al. 2022 (iPIC3D, Mercury, Dp ≈ 8, absorbing surface, fields relaxed to the solar-wind values);
OSIRIS (Cruz 2022), WarpX, Smilei (the dipole is an external field felt by the particles);
TRISTAN (pairs injected at one position; a charge-conserving deposit leaves charge at a removal
face); Daughton 2006 / WarpX (flux injection); Marder 1987 (diffusion of the Gauss residual).

## Approach

All features are explicit-only and rejected at construction with the implicit scheme.

- **D-A External static fields (PIC-D12).** The Yee arrays hold the difference fields; particles
  feel difference + external. External E is uniform; external B is uniform + dipoles, each the
  discrete curl of a regularised vector potential A = m×r/(r²+a²)^{3/2}, so the discrete div B is
  exactly 0. Maxwell update, CPML and wall conditions are unchanged; the CPML then absorbs only
  the difference to the solar-wind fields. Outputs `Ex..Bz` return the total field.
- **D-B Particle injection at open faces (PIC-D13, closes R1).** Two modes per face, chosen from
  the Phase 0 measurements below. `flux`: every step a one-cell shell outside the face is sampled
  from the species' own beams and moved for one step; particles that end inside are kept. `paired`
  (zero net charge): only the ions are sampled this way, each brings one electron born at the same
  point with a velocity from the inward flux of the electron distribution, and electrons that
  reach the face from inside are returned. Random keys are (seed, step, global shell cell, slot).
- **D-C Marder zone at open faces (PIC-D16).** `E += c·dx·∇(div E − filtered ρ)` on the edges
  whose two nodes lie in the zone: the CPML layer, the face node and `passes + 2` nodes inside.
  The conducting wall behind the layer absorbs the residual. Replaces the relaxation layer of the
  first draft (see Phase 0).
- **D-D Absorbing sphere (PIC-D14).** Particles ending inside are removed after the deposit (the
  charge stays in the sphere: a floating insulator); particles loaded inside are removed at start;
  nodes within R + √3·dx of the centre are left out of the Gauss check.
- **D-E Filter with open faces (PIC-D15, amends PIC-D10).** The filter treats an open face as a
  wall at the box face (its existing wall rules), and the particle current beyond an open face is
  not deposited (the path is cut at the face). The Gauss check retreats to the edge of the zone.
- **D-F Diagnostics and capacity.** History keys for injected / lost / absorbed charge, energy and
  counts and the fill ratio, on the existing reduction; `SimConfig.capacity_factor`.
- **Example.** Wind along +x; x− and the four side faces inject, x+ only removes. IMF +z, dipole
  moment −z. Gate: m_i/m_e = 16, ω_pe/Ω_ce = 4, M_A = 4 (v_sw = 0.25c), dx = 0.5, dt = 0.25,
  v_te = 0.1c, Dp = 5 (R_mp = 40 cells), 240×320×320, 8 ppc, 3 crossings. Production: m_i/m_e = 100,
  M_A = 5 (v_sw = 0.125c), about 600³. Gate criteria (fixed before the first gate run): Gauss
  ≤ 1e-10, upstream density and u_x stationary within 5 % over the last crossing, sheath
  compression ≥ 2 on the subsolar line, magnetopause within 25 % of pressure balance, shock
  upstream of the magnetopause.

Phases (one branch each): 0 measurement; 1 D-A; 2 D-B + D-F and the null test M2 (a magnetised
oblique uniform flow through five injecting faces stays uniform); 3 D-E + D-C; 4 D-D; 5 the
example and gate M3 (registered in `scripts/gpu_gates.py`).

Amendment (Phase 2, 2026-11-11): the verdict of M2 moves to the end of Phase 3. Run `m1` below shows
that unfiltered open flow at the gate parameters does not stay stationary, and the filter with open
faces and the Marder zone arrive in Phase 3. Phase 2 verifies the injection on deterministic
observables instead: the entering flux, the edge cells, the charge balance of the paired face and
1-GPU against N-GPU equality (PIC-D13, Measured).

cut of the deposit, wall-type filter, Marder zone and face rules are emulated between steps.

Also measured: Marder number 0.03 (`prf4mw03`), filter 2 (`prf2mw`), u = 0.125 (`pruf4mw`) and
m_i = 100 (`pr4m100`) give the `prf4mw` state; without the wall-type filter the filter-2 run fails
by 6e4 steps (`prf2m6`); unfiltered runs heat numerically (periodic: T_e,x 82 without a filter,
2.0 with filter 4 after 2e4 steps in a closed box).

What the measurements decide (each against the first draft of the plan):

1. **D-C is the Marder zone, not a relaxation layer.** Relaxing E in the layer changed nothing at
   any rate, with or without the filter (`iso*`, `nrm*`, `prf4r`); the zone did (`prf4mw`). This is
   the alternative the draft named for that outcome.
2. **D-E: the filter's wall is the box face, and the deposit is cut there.** With a filter, neither
   the zone alone (`prf4m`, `prf2m6`) nor the wall-type filter alone (`prf4w`) is stable; the
   uncut deposit is worse (`prf4mn`). All three together are stationary for 6e4 steps.
3. **D-B: the wind inflow face is `paired`; the side faces are `flux`.** Flux injection of a wind
   with u / v_te = 2.5 settles in a source-sheath state (`cb4`; not at u / v_te = 1.25, `cb4u`),
   while the paired face is stationary for 6e4 steps at both speeds (`zt46`, `ztu`). Where the
   normal velocity is thermal the order is reversed: flux is clean with and without a tangential
   drift (`thb4`, `tyb4`) and the paired rule accumulates density (`zt0b`, `tyz`).
4. **Ions that leave through a paired face are paid for by the next entering ions,** which enter
   without their electron in that face column (`zbw`, `zbw6`). Letting an electron leave instead
   keeps a larger debt standing (`zbl`: −2233 macro-charges over the 1024 face columns, −24 in the
   worst one, against −611 and −1.9), with a face charge of 0.12 instead of 0.03. T_e,x ≈ 3 T0 in
   M1b is the electron–beam instability, present in the periodic reference too (`bpf4`), not a
   boundary effect.
5. The heating excursion of `prf4mw6` came from the reflecting-wall pair set-up of the probe (it
   sits in the inflow quarter and is absent with the open paired face, `zt46`).

Not established: the mechanisms (the source sheath of the flux face, the density growth of the
paired thermal face) are not identified. The probe is one-dimensional in effect and unmagnetised;
magnetised oblique flow through five faces, and the edges where a `paired` and a `flux` face meet,
are first tested by M2. The depth of the zone (`passes + 2`) was not varied. The cost of the zone
residual in the engine (ρ is needed in the zone every step) and whether the filter needs a zone at
the sphere are open until Phases 3 and 4.

## Invariants

- Gauss / div B: external E is uniform and external B a discrete curl, so both contracts hold for
  the difference fields. In the checked region charge conservation comes from the deposit alone.
  The Marder zone changes E only on edges between zone nodes, all outside the checked region; it
  is the boundary condition for the charge of removed and entering particles, which no deposit
  balances. `.claude/rules/numerics-invariants.md` states the checked region in Phase 3.
- 1 vs N GPUs ≤ 1e-10: injection keys are (seed, step, global cell, slot), the dipole is computed
  from global indices, and every new collective (injected counts, capacity overflow, the migrate
  after the body exclusion) is reached by every rank.
- Periodic and reflecting cases keep their bits (loader random streams; kernels without
  `BODY_SPHERE`). The cut of the deposit changes the bits of the open-face cases only
  (`tests/gpu/mgpu_regress.py`, `tests/gpu/mgpu_case.py`, `tests/gpu/test_engine.py`); no example
  uses an open face today.
- The implicit scheme is not touched.

## Verification

- CPU (CI): `ruff check .`, `python scripts/check_kernels.py`, `pytest tests/cpu` (discrete div B
  of the dipole, rejection rules, planner, `theory.py`).
- GPU tests, fixed seeds, judged on observables: external field equals the same field on the grid
  (particle orbits); outputs are total fields; injected flux against the drifting-Maxwellian value
  within 5σ; Gauss exact inside while injecting; an edge shell cell is sampled once; a uniform
  flow through an open box stays uniform (M1), also with ions leaving upstream (M1b); the sphere
  absorbs and books the charge; neutral after the body exclusion; Gauss with filter and open
  faces; the zone keeps Gauss in the checked region; `tests/gpu/mgpu_open_flow.py` in four
  variants (inject, inject_filter, body, mixed) for 1 vs N GPUs ≤ 1e-10.
- End of each phase: `pytest tests/gpu` and `python scripts/gpu_gates.py --gpus 8` on a clean
  commit, recorded in `bench/results/` and `index.json`, `PROGRESS.md` updated.
- Final: `python examples/bow_shock/run.py configs/gate.yaml`, `analyze.py` reports `passed`; the
  production configuration is run once to record performance and the fill ratio.

## Out of scope

Implicit scheme; closing the energy budget with the work of the external field; tracking of
injected particles; restart (R2 — the production run is planned as one run of about 2 hours).
