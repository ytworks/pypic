## Claim
ISEE observations: electrons of >= 16 keV leave the bow shock "in a thin sheet which lies just behind the
sheet of interplanetary magnetic field lines that are tangent to the shock surface"; lower-energy
electrons leave over a much broader region.

## Method
- Observation (ISEE).

## Numbers to compare against
- >= 16 keV; the sheet is nearly always present.

## Assessment
- For this topic: the earliest source found for "the most energetic reflected electrons come from just
  behind the tangent field line".
