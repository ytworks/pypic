# Bai et al. (2023): Electron Dynamics and Whistler-Mode Waves Inside the Short Large-Amplitude Magnetic Field Structures

- Ref: JGR Space Physics 128(11), e2023JA031816; DOI 10.1029/2023JA031816 (12 authors: Bai, Shi, Zhang,
  Guo, Shen, Liu, Degeling, Tian, ...). Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Inside a SLAMS upstream of Earth's quasi-parallel bow shock, electrons gain energy by betatron
acceleration while the structure steepens, and generate whistler-mode waves ahead of the field peak. Behind
the peak the electrons lose energy to the convection electric field during gradient drift and the waves
decay.

## Method
- MMS observations of one SLAMS; test-particle simulations.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Abstract only; the electron energies involved are not stated there.
- For this topic: an in situ account of what one SLAMS does to electrons at Earth. Gain ahead of the peak
  and loss behind it describes a reversible, adiabatic change, not a net acceleration to high energy;
  compare the trapping picture proposed for Saturn (masters2013electron, masters2017situ).
