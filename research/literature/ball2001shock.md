# Ball & Melrose (2001): Shock drift acceleration of electrons

- Ref: Publ. Astron. Soc. Aust. 18, 361–373 (2001); DOI 10.1071/AS01047; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
A review of shock drift acceleration, "developing the theory in detail for gyrophase-averaged particles".
It shows how the upstream and downstream velocity spaces "separate into different regions according to the
interaction of the particles with the shock (reflection, transmission, head-on, overtaking)".

## Method
- Analytic theory. The effects of the cross-shock electric field and of the magnetic overshoot are
  discussed. "The effectiveness of acceleration is estimated for Maxwellian and power law distributions."

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.
- Result on the upstream beam: "The condition for a beam instability to be generated by reflected
  particles is determined and found to be independent of the distribution function for isotropic
  inflowing electrons."

## Assessment
- For this topic: a reference for the plane-shock theory of mirror reflection and transmission, with the
  cross-shock electric field and the overshoot included. It can serve as the plane-shock baseline for a
  curved front.
- Curvature of the shock is not in the abstract.
