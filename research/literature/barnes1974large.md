# Barnes & Hollweg (1974): Large-amplitude hydromagnetic waves

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. 79(16), 2302-2318 (1974) (Crossref); DOI 10.1029/JA079i016p02302; no arXiv;
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence. No abstract or full text could be
  reached (AGU/Wiley 403, ADS 405, Crossref and EarthRef carry no abstract). The content below is the
  result as stated by citing works, named per item; it is not read from the paper itself.

## Claim
(As cited.) Ideal MHD admits an exact nonlinear Alfvenic solution of arbitrary amplitude: any
perturbation with constant pressure, constant density and constant |B|, and with
delta u = +- delta B / sqrt(4 pi rho), propagates at the Alfven speed without steepening. Large-amplitude
oblique waves of this kind are arc-polarized (the tip of B moves on an arc of a sphere).

## Method
Analytic, ideal (and per the title, hydromagnetic) theory. Details of the derivation: not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- Conditions for the exact solution, as quoted in Eq. (1.1) of a citing J. Plasma Phys. paper found in
  the search (attributed to Squire & Mallet by the search summary; author not checked):
  P = const, rho = const, |B|^2 = const, delta u = +- delta B/sqrt(4 pi rho).
- Related exact 3-D packets in relativistic MHD: arXiv:2105.14126 (not read).
- Because |B| = const couples terms linear and quadratic in delta B, a monochromatic oblique wave of
  finite amplitude is impossible (as stated in a citing work in the same search); a single circularly
  polarized parallel wave is the special case that is monochromatic.

## Assessment
- Design use for us: the exact |B| = const state is the natural initial condition for a large-amplitude
  Alfvenic upstream that is an MHD equilibrium (no initial compressive transient). It is exact only in
  ideal MHD; in a kinetic plasma it is unstable to parametric decay and modulational instabilities
  (see matteini2010kinetics, gonzalez2023particle) and is modified by dispersion at k d_i ~ 1.
- For a spectrum (turbulence) the |B| = const constraint cannot be met by superposing random-phase
  modes; it needs a constructive method (e.g. the 3-D packets of arXiv:2105.14126, or field-line
  construction). Not covered here.
- Limit of this note: the original equations, the treatment of compressive/dispersive cases and any
  stability statement were not read. Re-read the paper before quoting a formula from it.
