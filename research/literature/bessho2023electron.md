# Bessho, Chen, Hesse, Ng, Wilson & Stawarz (2023): Electron Acceleration and Heating during Magnetic Reconnection in the Earth's Quasi-parallel Bow Shock

- Ref: ApJ 954, 25; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ace321. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only, together with the abstracts of the two follow-ups (ApJ 2024,
  DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ad7678; ApJ 2025, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae0327); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
In a 2.5-D PIC simulation of a quasi-parallel shock with Earth bow shock parameters, electrons are
energized in the shock transition by reconnection: Fermi reflection between electron-only reconnection
sites, and acceleration inside moving ion-scale magnetic islands, which gives the highest energies.

## Method
- 2.5-D fully kinetic PIC, parameters of Earth's bow shock; tracing of selected electron trajectories.
- Follow-ups: theory and simulation of acceleration in islands (Hall electric field, "island betatron");
  energization in thinning current sheets that do not reconnect.

## Numbers to compare against
- Electron spectrum: a power law with index around 6.
- Electron-only reconnection accounts for no more than half of the maximum energy.
- 2024: about half of the high-energy electrons in the transition region gain their energy from the Hall
  field and island betatron acceleration; stated to apply to quasi-parallel shocks with M_A > 10.
- 2025: electrons energized in non-reconnecting sheets number about a quarter of those in reconnection
  regions; both are said to reach "the injection energy for diffusive shock acceleration".

## Assessment
- Abstracts only: run time, box, mass ratio and the energies reached are not known to us from these.
- A steep spectrum (index ~6) in the transition region, not a DSA tail: a pre-acceleration result. The
  papers say injection into DSA is possible, not that it occurs in the run.
- For this topic: these mechanisms need two dimensions in the plane of the reconnecting field. A
  quasi-1-D run cannot contain them, which bears on the 2-D check (run X) and on H6.
