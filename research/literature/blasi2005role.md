# Blasi, Gabici & Vannoni (2005): On the role of injection in kinetic approaches to non-linear particle acceleration at non-relativistic shock waves

- Ref: Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 361, 907–918 (2005); DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2966.2005.09227.x; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Kinetic models of cosmic-ray-modified shocks predict multiple solutions. With an injection recipe that is
"a variation of what is sometimes referred to as thermal leakage", "multiple solutions basically disappear
and when they are present they are limited to rather peculiar values of the parameters."

## Method
- A semi-analytic kinetic model of the shocked fluid and of the transport of the accelerated particles,
  with "a simple phenomenological recipe" for injection.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. It says the work gives the acceleration efficiency, the fraction of energy
  advected downstream and the fraction that escapes upstream at the maximum momentum.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It gives an injection recipe of the thermal-leakage kind for
  non-linear acceleration models (`arbutina2021nonlinear` and `morlino2012strong` cite it). The recipe
  is phenomenological; obliquity and electrons are not in the abstract.
