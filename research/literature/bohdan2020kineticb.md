# Bohdan, Pohl, Niemiec, Morris, Matsumoto, Amano & Hoshino (2020): Kinetic simulation of nonrelativistic perpendicular shocks of young supernova remnants. IV. Electron heating

- Ref: ApJ 904, 12 (2020) (volume and article number from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/abbc19; arXiv:2008.05920 (v1); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
At perpendicular shocks of high Mach number, "Electrons are heated via shock surfing acceleration, the shock
potential, magnetic reconnection, stochastic Fermi scattering and the shock compression. The main contributor
is the shock potential." The Weibel instability tangles the field lines, "which allows the parallel electron
heating by the shock potential".

## Method
- "a set of large-scale Particle-In-Cell simulations of non-relativistic shocks in high Mach number regime".
  Dimensionality, Mach numbers and mass ratios: not in the abstract.
- The physics is "defined by ion reflection at the shock ramp"; the reflected ions excite the "electrostatic
  Buneman and two-stream ion-ion Weibel instabilities".

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. It says that electrons are heated "well above the Rankine-Hugoniot prediction" and
  that the model "predicts the ion-to-electron temperature ratio within observed values at SNR shocks and in
  Saturn's bow shock"; the values are not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: it concerns heating, not acceleration, at the perpendicular end. Its use is the list of
  processes that a run at the equator must separate, and the statement that the shock potential dominates
  the heating once the field lines are tangled.
- It is part IV of a series; parts I to III are `bohdan2019kinetic`, `bohdan2019kineticb` and
  `bohdan2020kinetic`.
- The tangling by the Weibel instability needs a high Mach number. Whether a front of this topic is in that
  regime has to be shown from the run. (This remark is ours.)
