## Claim
In the extended foreshock of an oblique high-Mach-number shock, the reflected electron beams drive two
instabilities: "the electrostatic electron-acoustic instability dominates in the outer region of the
foreshock, while the denser electron beams in the inner foreshock drive the gyroresonant oblique-whistler
instability." Both agree "very well" with a linear dispersion analysis.

## Method
- A particle-in-cell simulation of the shock gives the phase-space distributions in the inner and outer
  foreshock. These are the initial conditions of periodic-box simulations that follow the relaxation.
- The obliquity, the Mach number and the mass ratio are not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: which waves the reflected electrons drive upstream of an oblique shock. It bears on the
  oblique part of a sphere, between the equator and the poles.
- It is a shock simulation followed by periodic boxes. Curvature and the change of obliquity along a
  front are not in the abstract. `morris2023preacceleration` and `fulat2026pic` cite this work.
