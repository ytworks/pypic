# Bondarenko et al. (2017): Collisionless momentum transfer in space and astrophysical explosions

- Ref: Nature Physics 13(6), 573 (2017); DOI 10.1038/nphys4041; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (first paragraph, from the description field of the publisher's page). The full text is
  behind the paywall and no open copy was found. Nothing below goes beyond that paragraph.

## Claim
Larmor coupling, a collisionless exchange of momentum between an exploding plasma cloud and a magnetised
ambient plasma, is detected in a reproducible laboratory experiment.

## Method
- "an explosive laser-produced plasma cloud with preformed, magnetized ambient plasma" in a parameter
  regime relevant to the AMPTE barium releases.

## Numbers to compare against
- No numbers in the paragraph read.
- Larmor coupling is said to be "believed to occur in various astrophysical and space-plasma
  environments" and "to participate in cosmic magnetized collisionless shock formation".
- The AMPTE observation that motivates the work: the barium cloud moved transverse to both the solar
  wind flow and the interplanetary field, and the hydrogen ions were deflected the other way.

## Assessment
- For this topic: it establishes the coupling mechanism that the piston picture rests on. It gives no
  condition for a shock and no Mach number.
- Useful as the reference for "Larmor coupling" in the background of a write-up, not for the design.
