## Claim
"electrons accelerated at the Type II shock naturally develop a distribution function with an abrupt
cutoff at a characteristic minimum parallel velocity (a 'cutoff distribution) in a limited spatial volume
ahead of the shock, irrespective of the acceleration process active at the shock."

## Assessment
- For this topic: the electron distribution ahead of a shock has a low-velocity cutoff that arises
  "irrespective of the acceleration process". So a beam seen upstream of our front does not by itself
  identify the mechanism.
- The application is to Type II bursts (Langmuir waves from the cutoff distribution, split bands,
  herringbones). The shape of the shock is not in the abstract; `cairns1987electron` gives the theory.
