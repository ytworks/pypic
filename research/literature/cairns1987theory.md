# Cairns (1987b): A theory for the Langmuir waves in the electron foreshock

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. 92(A3), 2329–2342 (1987); DOI 10.1029/JA092iA03p02329; peer-reviewed.

## Claim
"Free energy for the Langmuir wave growth is contained in cutoff distributions of energetic electrons
streaming from the bow shock. These cutoff distributions drive Langmuir wave growth primarily by the
kinetic version of the beam instability, and wave growth is limited by quasi-linear relaxation."

## Method
- Theory with "only plausibility arguments", "since suitable treatments of quasi-linear relaxation are not
  presently available".
- It shows that wave-wave processes with ion sound and transverse waves, refraction, diffusion by ion
  sound turbulence, wave convection and gradients of the beam velocity "are unable to suppress the beam
  instability".

## Assessment
- For this topic: background. It says what happens to the cutoff distribution of `cairns1987electron`
  after it leaves the shock: the observed bump-on-tail distributions are "the remnants of cutoff
  distributions after quasi-linear relaxation".
- For our run: an observed upstream bump is, in this theory, a relaxed remnant and not the distribution
  that left the shock. Nothing on the acceleration at the shock is in the abstract.
