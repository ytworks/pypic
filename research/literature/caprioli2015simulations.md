# Caprioli, Pop & Spitkovsky (2015): Simulations and theory of ion injection at non-relativistic collisionless shocks

  peer-reviewed.

## Claim
Quasi-parallel shocks "reform quasi-periodically on ion cyclotron scales. Ions that impinge on the shock
when the discontinuity is the steepest are specularly reflected. This is a necessary condition for being
injected, but it is not sufficient."

## Method
- Kinetic hybrid simulations (kinetic ions, fluid electrons). Trajectories of reflected ions give "the
  minimum energy needed for injection into diffusive shock acceleration, as a function of the shock
  inclination".
- A minimal model accounts for the reflection from the quasi-periodic barrier, the injected fraction and
  the ion spectrum from thermal to non-thermal energies.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It is the ion side of injection, with fluid electrons. The link to our
  question is the dependence of the injection energy on the shock inclination.
- The abstract says the model holds for "arbitrary strengths and magnetic inclinations"; the range of
  inclinations simulated is not in the abstract.
