# Cassam-Chenaï, Hughes, Reynoso, Badenes & Moffett (2008): Morphological evidence for azimuthal variations of the cosmic-ray ion acceleration at the blast wave of SN 1006

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 680, 1180–1197 (2008); DOI 10.1086/588015; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In the southeast of SN 1006 the structure of the remnant changes with azimuth in the way expected from
efficient ion acceleration: "the observations are consistent with a model in which these parameters are
all azimuthally varying, being largest in the brightest regions." The parameters are the injection rate,
the magnetic field and the level of turbulence.

## Method
- Radio, X-ray and optical data. The Balmer filament traces the blast wave where no synchrotron emission
  is seen; low-energy X-rays (oxygen band) trace the contact discontinuity.
- The azimuthal variation of the ratio of the two radii and of the synchrotron emission at the blast wave
  is compared with cosmic-ray-modified hydrodynamic models with assumed azimuthal profiles.

## Assessment
- For this topic: evidence at the scale of the remnant that ion injection is highest where the electron
  synchrotron emission is brightest. It is about ions and about a fit of assumed profiles.
- Background for the electron mechanism: the abstract says nothing on how electrons are injected, and it
  does not name the obliquity of the bright regions.
