# Chen, Bale, Bonnell, Borovikov, Bowen, Burgess, et al. (2020): The Evolution and Role of Solar Wind Turbulence in the Inner Heliosphere

- Ref: Astrophys. J. Suppl. Ser. 246, 53 (2020); DOI 10.3847/1538-4365/ab60a3; arXiv:1912.02348; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (ar5iv text, via extraction; figures not viewed); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Over PSP's first two orbits (0.17–0.82 au) the solar wind was mostly slow, Alfvénic, with large-amplitude
(δB/B ∼ 1) fluctuations. Toward the Sun the magnetic spectrum flattens (−5/3 → −3/2), compressibility and
slow-mode fraction drop, cross-helicity rises, and the outer scale shrinks (∝ r^1.1); the Alfvénic energy flux
is ~10 % of the bulk kinetic flux at 0.17 au.

## Method
- PSP FIELDS/MAG (0.44 s), RFS quasi-thermal-noise density, SWEAP/SPC proton moments; 2018-10-06 to 2019-04-18.
- Spectral fits in the inertial range (10–100 s); Elsasser, cross-helicity and residual-energy statistics;
  correlation times from autocorrelation functions; radial power-law fits.

## Numbers to compare against
- "large amplitude (δB/B ∼ 1) fluctuations" (Sect. II).
- Spectral index ≈ −3/2 at 0.17 au, ≈ −5/3 at ~0.6 au (Fig. 2).
- σ_c ≈ 0.8 → 0.3 outward; σ_r ≈ −0.2 (Fig. 4); r_A ≈ 0.69 at 0.17 au (Fig. 3).
- Magnetic compressibility ∝ r^1.68±0.23, ~10× lower at perihelion than at 1 au (Fig. 5).
- Correlation times at perihelion 1: B, v, z⁺ ≈ 7 min (417, 419, 407 s), z⁻ ≈ 3300 s (Table 2);
  break and correlation scales ∝ r^1.1 (Fig. 9).
- Alfvénic flux F_A ∝ r^−3.52; 0.72 mW m⁻² at 0.17 au; F_A/F_k ∝ r^−1.75, ~10 % at 0.17 au (Sect. III.3).
- Inertial range ~3 decades wide, roughly constant over 0.17–1 au (Sect. IV).
- No numerical β, d_i or outer scale in km given in the text read.

## Assessment
- Gives the near-Sun context: δB/B ∼ 1, highly Alfvénic (σ_c up to 0.8), weakly compressive fluctuations —
  this is the regime where "large-amplitude Alfvénic turbulence" upstream of a shock is the norm, not a choice.
- At 1 au the same quantities are lower (σ_c ≈ 0.3 in their trend, compressibility ×10 higher), so a δB/B ~ 1
  Alfvénic upstream is more representative of ≲ 0.3 au than of 1 au.
- Switchbacks and slow-wind dominance bias the sample; only two orbits.
