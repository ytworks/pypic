# Chiappetta et al. (2026): Evolution of the Shock Properties of the 2023 March 13 Event from In Situ and Remote-sensing Data

- Ref: ApJ 997, 190; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae230e; arXiv:2511.03496 (9 authors: Chiappetta, Nistico,
  Chimenti, Larosa, Malara, Pucci, Sorriso-Valvo, Zimbardo, ...). Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract and the in situ parameters of the PSP crossing (arXiv HTML); the
  remote-sensing analysis skimmed; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
The shock of 2023-03-13 is followed from the corona (coronagraph images, an expanding ellipsoid) to the in
situ crossings at PSP, Solar Orbiter, STEREO A and Wind. Compression ratio and Alfven Mach number stay
almost constant near the Sun and rise further out; the compression varies strongly around the front.

## Method
- Shock geometry from STEREO-A/COR2 and SOHO/LASCO; density compression from fits of the image brightness
  to ray-traced model profiles; in situ shock parameters at four spacecraft.

## Numbers to compare against
- PSP crossing: theta_Bn 19.6 +- 9.1 deg; compression 1.39 +- 0.70 by one method and ~2.54 by another;
  M_A 4.68 +- 3.45.
- PSP crossed the flank of the shock.
- "Significant inhomogeneities" of the compression ratio around the front in the corona.

## Assessment
- For this topic: an independent estimate of the PSP crossing that disagrees with
  jebaraj2024acceleration (theta_Bn 8 deg, M_A 9.1, compression 4) and lies near giacalone2025source
  (M_A ~4). The input table of the PSP case has to carry this spread (M_A ~4 to ~9). Every central value
  lies below the M_A ~10 boundary of gupta2024electron.
- A compression of 1.4-2.5 instead of 4 changes the expected DSA slope strongly (H8).
- The parameters change along the path and around the front (H7).
- The errors are large (M_A +- 3.45); the two compression values come from different methods.
