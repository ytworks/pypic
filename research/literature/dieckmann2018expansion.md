# Dieckmann, Moreno, Doria, Romagnani, Sarri, Folini, Walder, Bret, d'Humières & Borghesi (2018): Expansion of a radially symmetric blast shell into a uniformly magnetized plasma

- Ref: Phys. Plasmas 25 (2018); DOI 10.1063/1.5024851; arXiv:1805.02875 (read); peer-reviewed. Article
  number not verified.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, set-up, summary; text search for electron acceleration);
  topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
A radially expanding blast shell in a uniformly magnetized plasma, in 2-D full PIC, drives a forward shock
whose type changes with the angle to the field: ion acoustic, slow magnetosonic, fast magnetosonic. For
perpendicular propagation the slow-mode structure becomes a tangential discontinuity (abstract).

## Method
- 2-D full PIC; a dense hot cylinder of radius r_0 = 2 mm expands by thermal pressure into an ambient
  plasma (2 keV electrons, nitrogen ions with the true mass ratio, about 2.6e4).
- Simulation 1: B_0 in the plane (along y), 16 x 32 mm, 2000 x 4000 cells. Simulation 2: B_0 out of the
  plane, 2000 x 2000 cells. Low Mach number.

## Numbers to compare against
- None for electron acceleration: the paper does not analyse energetic electrons (the word "acceleration"
  does not occur as a result for electrons). It reports an electron-cyclotron drift instability triggered
  by the expulsion of the magnetic field by the blast shell (abstract).

## Assessment
- For this topic: the closest set-up found to a full-PIC spherical (here cylindrical) blast in a uniform
  field with theta_Bn from 0 to 90 deg on one front, but at low Mach number, electrostatic to
  magnetosonic, and without any electron-acceleration result. It does not pre-empt a claim on electron
  injection.
