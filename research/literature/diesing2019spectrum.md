# Diesing & Caprioli (2019): On the Spectrum of Electrons Accelerated in Supernova Remnants

- Ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 123, 071101 (2019); DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.123.071101; arXiv:1905.07414 (v2,
  "accepted to PRL"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Electrons accelerated in supernova remnants (SNRs) "experience synchrotron losses in the amplified magnetic
fields characteristic of SNRs" and so "exhibit substantially steeper spectra than protons".

## Method
- "a semi-analytic model of non-linear diffusive shock acceleration", applied to the spectrum of cosmic-ray
  electrons accelerated by SNRs.

## Numbers to compare against
- "the difference between the electron and proton spectral index (power law slope) ranges from 0.1 to 0.4".

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It concerns the spectrum of electrons that are already in diffusive shock
  acceleration, over the life of a remnant. It says nothing on how electrons are injected, on the shock
  obliquity or on the shape of the front.
