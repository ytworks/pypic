# Dresing, Theesen, Klassen & Heber (2016): Efficiency of particle acceleration at interplanetary shocks: Statistical study of STEREO observations

- Ref: A&A 588, A17; DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/201527853; arXiv:1602.03440. Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv
  version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, method, the electron results and the summary (arXiv HTML); topics:
  qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
At 1 au, interplanetary shocks are "nearly ineffective" for 65-75 keV electrons: shock-associated
increases occur at 1 % of the crossings, against 5 % for 2 MeV ions and more for 100 keV ions.

## Method
- STEREO A and B, 475 shock crossings; an increase counts when the intensity at the shock is above a
  quiet-time level and at least twice the 4-hour pre-event background.
- Electron channels corrected for ion contamination; increases with more than 60 % contamination or
  caused by a solar event are rejected.

## Numbers to compare against
- 65-75 keV electrons: five shock-associated increases (1 %); eight real increases in all, three of them
  solar.
- 73 % of the detected electron increases are contaminated by ions.
- The five: fast-mode Mach number 1.25-2.85; four at quasi-perpendicular shocks, one at a quasi-parallel
  shock. All spike-like increases (four of five) are at quasi-perpendicular shocks, as in
  tsurutani1985acceleration.

## Assessment
- For this topic: one quasi-parallel electron event in 475 crossings at 65-75 keV. Weak shocks
  (M 1.25-2.85) can show an increase, so Mach number is not the separating variable in this sample.
- The selection requires the intensity to be above quiet time; whether an elevated pre-event population
  is a condition (H4) or only a requirement of the method cannot be told from this paper.
- No energies above ~75 keV for electrons.
