# Dresing, Kouloumvakos, Vainio & Rouillard (2022): On the Role of Coronal Shocks for Accelerating Solar Energetic Electrons

- Ref: ApJL 925, L21; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ac4ca7. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (the publisher page refused automated reading; an open copy at the
  Kiel university repository was not fetched); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
The peak intensities of solar energetic electrons measured in situ correlate with the strength of the
coronal shock at the magnetically connected region. High-energy electrons are mainly accelerated by the
shock; low-energy electrons are a mixture of flare and shock populations.

## Method
- STEREO in-situ electron and proton peak intensities of solar energetic particle events.
- Shock parameters at the connected field lines from a 3-D reconstruction of the coronal shock and a
  coronal model.
- Null test: the intensities at STEREO A are related to the Mach number at the footpoint of STEREO B, and
  the reverse.

## Numbers to compare against
- Correlation with the Mach number: ~0.7 for ~1 MeV electrons and for > 60 MeV protons; 0.47 for electrons
  below 100 keV.
- With the spacecraft exchanged: 0.03 (~1 MeV) and -0.13 (< 100 keV).

## Assessment
- Remote and statistical: shock parameters are modelled, the geometry at the acceleration site is not
  resolved, and the abstract gives no split by theta_Bn.
- For this topic: evidence that the shock near the Sun matters for MeV electrons, which bears on H7 (the
  history of the PSP shock of 2023-03-13 before it reached 0.24 au). Not an input of the four cases.
