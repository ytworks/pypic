# Fang, Xia, Tian, Zhou & Yu (2022): Kinetic Simulation on Electron, Proton and Helium Acceleration in a Nonrelativistic Quasiparallel shock

- Ref: MNRAS 512, 5418-5422 (2022) (volume and pages from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1093/mnras/stac886; arXiv:2202.02689 (v3, "Accepted for publication in MNRAS"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
At a non-relativistic quasi-parallel shock with helium, "waves can be excited by the flow of the energetic
protons and helium ions upstream", "and the charged particles are scattered by the self-generated waves and
accelerated gradually". "the spectra of the charged particles downstream of the shock are thermal plus a
nonthermal tail".

## Method
- 1-D PIC simulation with electrons, protons and helium ions; "helium-to-proton number density ratio of
  0.1".
- Sonic Mach number 14 and Alfvén Mach number 19.5 "in the shock rest frame". Obliquity, mass ratio and run
  time: not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- "about 7% and 5.4% of the bulk kinetic energy transferred into the nonthermal protons and helium ions in
  the near downstream region at the end of the simulation, respectively".
- The electron energy fraction is not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background for the quasi-parallel end. The title and the first sentences include
  electrons, but the abstract gives numbers for protons and helium only.
- 1-D, so it cannot show effects of the dimension or of the shape of the front. A related earlier work on
  quasi-parallel shocks is `fang2019early` (Fang, Lu, Yan & Yu 2019).
