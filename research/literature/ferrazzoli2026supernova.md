## Claim
IXPE gave the first spatially resolved X-ray polarimetry of supernova remnants (SNRs). All observed remnants
show significant polarization. Three have mainly radial magnetic fields and two have tangential ones. This
"dual behavior" is "not simply correlated with evolutionary stage"; the abstract suggests that shock velocity
and circumstellar density both shape the field topology.

## Method
- A review of the IXPE observations of six young SNRs: Cas A, Tycho, SN 1006, RX J1713.7-3946, Vela Jr. and
  RCW 86, "with published results on the first five".

## Numbers to compare against
- Degree of polarization: "ranging from 5% to over 30%".
- Radial fields: Cas A, Tycho, SN 1006. Tangential fields: RX J1713.7-3946, Vela Jr.
- Values per remnant and per region: not in the abstract.
