# Galishnikova, Gupta, Spitkovsky & Sun (2026): self-regulated electron-driven turbulence at quasi-parallel shocks (preprint)

- Ref: arXiv:2611.15879; submitted to ApJ. Not peer reviewed at the time of reading. The exact title was
  not recorded; check it before citing.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
In MHD-PIC simulations of non-relativistic, high-Mach-number quasi-parallel shocks with cosmic-ray
electrons, the upstream turbulence and the escaping current settle into "a common regulated state largely
insensitive to the details of CR injection".

## Method
- 1-D and 2-D MHD-PIC: a fluid background, kinetic cosmic-ray electrons. The mass fraction of injected
  electrons is varied; injection is prescribed, not obtained from first principles.
- Trajectories through the evolving upstream turbulence; Bell-type non-resonant turbulence.

## Numbers to compare against
- The saturated upstream magnetic energy and the far-upstream cosmic-ray current converge across the
  injection fractions.
- 1-D: the downstream spectrum approaches test-particle DSA; the nonthermal pressure reaches a few tens of
  percent of the ram pressure.

## Assessment
- Abstract only; the Mach numbers, the injection fractions and the run times are not known to us.
- For this topic: it treats the stage after injection and takes the injection rate as an input. If the
  long-term state does not depend on the injection details, the injection rate matters for whether and
  when the state is reached, which is the question here. It does not measure injection or D_e at event
  conditions.
- "High Mach number": the low-M_A cases of this topic (PSP, 1 au) are probably outside its range.
- Same group as gupta2024electron and gupta2025speed (on `research/spherical-shock-injection`).
