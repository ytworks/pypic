# Giacalone (2005): The efficient acceleration of thermal protons by perpendicular shocks

- Ref: Astrophys. J. Lett. 628, L37-L40 (2005) (Crossref); DOI 10.1086/432510; no arXiv version found; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
In large-scale self-consistent plasma (hybrid) simulations of a perpendicular shock moving through large-scale
magnetic fluctuations, some thermal protons are reflected and travel upstream along field lines that are multiply
connected to the shock, and are accelerated far above the ram energy: "there is not an injection problem at
perpendicular shocks".

## Method
- Self-consistent large-scale simulation (hybrid, per the citing literature and the abstract's "self-consistent
  plasma simulations"), perpendicular mean field with pre-existing fluctuations. Parameters not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- None from the abstract.

## Assessment
- The ion analogue of the field-line-meandering picture that Guo & Giacalone (2010) carried over to electrons with
  test particles. Multiple connection of a field line to the front is the stated mechanism.
- 2D with an ignorable coordinate presumably (not checked); Jokipii et al. (1993) restrictions would apply.
- Protons only; electrons fluid.
