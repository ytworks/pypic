# Giacalone (2005): Particle acceleration at shocks moving through an irregular magnetic field

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 624, 765-772 (2005) (Crossref); DOI 10.1086/429265; no arXiv version found; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
In non-diffusive test-particle simulations of a shock moving through large-scale magnetic turbulence, the
injection velocity depends only weakly on the mean theta_Bn and low-energy particles are readily accelerated at
any mean angle; the acceleration rate is larger near perpendicular, and at parallel shocks in large-scale
fluctuations it exceeds the simple first-order Fermi prediction.

## Method
- Non-relativistic test particles integrated in a prescribed (kinematic) shock plus irregular magnetic field; no
  diffusion assumption. Details beyond the abstract not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- None from the abstract.

## Assessment
- Valid, as stated, for shocks planar on scales larger than the turbulence coherence scale and for gyroradii
  smaller than it - the regime our topic sets up (L_c >= 10 d_i, electrons).
- Note: the task list called this a hybrid study; the abstract describes test particles in prescribed fields.
  The self-consistent hybrid counterpart is Giacalone (2005, ApJL 628, L37).
- Ions/generic particles; electrons not singled out in the abstract.
