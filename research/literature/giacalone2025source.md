# Giacalone, Trotta, Mitchell, Cohen, Fraschetti & Bale (2025): The Source of Energetic Storm Particles for the 2023 March 13 Event Observed by Parker Solar Probe

- Ref: ApJ 996, 87; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae1ef5. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
The energetic protons at the PSP shock of 2023-03-13 come from shock-heated solar wind protons, not from
the energetic particles present before the shock. Hybrid simulations compared with the observed spectra
and field also give a slower and weaker shock than estimated before.

## Method
- A "seeds test": the enhancement expected at the shock if a pre-existing population were re-accelerated.
- Self-consistent hybrid simulations (kinetic ions, fluid electrons) of the event, compared directly with
  the observed particle spectra and magnetic field.

## Numbers to compare against
- PSP at ~0.23 au; protons of ~0.08-10 MeV enhanced at the shock.
- At 0.1-1 MeV the intensity at the shock is 3-4 orders of magnitude above that ~4 hr earlier.
- Best match: shock speed ~1350 km/s, M_A ~4, "considerably lower than previously estimated".

## Assessment
- Protons only; hybrid simulations cannot treat electrons. This is the existing confrontation of the PSP
  event with a self-consistent simulation, for ions.
- For this topic: (1) for protons the seed hypothesis is tested and rejected at this event; the same test
  for electrons has not been published as far as our survey found. (2) M_A ~4 and 1350 km/s differ from
  jebaraj2024acceleration (9.1, 2800 km/s) and jebaraj2026wave (7-7.5, u_1 ~2400 km/s).
- Abstract only: how the speed and M_A follow from the comparison, and their errors, are not known to us.
