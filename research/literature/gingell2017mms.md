# Gingell, Schwartz, Burgess, Johlander, Russell, Burch, Ergun, Fuselier et al. (2017): MMS Observations and Hybrid Simulations of Surface Ripples at a Marginally Quasi-Parallel Shock

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Physics 122 (11); DOI 10.1002/2017JA024538 (pages not given by Crossref).
  Peer reviewed. 19 authors.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Surface ripples exist at a marginally quasi-parallel shock (θ_Bn ~45°). They are transient: they develop
faster than an ion gyroperiod and only during part of the reformation cycle.

## Method
- MMS observations of one crossing; 2-D hybrid simulations at its parameters.

## Numbers to compare against
- θ_Bn ~45°.

## Assessment
- Ion scales; no energetic electrons in the abstract.
- For this topic: (1) one more event-and-run pair on shock structure; (2) the shock surface is structured
  along the front on the ion scale and in time, at the border between quasi-parallel and oblique. A quasi-
  1-D run has a flat, coherent front by construction, as `caprioli2015simulations` also notes for
  reformation. What that removes for electrons is not known from what we read; it is a limit to name.
