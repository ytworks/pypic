# Guo & Giacalone (2012): Particle acceleration at a flare termination shock: effect of large-scale magnetic turbulence

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 753, 28 (2012) (Crossref); DOI 10.1088/0004-637X/753/1/28; arXiv:1204.5096; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, introduction, method); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
At a low-Mach perpendicular (flare termination) shock moving through large-scale Alfvenic fluctuations, test
electrons started from a thermal distribution are accelerated to MeV within about 100 Omega_ci^-1; with
dB^2/B0^2 about 0.3, about 10 % of thermal electrons exceed 15 keV. Protons gain less, with steeper spectra.

## Method
- Same scheme as Guo & Giacalone (2010): 2D hybrid (x-z plane, three components), piston, injected 1D fluctuations
  B(z,t) = dB(z,t) + B10 z_hat, equal mix of circularly polarized parallel Alfven waves, L_c = L_z; then
  relativistic test electrons in the stored fields.
- Inflow M_A0 = 1.0, shock M_A about 2.0 (shock frame); dx = dz = 0.5 c/omega_pi; dt = 0.01 Omega_ci^-1;
  beta_i = beta_e = 0.03; c/V_A0 = 410 (T = 2e6 K, n = 8e9 cm^-3, B = 30 G).
- 1.6e6 test electrons, Maxwellian with T_e = 2e6 K (thermal, not a suprathermal shell as in 2010).

## Numbers to compare against
- About 10 % of thermal test electrons above 15 keV for dB^2/B0^2 about 0.3; energies of a few MeV within
  100 Omega_ci^-1 (abstract; per-case variances in Table 1, not read).

## Assessment
- The one hybrid+test-electron study in this line that starts electrons from the thermal pool - the closest to an
  injection claim. But the electrons are test particles: no electron-scale shock structure, no self-consistent
  electron heating, and the cross-shock potential is the fluid-electron one.
- Low Mach number (M_A about 2), low beta; very different from SNR-like high-Mach shocks.
- Same 2D ignorable-coordinate limit as Guo & Giacalone (2010).
