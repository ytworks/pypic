# Guo, Giacalone & Zhao (2021): Shock propagation and associated particle acceleration in the presence of ambient solar-wind turbulence

- Ref: Front. Astron. Space Sci. 8, 644354 (2021) (Crossref); DOI 10.3389/fspas.2021.644354; arXiv:2102.06054;
  peer-reviewed review
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (Sects. IV-VI read; II-III skimmed); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Review: solar-wind turbulence ripples interplanetary shock surfaces on many scales and, through large-scale
field-line meandering, lets low-rigidity particles return to the shock repeatedly; pre-existing turbulence is put
forward as a solution of the injection problem at quasi-perpendicular shocks, for protons and electrons.

## Method
- Review of hybrid simulations with test-particle electrons (Guo & Giacalone 2010, 2012, 2015), test particles in
  kinematic turbulent fields, and interplanetary-shock observations (Tsurutani & Lin 1985, Simnett et al. 2005,
  Bale et al. 1999, Pulupa & Bale 2008).
- States the mechanism of Jokipii & Giacalone (2007): electrons conserve mu, run along meandering field lines and
  cross the shock repeatedly, gaining energy from the flow difference; ripples add mirroring between them.

## Numbers to compare against
- Reproduced figures: electron energy-flux spectra versus <theta_Bn> (Fig. 5, from Guo & Giacalone 2010);
  energetic electrons (E > 10 E0, E0 = 100 eV) far upstream at z = 200 but not at z = 800 c/omega_pi (Fig. 6).
- Fraction of electrons accelerated from 1 keV to > 10 keV versus theta_Bn for variances up to
  sigma = <dB^2>/B0^2 = 10: strongest at perpendicular shocks, quasi-parallel shocks accelerate when the
  amplitude is large (Fig. 7, from Guo & Giacalone 2015).

## Assessment
- Useful as the authors' own statement of the field-line-meandering picture and of what remains open: the role of
  turbulence "has not been understood in a quantitative way" (Sect. V).
- All electron results cited are test-particle in hybrid or prescribed fields; no fully kinetic electron
  physics (whistlers, electron-scale front structure, self-consistent electron heating) is discussed as tested.
- No new results; a review.
