# Gupta, Caprioli & Spitkovsky (2023): What regulates electron injection in diffusive shock acceleration?

- Ref: PoS(ICRC2023) 396; DOI 10.22323/1.444.0396. Conference proceeding, not peer reviewed in the journal
  sense.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
PIC simulations of quasi-parallel shocks "for a wide range of shock speeds and magnetizations" identify
the steps an electron must pass to be injected into DSA, and "a minimal model" accounts for the trends of
the simulations.

## Method
- Fully kinetic PIC of non-relativistic quasi-parallel shocks; a minimal injection model.

## Numbers to compare against
