# Hanusch, Liseykina & Malkov (2020): Electron energization in quasi-parallel shocks: Test-particle electrons in a proton-driven turbulence

- Ref: A&A 642, A47; DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202038915; arXiv:2007.06478. Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv
  version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract and the passages on Saturn and on the run parameters (arXiv PDF text);
  topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Test-particle electrons in the turbulence of a 1-D hybrid quasi-parallel shock are preheated by the
precursor waves. Electrons and protons do not equilibrate downstream; T_e/T_p falls with Mach number and
saturates at high Mach number.

## Method
- 1-D hybrid simulation (kinetic protons, fluid electrons) with electrons followed as test particles in
  its fields.
- Runs with v_0e / v_A = 100 and M_A 6-10.

## Numbers to compare against
- None taken; the electron spectra were not read in detail.

## Assessment
- The abstract names Saturn's bow shock as the observation that suggests efficient electron acceleration
  at quasi-parallel shocks; a footnote gives its M_A = 100. The runs are at M_A 6-10, not at Saturn's
  value.
- For this topic: an earlier use of the method "test electrons in self-consistent ion-scale fields". The
  fields are hybrid, so electron-scale waves (whistler precursors) are absent, and no diffusion coefficient
  or injection rate is reported in what we read.
- Heating and preheating, not acceleration to relativistic energy.
