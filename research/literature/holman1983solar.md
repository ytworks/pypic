# Holman & Pesses (1983): Solar type II radio emission and the shock drift acceleration of electrons

## Claim
Shock drift acceleration of electrons can produce type II bursts if the shock normal is at a high angle
to the field; no electrons are reflected within a few degrees of 90 deg; a curved front moving across
field lines gives herringbone structure and band splitting.

## Method
- Analytic theory.

## Numbers to compare against
- For a 1000 km/s shock in a 2e6 K corona, psi about 80 deg is required.
- "A curved shock front propagating across magnetic field lines can naturally result in herringbone
  structure, herringbone structure without a backbone, or band splitting, the result depending upon the
  orientation and radius of curvature of the shock front and magnetic field lines and upon the energy of
  the accelerated electrons."
- "The electrons responsible for each band originate from different regions of the shock front."

## Assessment
- For this topic: the earliest source found (1983) for electron reflection at a propagating curved front
  with the source region set by the local angle and a gap at 90 deg.
