# Hoshino (2008): Wakefield acceleration by radiation pressure in relativistic shock waves

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 672, 940–956 (2008); DOI 10.1086/523665; arXiv:0709.1783; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In a relativistic perpendicular shock, "non-thermal, high energy electrons and ions can be quickly
produced by an action of electrostatic wakefields generated by the ponderomotive force of the precursor
waves."

## Method
- The precursor is large-amplitude electromagnetic waves excited in the front by the synchrotron maser
  instability, for an upstream bulk Lorentz factor gamma_1 much greater than 1.
- How the result was obtained (theory or simulation) is not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- In the coherent wakefield region: eps_max / (gamma_1 m_e c^2) of the order of gamma_1.
- Estimate of the maximum energy: eps_max / (gamma_1 m_e c^2) of the order of L_sys / (c/omega_pe), with
  L_sys the size of the object.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. The mechanism needs a relativistic shock (gamma_1 much greater than
  1) and its electromagnetic precursor. Our shock is non-relativistic.
- Shock curvature and obliquity other than perpendicular are not in the abstract.
