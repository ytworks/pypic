## Claim
In relativistic shocks the precursor wave amplitude "is significantly enhanced by a positive feedback
process associated with ion-electron coupling through the wakefields for high magnetization. The
wakefields collapse during the nonlinear process of the parametric decay instability in the near-upstream
region, where nonthermal electrons and ions are generated."

## Method
- Two-dimensional particle-in-cell simulations of relativistic shocks. The Lorentz factor, the
  magnetization values and the mass ratio are not in the abstract.
