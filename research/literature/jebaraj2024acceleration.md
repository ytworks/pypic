# Jebaraj, Agapitov, Krasnoselskikh, Vuorinen, Gedalin, Choi, et al. (2024): Acceleration of Electrons and Ions by an "Almost" Astrophysical Shock in the Heliosphere

- Ref: Astrophys. J. Lett. 968, L8 (2024); DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ad4daa; arXiv:2405.07074; peer-reviewed (letter).
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (arXiv v1 text: Table 1, Sect. II–III, App. B–C); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Parker Solar Probe crossed one of the fastest IP shocks measured in situ (≈ 2800 km/s, M_A ≈ 9) at 0.24 au. The
shock is quasi-parallel at large scale, but its ramp and whistler precursors are locally quasi-perpendicular;
electrons are accelerated locally up to ~6 MeV and ions up to ~10 MeV, amid intense multi-scale waves.

## Method
- Event 2023-03-13, shock at 07:13 UT; FIELDS (MAG, SCM, RFS), SWEAP/SPAN-i, IS☉IS (EPI-Lo, EPI-Hi in dynamic
  threshold mode).
- Normal from MVA + coplanarity; shock speed from mass flux with SPAN-i/EPI-Lo; density from RFS quasi-thermal
  noise (App. B.2); M_A cross-checked by an overshoot-amplitude proxy (App. C.3).
- δB/B = |B − ⟨B⟩|/⟨|B|⟩ over windows τ = 5 s and 1 min, split into δB∥ and δB⊥ (App. E).

- Particles: electrons tens of keV to ~6 MeV; protons E⁻¹ above ~250 keV (Fig. 2E); >1 MeV ions stream ~90 min

## Assessment
- Shown: MeV electron acceleration at a heliospheric shock is possible when M_A ~ 9 and β is low; the local
  ramp geometry differs strongly from the large-scale θBn.
- The electron mechanism is not identified; "injection by high-frequency oblique waves and whistler precursors"
  is proposed qualitatively.
- Stated limits: downstream plasma outside SPAN-i range; density uncertain by up to ×2; normal poorly defined
  (B_n/B ≈ 0.66); EPI-Hi in dynamic-threshold mode; low electron time resolution.
- The large upstream δB/B here is shock-generated (foreshock of a quasi-parallel shock), not pre-existing
  solar-wind turbulence; it is an upper bracket of δB/B for our topic.
