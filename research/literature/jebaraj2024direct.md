# Jebaraj et al. (2024): Direct Measurements of Synchrotron-emitting Electrons at Near-Sun Shocks

- Ref: ApJL 976, L7; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ad8eb8; arXiv:2410.15933 (25 authors: Jebaraj, Agapitov,
  Gedalin, Vuorinen, Miceli, Cohen, Voshchepynets, Kouloumvakos, ...). Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv
  version (v2).
- Read: 2026-11-14, full main text (arXiv v2, HTML); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Parker Solar Probe crossed two interplanetary shocks that emit synchrotron radiation, and measured the
relativistic electrons that emit it. The strong near-parallel shock radiates about an order of magnitude
more intensely than the quasi-perpendicular one, which the authors attribute to efficient acceleration of
ultra-relativistic electrons at the quasi-parallel shock.

## Method
- PSP FIELDS radio spectra and IS(sun)IS energetic electrons at two crossings: S1, 2022-09-05 at 15.1
  solar radii, quasi-perpendicular; S2, 2023-03-13 at 49 solar radii, near-parallel (the shock of
  jebaraj2024acceleration).
- Electron spectra are related to the optically thin synchrotron spectra (Sect. II.4).

## Numbers to compare against
- Intrinsic brightness of the radiation from S2 an order of magnitude above S1 (Fig. 6).
- S1: the electron intensity toward the Sun (HET-A) is more than an order of magnitude above the anti-Sun
  direction (HET-B): strong anisotropy. S2: electrons are isotropic (see jebaraj2024acceleration).
- S1 electron intensities are below those of S2 at all energies (about one order; read from the text and
  figures, value not tabulated).
- Shock parameters of S1 (density compression ~2.5) and S2: see the cited event papers; not re-derived here.

## Assessment
- Two events, differing in geometry, strength and distance from the Sun at once; the paper does not
  separate these.
- The comparison supports "quasi-parallel can be the more efficient emitter", not a condition for it.
- How sub-relativistic electrons become relativistic is not addressed (also stated in
  jebaraj2024acceleration).
- The parameters of S2 were revised later (jebaraj2026wave, chiappetta2026evolution, giacalone2025source).
