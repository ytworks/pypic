# Jebaraj, Malkov, Wijsen, Pomoell, Krasnoselskikh, Dresing & Vainio (2025): Turbulence-driven corrugation of collisionless fast-magnetosonic shocks

- Ref: arXiv:2512.24425 (v2); no journal reference found; not peer-reviewed as far as checked
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
A linear-MHD theory treating the shock as a moving interface maps an assumed upstream turbulence spectrum to the
corrugation amplitude, its drift along the front and a coherence scale, through an effective impedance set by the
Rankine-Hugoniot state and geometry. The response peaks when the transmitted downstream fast mode propagates
nearly parallel to the front (Lorentzian enhancement set by the downstream normal group speed).

## Method
- Linear MHD, shock as a moving discontinuity; dependence on compression, beta and obliquity examined.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Gives a prediction for the front corrugation that upstream Alfvenic turbulence produces - a quantity a kinetic
  run can measure and compare against, separating turbulence-driven corrugation from self-generated ripples.
- MHD only; says nothing on particles beyond "conditions for injection".
