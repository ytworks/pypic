# Jebaraj et al. (2026): Does Diffusive Shock Acceleration Reach Steady State? Insights from a Near-Sun Shock Observed by Parker Solar Probe

- Ref: arXiv:2610.08034v1 (10 authors: Jebaraj, Malkov, Wijsen, Agapitov, Kouloumvakos, Afanasiev,
  Fraschetti, Husidic, ...). Preprint; the arXiv comment names ApJ Letters; a journal reference was not
  found (Crossref, 2026-11-14).
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract and the passages on the age and size limits and on confinement (arXiv HTML);
  the derivation of the mean free path from the wave spectra was skimmed; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
At the 2023-03-13 shock, met four hours after the eruption, the proton mean free path derived from the
measured wave spectra follows the steady self-generated solution in its distance dependence, but the steady
balance holds at one energy only. The waves are still growing. "The growth of the waves its own particles
drive, rather than its age or its size, limits the energy an evolving shock confines."

## Method
- PSP upstream wave spectra and protons on the same field line; parallel mean free path of 0.05-30 MeV
  protons from the wave spectra alone, resolved in energy and distance; gyroresonant scattering plus the
  mirror force of the measured compressive fluctuations for the pitch angles resonance cannot reach.

## Numbers to compare against
- u_1 ~2400 km/s, theta_Bn ~8 deg, M_A ~7-7.5 (as in jebaraj2026wave).
- Steady balance holds only near 1.3 MeV.
- Intensity profiles are a factor of two shallower than the measured coefficient predicts.
- Confinement fails between 2.3 and 6 MeV, "beginning a factor of two below what the age of the shock
  allows". Age limit ~8.9 MeV, size limit ~9.5 MeV (protons, their estimates).

## Assessment
- Protons only. For this topic it is a measured counter-example to "age or size sets the limit" (H1a, H1b
  of the topic) for ions at the one event where relativistic electrons are also seen. Whether the same
  holds for electrons is not addressed: electrons of a given energy resonate with much shorter waves than
  the proton-driven ones measured here.
- The age and size limits and the confinement onset differ by factors of 1.5-4; with the stated accuracy
  of the inputs this is a real but not a large separation.
- Not peer reviewed in the version read.
