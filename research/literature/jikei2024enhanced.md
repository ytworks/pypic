# Jikei, Amano & Matsumoto (2024): Enhanced Magnetic Field Amplification by Ion-Beam Weibel Instability in Weakly Magnetized Astrophysical Shocks

- Ref: ApJ 961, 157 (2024) (volume and article number from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ad1594; arXiv:2312.07933 (v1); peer-reviewed.

## Claim
"a finite background magnetic field substantially affects both linear and nonlinear phases" of the ion-beam
Weibel instability at strong shocks in weakly magnetized media, "depending on whether the background
electrons behave magnetized or not". For magnetized electrons, PIC simulations "identify a dynamo-like
mechanism of magnetic field amplification, which eventually leads to spontaneous magnetic reconnection".
"We conclude that this scenario is applicable to typical young supernova remnant shocks."

## Method
- Linear and nonlinear study of the ion-beam Weibel instability with a finite background field.
- Particle-in-cell simulations "for magnetized electrons". Whether a shock or a beam-plasma system is
  simulated, and the dimensionality: not in the abstract.

- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background on the turbulence of the transition region of a strong, weakly magnetized
  shock. By the abstract it does not treat electron acceleration. Magnetic reconnection at perpendicular
  shocks of high Mach number is the subject of `bohdan2020kinetic`.
- The condition, whether the background electrons are magnetized, can be evaluated for a run. The abstract
  does not give the criterion in numbers.
