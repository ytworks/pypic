# Jikei, Grošelj & Sironi (2026): Magnetic Field Amplification and Particle Acceleration in Weakly Magnetized Trans-relativistic Electron-ion Shocks

- Ref: ApJ 998, 149 (2026); DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae3723; arXiv:2512.03169 (v2, "matches the journal
  version"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
At quasi-parallel trans-relativistic shocks in weakly magnetized plasmas, "The structure of the shock
precursor is shaped by a competition between the Bell instability and the Weibel (filamentation)
instability". Shocks with Bell precursors accelerate ions efficiently and electrons poorly. Shocks with
Weibel precursors generate "both nonthermal ions and electrons" efficiently, with a slower growth of the
maximum energy.

## Method
- "long-duration two-dimensional particle-in-cell simulations" of quasi-parallel trans-relativistic
  electron-ion shocks. Shock speed, obliquity and mass ratio: not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- Bell regime, magnetization sigma >~ 1e-3: epsilon_i ~ 0.2 of the upstream flow energy in downstream
  nonthermal ions; epsilon_e << 0.1; E_max proportional to t ("Bohm scaling").
- Weibel regime, sigma <~ 1e-4: epsilon_i ~ epsilon_e ~ 0.1; E_max proportional to t^(1/2).

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. The shocks are trans-relativistic; the abstract applies the results to
  "the termination shocks of extragalactic jets, the late stages of gamma-ray burst afterglows, and shocks
  in fast blue optical transients", not to non-relativistic fronts.
- What transfers is the form of the result: at a quasi-parallel shock the electron efficiency depends on
  which instability shapes the precursor, and that is set by the magnetization. A work of the same authors
  on the dependence on shock velocity is `jikei2026dependence`.
