# Annie John, Nyberg, Vuorinen, Vainio, Afanasiev, Poedts & Wijsen (2024): Effects of adiabatic focusing and free-escape boundaries in coronal shock acceleration

- Ref: J. Space Weather Space Clim. 14, 15; DOI 10.1051/swsc/2024012. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
In a 1-D Monte Carlo model of an oblique coronal shock, the particle spectrum reaches a steady state when
adiabatic focusing in the diverging field is included, and does not without it.

## Method
- 1-D oblique-shock Monte Carlo simulation with a mean free path of the form of Bell (1978).

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- "the effects of focusing can be mimicked in a non-focused simulation by introducing a free-escape boundary
  ahead of the shock".
- For this topic: the free-escape boundary that our transport model uses for the size limit (H1b) has a
  physical counterpart in focusing along a diverging field, which matters for the PSP shock near the Sun.
  The position of that boundary is then an input with a meaning, not only a box edge. The abstract does not
  say which species the runs follow.
