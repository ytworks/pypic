# Jokipii (1987): Rate of energy gain and maximum energy in diffusive shock acceleration

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 313, 842 (1987); DOI 10.1086/165022; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In diffusive shock acceleration the direction of the mean magnetic field at the shock has a large effect
on the rate of energy gain: "If the perpendicular diffusion coefficient is much smaller than the parallel
coefficient, particles can gain much more energy if the shock is quasi-perpendicular than if it is
quasi-parallel."

## Method
- Analytic theory of diffusive shock acceleration. Details are not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- The maximum energy at a quasi-perpendicular shock "can be substantially higher (by a factor of 100 or
  more) than previous discussions would predict".
- "The energy gain increases as kappa-perpendicular decreases."

## Assessment
- For this topic: background for the equatorial side of a sphere. It concerns particles that are already
  fast enough for the diffusion approximation.
- Stated limit: "diffusion be a valid approximation to the particle motion, and that the particle be able
  to diffuse fast enough to encounter the shock many times". The injection of thermal electrons is not in
  the abstract.
