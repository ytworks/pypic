# Kang, Ryu & Ha (2019): Electron Preacceleration in Weak Quasi-perpendicular Shocks in High-beta Intracluster Medium

- Ref: ApJ 876, 79; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ab16d1. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
In weak quasi-perpendicular shocks of a high-β plasma, electrons are pre-accelerated by shock drift
acceleration together with the electron firehose instability, and only above a critical Mach number.

## Method
- 2-D PIC of weak quasi-perpendicular shocks at intracluster conditions.

## Numbers to compare against
- Critical sonic Mach number ≈ 2.3 (the number stands in the abstract; the symbol before it is lost in
  the stored text).

## Assessment
- Even above the critical Mach number, "electrons may not reach high enough energies to be injected to the
  full Fermi-I process of DSA, because long-wavelength waves are not developed via the EFI alone". Fossil
  relativistic electrons may be needed.
- For this topic: the border regime of weak shocks, from the quasi-perpendicular side. It gives a published
  example of the answer form "pre-acceleration occurs, injection into DSA does not, because the waves at
  the required wavelength are missing", which is the form a negative result of our 1-au run W (M_A 3)
  would take. It also names seeds (fossil electrons) as the way out, as candidate H4 does. Not
  quasi-parallel; high β.
