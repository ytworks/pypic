# Kilpua, Lumme, Andreeova, Isavnin & Koskinen (2015): Properties and drivers of fast interplanetary shocks near the orbit of the Earth (1995–2013)

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Physics 120, 4112–4125 (2015); DOI 10.1002/2015JA021138; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract (from search-engine excerpts of the abstract; publisher page and ADS returned
  403/405); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
679 fast shocks observed near 1 au (Wind, ACE, STEREO-A, 1995–2013): fast-forward shocks dominate except at solar
minimum; CMEs drive most fast-forward shocks and slow–fast stream interaction regions (SIRs) drive nearly all
fast-reverse shocks. CME-driven shock occurrence and speeds follow the sunspot cycle; shock strength (Mach number,
field and density jumps) varies little over the cycle; CME-driven shocks are stronger and faster on average.

## Method
- Shocks from the Heliospheric Shock Database (ipshocks.fi, University of Helsinki), identified visually with
  one procedure; parameters from up/downstream averages.
- Driver association (CME vs SIR) and solar-cycle phase binning.

## Numbers to compare against
- Sample: 679 shocks, 1995–2013.
- The abstract read gives no median M_A, M_fms, θBn or β.
- Trotta et al. (2025, Fig. 4) used 586 fast-forward shocks from this sample (1995–2009, STEREO-A + Wind) as
  the 1 au reference.

## Assessment
- The standard 1 au reference population for IP shock parameters; numbers must be taken from its figures or
  from the database itself (not read here).
- For our topic: IP shocks at 1 au are mostly CME-driven fast-forward shocks propagating into ambient solar
  wind with pre-existing turbulence; the parameter distributions (M_A, θBn, β upstream) are the quantities a
  PIC parameter choice should be placed against. To be read in full when access allows.
