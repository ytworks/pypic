## Claim
An analytic model of type II bursts: electron beams from shock drift acceleration in the foreshock of an
interplanetary shock, with reflection in the de Hoffmann–Teller frame and a loss cone that includes the
cross-shock potential, then Langmuir wave growth and conversion to radio emission.

## Method
- Analytic; applied to the event of 1998 August 26. Later papers of the group (Knock et al. 2003a,b) add a
  ripple on a global shock (known from their abstracts only).

## Assessment
- For this topic: the standard type II model evaluates single-encounter SDA at each point of a curved
  front with the local theta_Bn. The abstract does not mention multiple encounters or a cross-latitude
  path.
