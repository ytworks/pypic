# Kong, Chen, Guo, Feng, Wang, Du & Li (2015): The possible role of coronal streamers as magnetically closed structures in shock-induced energetic electrons and metric type II radio bursts

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 798, 81 (2015); DOI 10.1088/0004-637X/798/2/81; arXiv:1410.7994 (read); peer-reviewed.
  Cited as "Kong et al. 2015" by Kong et al. 2016; OpenAlex lists the year 2014. Authors and title as on
  the arXiv copy (its title starts "Possible role of coronal streamer ...").
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, model and result sections; the observation section only in outline);
  topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
Closed field lines of a streamer act as a trap that returns electrons to a coronal shock many times, so
that a planar shock crossing a streamer accelerates electrons far more than on open field.
- Question: why do two type II bursts (2011 March 25 and 27) end when the CME front passes the streamer
  cusp?
- Idea: the closed field ahead of the shock connects the front to itself.

## Method
- Observation of two events, plus test-particle electrons in prescribed fields: planar shock,
  U_sh = 600 km/s, compression 4, streamer field with the cusp at 2.5 R_sun; domain x = 1.5–3.0 R_sun,
  z = +-0.8 R_sun; E_0 = 300 eV; pitch-angle scattering every tau = 1e4 Omega_ci^-1.

## Numbers to compare against
- Maximum energy about 530 E_0 with scattering, about 48 E_0 without.
- Fractions above 5, 10, 20, 30 E_0: 8.4 %, 3.9 %, 1.1 %, 0.39 % with scattering; 2.4 %, 0.25 %, 0.005 %,
  0.002 % without.
- No electron injected on open field exceeds 5 E_0.
- Energetic electrons gather at the loop apex and are counter-streaming.

## Assessment
- Shown: multiple shock encounters along one field line when the field, not the shock, is curved.
- Stated limits: prescribed fields and scattering; planar front.
- For this topic: prior work for "one field line, two contact points"; the front here is planar, so it
  says nothing on the theta_Bn sweep of a curved front. Extended to a circular front in Kong et al. 2016.
