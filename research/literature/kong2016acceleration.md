# Kong, Chen & Guo (2016): The acceleration of electrons at a spherical coronal shock in a streamer-like coronal field

- Ref: AIP Conf. Proc. 1736, 070003 (2016); DOI 10.1063/1.4943840; conference proceedings, peer review not
  established.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.
- Not to be confused with `kong2016electron` (ApJ 821, 32), the full paper of the same group.

## Claim
"It shows that the closed field plays the role of a trapping agency of shock-accelerated electrons, allowing
for repetitive reflection and acceleration, therefore can greatly enhance the shock-electron acceleration
efficiency."

## Method
- Test-particle electrons at a spherical coronal shock; coronal field from "an analytical solution with a
  streamer-like magnetic field featured by partially open magnetic field and a current sheet at the equator
  atop the closed region"; "ad hoc pitch-angle scattering" in some runs.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. Qualitative: with scattering, electrons "injected in the open field at the shock
  flank can be accelerated to high energies as well"; "if the shock is faster or stronger, a relatively
  harder electron energy spectrum and a larger maximum energy can be achieved."

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstract): the trap effect of `kong2015possible` also holds for a spherical front.
- Limits: prescribed fields, test particles, ad hoc scattering; proceedings paper.
- For this topic (our reading): the nearest prior statement of "a spherical shock in a closed field traps
  electrons between two shock crossings". Our self-consistent PIC run must be set against it; the
  abstract gives no number to compare with.
