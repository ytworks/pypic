# Kouloumvakos et al. (2026): Wave-Growth-Limited Particle Confinement in a Near-Parallel Interplanetary Shock Observed by Parker Solar Probe

- Ref: ApJL 1011, L1; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/aeaf56 (Crossref); arXiv:2610.10067 (14 authors: Kouloumvakos,
  Jebaraj, Malkov, Berland, Gedalin, Wijsen, Cohen, Agapitov, ...). Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, the data section and the passages on the confinement energy (arXiv HTML);
  the rest skimmed; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
At the 2023-03-13 shock the boundary between confined and escaping protons (the spectral folding energy)
falls with time toward shock arrival. Read through quasi-linear marginal stability, the growth time of the
resonant waves exceeds the precursor convective time above a confinement energy of ~2.5 MeV: the rigidity a
precursor confines is set by how fast the streaming particles build their own waves.

## Method
- PSP IS(sun)IS protons: EPI-Lo ChanP (~0.07-9.5 MeV, 1 min cadence) and EPI-Hi/HET (> 10 MeV); spectra
  and first-order anisotropies with Compton-Getting correction; upstream DSA reference A_1 = 3 u_1 / v.

## Numbers to compare against
- Folding energy falls from ~6 to ~1.5 MeV as E_fold ~ Delta t^alpha, alpha ~0.34 (Delta t = time before
  shock arrival).
- One precursor convective time before arrival: E_c ~2.46 (+0.24, -0.16) MeV (Fig. 5c).
- tau_growth(E) ~ E^(1/alpha) ~ E^2.92.
- The post-shock anisotropy reverses sign at 2-5 MeV (above 3 MeV in the shock frame) (Fig. 5a).
- Instrument: EPI-Lo saturated from ~07:16 UT to ~08:55 UT, i.e. just after the shock arrival (07:13 UT);
  EPI-Hi entered its dynamic threshold mode.

## Assessment
- Protons only; "electron" appears only in the reference list.
- E_c is a confinement boundary, not the maximum energy: the spectrum continues far above it.
- The downstream intensities right after the shock come from a saturated instrument; this bears on any
  comparison of downstream spectra, electrons included.
- Same event and group as jebaraj2026wave and jebaraj2026diffusive; the three are not independent tests.
