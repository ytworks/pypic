# Koyama, Petre, Gotthelf, Hwang, Matsuura, Ozaki & Holt (1995): Evidence for shock acceleration of high-energy electrons in the supernova remnant SN1006

- Ref: Nature 378, 255–258 (1995); DOI 10.1038/378255a0; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (Semantic Scholar record; the OpenAlex record holds no abstract). The full text was not
  read. Nothing below goes beyond that text.

## Claim
X-ray observations of SN 1006 "indicate that emission from the edges of the remnant shell is dominated by
radiation from electrons accelerated to energies of [about] 100 TeV within the shock front" (the symbol
before 100 is garbled in the record).

## Method
- X-ray observations with the ASCA satellite. Nothing more is in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- Electron energy about 100 TeV.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It is X-ray evidence that the rims of SN 1006 hold electrons of very
  high energy. The abstract says "the edges of the remnant shell"; it does not relate them to the
  direction of the magnetic field.
- The abstract adds that ions "are likely to have been accelerated to similar energies"; that is an
  inference, not a measurement.
