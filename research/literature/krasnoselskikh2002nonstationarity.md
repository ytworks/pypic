# Krasnoselskikh, Lembège, Savoini & Lobzin (2002): Nonstationarity of strong collisionless quasiperpendicular shocks: Theory and full particle numerical simulations

- Ref: Phys. Plasmas 9, 1192–1209 (2002); DOI 10.1063/1.1457465; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
"the nonlinear whistler critical Mach number corresponds to the transition between stationary and
nonstationary dynamical behavior of the shock wave." Above it, a stationary nonlinear whistler wave train
cannot exist in the front, and the front overturns.

## Method
- Theory: two-fluid MHD equations at finite beta give the structure of nonlinear whistler waves and the
  nonlinear whistler critical Mach number.
- Simulation: a 1-D full-particle electromagnetic code. It shows that the transition to non-stationarity
  "is always accompanied by the disappearance of the whistler wave train within the shock front".

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract (no expression for either critical Mach number).
- Two thresholds are named: the whistler critical Mach number (above it no stationary linear wave train
  stands ahead of the ramp) and the nonlinear whistler critical Mach number.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background on front non-stationarity at oblique quasi-perpendicular shocks. A front that
  is not stationary reflects electrons in bursts (`lembege2002formation`).
- Electron acceleration is not in the abstract.
