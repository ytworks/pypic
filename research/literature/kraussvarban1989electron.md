## Claim
Test electrons in the fields of a 1-D hybrid shock reproduce the adiabatic (fast Fermi / gradient drift)
theory of electron reflection at a plane, nearly perpendicular shock; the effects of the cross-shock
potential and of unsteadiness are quantified.

## Method
- Test-particle electrons in fields from a 1-D hybrid simulation; plane shock.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. It states that the process "is most prominent close to the point where the upstream
  magnetic field is tangent to the curved shock".
