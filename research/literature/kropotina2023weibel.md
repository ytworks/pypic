# Kropotina, Petrukovich, Chugunova & Bykov (2023): Weibel-dominated quasi-perpendicular shock: hybrid simulations and in situ observations

- Ref: MNRAS 524, 2934-2944; DOI 10.1093/mnras/stad2038; arXiv:2310.13779. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract (the arXiv PDF gave no text with our extractor); topics:
  qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
A hybrid simulation reproduces quantitatively the in-situ observation of one high-Mach-number, high-β shock
in the solar wind; the ion Weibel instability acts in its foot.

## Method
- Hybrid kinetic simulation of the observed shock. "We launch virtual probes to demonstrate that the model
  quantitatively reproduces the observations".
- The parameters of the reflected ions are approximated linearly for use in analytical models.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Quasi-perpendicular, ion scales; no electron acceleration.
- For this topic: an example of the confrontation method "one event, one run at its parameters, virtual
  probes through the box". Together with `bohdan2021magnetic` (S) and `haggerty2019dhybridr` it shows that
  this method has been used on field structure, not on energetic electrons. How "quantitatively" was
  measured is not in the abstract.
