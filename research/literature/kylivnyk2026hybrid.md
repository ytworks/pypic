# Kylivnyk, Caprioli & Orusa (2026): Hybrid Simulations of Proton Acceleration at Oblique High-β Shocks

- Ref: arXiv:2607.08835. Preprint; no journal reference found.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Weak quasi-perpendicular shocks in a high-β plasma do not accelerate protons efficiently; stronger ones do;
the inclination of the field "plays a critical role".

## Method
- 3-D hybrid, θ = 80°, sonic Mach number ~3-15, β ≳ 15, "representative of cluster environments".

## Numbers to compare against
- M_s ≲ 5: efficiency ≲ 0.1 %. M_s ≳ 10: power-law tails with slope q ~4.0 and efficiency ~3 %.
- "reducing ϑ to ~45° leads to substantially higher acceleration efficiencies".

## Assessment
- Ions only (fluid electrons). The abstract links the result to radio relics, "whose polarization suggests
  that electrons are accelerated at oblique shocks".
- For this topic: a condition study of the same form as ours ("which shocks accelerate, as a function of
  Mach number and inclination"), for protons, at high β. Saturn's case has β ~20 by our input table, so this
  is the nearest hybrid result in β. It shows a threshold in Mach number between ~5 and ~10 for ions at 80°;
  nothing follows from it for electrons at 20°.
