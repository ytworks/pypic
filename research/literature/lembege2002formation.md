# Lembège & Savoini (2002): Formation of reflected electron bursts by the nonstationarity and nonuniformity of a collisionless shock front

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. 107(A3), 1037 (2002) (article number as printed in the citing works; the Crossref
  record gives none); DOI 10.1029/2001JA900128; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Electrons are reflected neither uniformly in time nor uniformly in space. "bursts of energetic reflected
electrons are formed by the shock front reformation. In addition, electrons are not reflected uniformly in
space, but packs of reflected electrons are formed along the rippled shock front."

## Method
- Two-dimensional full-particle electromagnetic simulation of "a quasi-perpendicular supercritical
  collisionless planar shock". Trajectories of preselected self-consistent electrons (not test particles)
  give the reflection conditions.
- A comparison with one-dimensional runs isolates the effect of the rippling.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract (no angle, Mach number, mass ratio or reflected fraction).
- "Four different classes of reflected electrons", sorted by the time spent inside the front.
- Two time scales of non-stationarity: the longer is the reformation by reflected ions; the shorter is
  "the propagation of front rippling along the shock front".

## Assessment
- For this topic: direct prior work on where and when electrons are reflected along a front. A census of
  reflected electrons on a sphere must separate this patchiness (ripples, reformation) from the smooth
  change of obliquity with latitude.
- The shock is planar: the non-uniformity along the front comes from ripples, not from curvature. The
  curved case by the same authors is `savoini2001twodimensional`.
- Rippling makes the bursts more diffuse "both in real and velocities space" than in one dimension.
