# Leroy & Mangeney (1984): A theory of energization of solar wind electrons by the Earth's bow shock

- Ref: Ann. Geophys. 2(4), 449–456 (1984); no DOI in the record; peer-reviewed journal.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record W1659164039). The record holds a short summary in French, not the
  authors' English abstract; the English abstract was not found (NASA NTRS, HAL: no record; ADS refused
  the request). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond that summary.

## Claim
Record text: "Theorie de la reflexion miroir adiabatique des electrons sur l'onde de choc
magnetospherique dans des zones d'intensification du champ magnetique. ... Bon accord avec des electrons
de ~50eV-50 keV observes". In English: a theory of the adiabatic mirror reflection of electrons at the
bow shock, in zones where the magnetic field increases; good agreement with observed electrons of about
50 eV to 50 keV.

## Method
- Analytic theory of adiabatic mirror reflection. The summary lists the derived quantities: "la densite,
  du flux, de l'energie par charge, de l'anisotropie de temperature et de l'angle moyen d'attaque des
  electrons" (density, flux, energy, temperature anisotropy and mean pitch angle of the electrons).

## Numbers to compare against
- Observed electron energies in agreement with the theory: about 50 eV to 50 keV.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a theory of the adiabatic mirror reflection of electrons at a shock, the subject of
  `wu1984fast` in the same year. `katou2019theory` cites both.
- The summary does not say how the shock geometry enters (obliquity, curvature). Read the full text before
  quoting a formula from it.
