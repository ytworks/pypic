# Levinson (1992): Electron injection in collisionless shocks

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 401, 73 (1992); DOI 10.1086/172039; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
"injection of thermal electrons may be controlled by self-generated whistlers, provided that the Alfven
Mach number ahead of the shock exceeds 43/(betae) exp 1/2, where betae is the ratio of thermal electron
pressure to magnetic pressure inside the shock."

## Method
- Analytic, quasi-linear theory. The diffusion coefficient is "calculated self-consistently". The upstream
  electrons are confined by "off-axis whistlers". Wave damping by induced scattering and by whistler mode
  coupling is considered.

## Numbers to compare against
- Threshold for injection by self-generated whistlers: M_A > 43 / sqrt(beta_e).
- "Mode coupling is shown to be unimportant when M(A) is greater than 13".
- Below the threshold, sub-relativistic electrons may still be accelerated by whistlers "if the thermal
  electrons are preinjected by some other mechanism".

## Assessment
- For this topic: an early injection condition in terms of M_A and beta_e, to compare with the conditions
  of later works (`amano2010critical`, `amano2022theory`).
- The obliquity of the shock is not in the abstract. Background.
