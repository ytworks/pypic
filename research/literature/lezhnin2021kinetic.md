# Lezhnin, Fox, Schaeffer, Spitkovsky, Matteucci, Bhattacharjee & Germaschewski (2021): Kinetic Simulations of Electron Pre-energization by Magnetized Collisionless Shocks in Expanding Laboratory Plasmas

- Ref: ApJL 908, L52; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/abe407. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
2-D PIC simulations with a Coulomb collision operator identify how electrons are energized at a laser-driven
magnetized shock and what an experiment needs to see it: "electron acceleration by magnetized collisionless
shocks is feasible in laboratory experiments with laser-driven expanding plasmas".

## Method
- 2-D PIC with binary Coulomb collisions, at the conditions of laser-driven expanding plasmas.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- A prediction for an experiment, not a comparison with one.
- For this topic: the laboratory boundary area. PIC is used there at the real parameters of the system,
  collisions included, so no extrapolation in speed or mass ratio is needed; that is the opposite situation
  to ours. The geometry is not given in the abstract.
