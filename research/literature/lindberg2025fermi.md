# Lindberg, Shi, Hietala, Vuorinen, Raptis, Koller & Lalti (2025): Fermi Acceleration of Electrons at Earth's Bow Shock Due To Current Sheet Interaction

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Physics 130 (9), e2025JA034314; DOI 10.1029/2025JA034314. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
In one MMS event, a solar-wind current sheet approaching the bow shock accelerates electrons from 10-20 eV
to 1-5 keV, with a power law near that of a first-order Fermi process.

## Method
- MMS case study. The data are "compared to a numerical model of Fermi acceleration considering two
  approaching magnetic mirrors and pitch angle scattering by whistler waves, and good agreement is shown".

## Numbers to compare against
- 10-20 eV → 1-5 keV.

## Assessment
- The model is a reduced one with prescribed mirrors and scattering.
- For this topic: a second example, after `liu2017fermi`, of the event confrontation used at Earth: a
  reduced Fermi model between two converging structures, compared with the measured spectrum. The upstream
  structure (a current sheet) is the cause; that is candidate H5. The energies are keV, the range of the
  seeds that `raptis2025revealing` (S) finds necessary for the relativistic events.
