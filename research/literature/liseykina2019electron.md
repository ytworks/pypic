# Liseykina, Hanusch & Malkov (2019): Electron Injection into Fermi Acceleration in Quasiparallel Collisionless Shocks: Combining Hybrid Simulations with Test Particle Acceleration

- Ref: Proceedings of the 36th International Cosmic Ray Conference, PoS(ICRC2019) 338; DOI
  10.22323/1.358.0338. Conference proceedings, not a peer-reviewed journal. The journal counterpart is
  `hanusch2020electron`.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Test-particle electrons in 1-D hybrid simulations of quasi-parallel shocks give converged electron velocity
distributions and an electron-to-proton temperature ratio that falls with Mach number and saturates at high
Mach number, in agreement with Balmer-dominated shocks.

## Method
- 1-D hybrid simulation with electrons followed as test particles.

## Numbers to compare against
- None taken; the journal paper holds the numbers.

## Assessment
- "The reduced spatial dimensionality allowed us to dramatically increase the number of macro-ions per cell
  and achieve the converged results for the velocity distributions of electrons".
- Link to observations: T_e/T_p against Mach number compared with Balmer-dominated shocks (Ghavamian 2013).
- For this topic: a published statement of the trade that our quasi-1-D set-up also makes: one dimension is
  given up to buy particles per cell and convergence. The observable confronted is a temperature ratio at
  supernova remnants, not energetic electrons at an event.
