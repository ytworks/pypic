# Liu, Lu, Angelopoulos, Hietala & Wilson (2017): Fermi acceleration of electrons inside foreshock transient cores

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Physics 122, 9248-9263; DOI 10.1002/2017JA024480. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Electrons gain energy by Fermi acceleration between the compressional boundary of a foreshock transient,
which converges on the bow shock, and the bow shock itself.

## Method
- THEMIS observations: 31 of 247 events show the signature.
- "a one-dimensional test particle simulation of an ideal Fermi acceleration model in fluctuating fields
  prescribed by the observations can reproduce the observed evolution of electron distributions, energy
  increase rate, and pitch angle isotropy".

## Numbers to compare against
- 31 of 247 foreshock transients.

## Assessment
- The fields of the model are prescribed from the data; nothing is self-consistent.
- For this topic: the standard form of event confrontation at Earth: a test-particle model in fields taken
  from the observation, compared with the measured evolution of the distribution. It tests sufficiency of a
  mechanism, as `shi2025compound` does. It supports candidate H5 (transients) from the observational side.
  Our runs have a uniform upstream and contain no transient of this kind.
