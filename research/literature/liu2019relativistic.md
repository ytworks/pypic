# Liu, Angelopoulos & Lu (2019): Relativistic electrons generated at Earth's quasi-parallel bow shock

- Ref: Science Advances 5(7), eaaw1368; DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aaw1368. Peer reviewed; open access.
- Read: 2026-11-14, full main text (abstract, results, discussion, methods; supplementary figures not
  seen); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Relativistic electrons upstream of Earth's quasi-parallel bow shock come from the interaction of the shock
with a foreshock transient: electrons accelerated at the bow shock to tens of keV enter a foreshock bubble
and gain energy twice by betatron acceleration. Transients raise the electron energy at a quasi-parallel
shock by an order of magnitude and should be treated as part of shock acceleration.

## Method
- THEMIS in situ observations of a foreshock bubble (two more events in the supplement); electron phase
  space density (PSD) 10 eV-700 keV from two instruments.
- A hybrid simulation of a foreshock bubble for the field-line geometry only; a betatron model with
  pitch-angle scattering (energy ~ B^(2/3)).

## Numbers to compare against
- Electrons of 100-400 keV inside the bubble.
- Energy at fixed PSD rises from ~80-100 keV in the expansion region to ~100-200 keV in the compression
  region; field increase ~2; E ~ B^(2/3), not ~ B (fig. S1), with field fluctuations as large as the
  background field isotropizing the electrons.
- Highest energy above the instrument noise rises from ~200 to ~300 keV between the two regions.
- Crossing the transient's shock raises the energy up to fourfold, the pile-up in the core two- to
  threefold; together "an order-of-magnitude increase".
- PSD slope ~4 in the transient and in the background foreshock: the source is the background foreshock
  population (tens of keV), whose slope betatron acceleration does not change.

## Assessment
- The bow shock alone gives tens of keV in this picture; everything above comes from the transient. This
  is the Earth case of "no relativistic electrons without a transient" (see also wilson2016relativistic).
- The energy gain is adiabatic compression of an existing population. The paper does not model how the
  tens-of-keV population forms at the quasi-parallel bow shock.
- One main event; transients need a solar wind discontinuity, i.e. an upstream structure that a uniform
  upstream simulation does not contain (H5 of the topic).

  condition; raptis2025multi follows the electrons downstream.
