# Liu, Song, Cao, Liu, Li, Russell, Pollock, Lindqvist, Burch & Lu (2026): Efficient acceleration of energetic electrons upstream of Earth's bow shock

- Ref: Nat. Commun. 17 (2026); DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-72197-y; peer-reviewed; open access (read).
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, results, discussion; methods in outline);
  topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
MMS observed electrons accelerated to tens of keV upstream of the bow shock on V-shaped field lines, made
by a rotational discontinuity meeting the shock, that are connected to the shock at both ends: the electrons
are reflected many times as the field lines shorten.

## Method
- Observation (MMS, 5 January 2019), with a test-particle example (E = 500 eV, pitch angle 50 deg) and a
  Liouville mapping.

## Numbers to compare against
- Bidirectional beams of 2–30 keV; phase-space density of 1.1, 2.4, 5.5 keV electrons up by 1–2 orders of
  magnitude; solar-wind electrons of about 17 eV reach > 200 k_B T_e.
- Geometry: "V-shaped field lines in the trap zone move towards and gradually pass" the shock; electrons
  bounce "on the shrinking field lines".
- It notes that "a previous study surmised that the curved magnetic field lines in the solar wind might
  establish a similar bidirectional connection state to the shock" (its ref. 31; not obtained).

## Assessment
- Shown: an observed case of multiple encounters between two contact points of one field line.
- For this topic: as in Kong et al. 2016 the trap comes from a bent field line, not from the convex front
  alone. It is the newest statement of the "two contact points, shrinking field line" mechanism.
