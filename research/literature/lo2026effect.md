# Lo, Guo, Giacalone, Zhao, Lario, Koban, Ho & Cohen (2026): The effect of large-scale magnetic fluctuations on energetic particle profiles across interplanetary shocks

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 1002, 10 (2026) (Crossref); DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ae5523; arXiv id not found; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Test-particle and hybrid simulations of a strong interplanetary shock moving into a turbulent magnetic field
reproduce the variety of observed energetic-storm-particle time-intensity profiles; the intensity peak is near but
often shifted from the shock, usually downstream.

## Method
- Test particles in prescribed fields and 2D(?) hybrid simulations (kinetic protons, fluid electrons) with upstream
  turbulence. Dimensionality and parameters not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Recent work of the Guo-Giacalone group continuing the large-scale-fluctuation programme for ions/energetic
  particles; no electron or fully kinetic content according to the abstract.
- Shows the group's current line (2026) is still hybrid/test-particle: no sign in the abstract of a fully kinetic
  test of the meandering picture.
