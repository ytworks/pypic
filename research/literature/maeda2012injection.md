# Maeda (2012): Injection and Acceleration of Electrons at A Strong Shock: Radio and X-ray Study of Young Supernova 2011dh

- Ref: ApJ 758, 81 (2012) (volume and article number from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1088/0004-637X/758/2/81; arXiv:1209.1466 (v1, "accepted for publication in ApJ on August 20,
  2012"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
A model of the radio and X-ray emission of the Type IIb supernova 2011dh in its first 100 days gives the
energy distribution of relativistic electrons in the pre-acceleration range. The electron acceleration
efficiency "must be low". Radio and X-rays agree only "if the pre-acceleration injection spectrum peaks at
gamma ~ 20-30 and then only a fraction of these electrons eventually experience the DSA-like acceleration
toward the higher energy". The findings "support the idea that the pre-acceleration of the electrons is
coupled with the generation/amplification of the magnetic field".

## Method
- Emission model for SN 2011dh, first 100 days after the explosion: radio by synchrotron, X-rays by inverse
  Compton. Fitted to the observed radio and X-ray data.

## Numbers to compare against
- epsilon_e <~ 0.01, "as compared to the conventionally assumed value of epsilon_e ~ 0.1".
- Peak of the pre-acceleration injection spectrum: gamma ~ 20-30.
- Seed electrons required by diffusive shock acceleration (DSA): gamma ~ 1-100.

## Assessment
- For this topic: an observational constraint on the output of electron pre-acceleration at a strong, young
  shock: a spectrum that peaks at gamma ~ 20-30, of which only a fraction goes on to DSA. A kinetic result on
  pre-acceleration could be compared with it in form.
- The inference is through an emission model of one supernova. The abstract gives no mechanism, no shock
  obliquity and no shock parameters, so the comparison would be qualitative.
