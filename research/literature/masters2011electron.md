# Masters et al. (2011): Electron heating at Saturn's bow shock

- Ref: JGR 116, A10; DOI 10.1029/2011JA016941 (10 authors: Masters, Schwartz, Henley, Thomsen, Zieger,
  Coates, Achilleos, Mitchell, Hansen, Dougherty). Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Electron heating at Saturn's bow shock takes a few per cent of the incident ram energy, and the share falls
with the Alfven Mach number.

## Method
- Cassini crossings of Saturn's bow shock. Upstream ion measurements are scarce (instrument pointing) and
  there is no upstream monitor.
- Solar wind speed from the Michigan Solar Wind Model, propagated from near-Earth data; therefore only
  crossings within +-75 days of apparent opposition: 94 crossings in 2005 and 2007.

## Numbers to compare against
- Electron heating ~3-7 % of the incident ram energy, decreasing with M_A.
- M_A up to ~150.

## Assessment
- The Mach numbers rest on a propagated solar wind model, not on local measurements.
- For this topic: the source behind "M_A ~100" of the 2007-02-03 crossing (masters2013electron cites it as
  previously reported). The M_A of the Saturn case is therefore a model value; its error is not given in
  what was read.
