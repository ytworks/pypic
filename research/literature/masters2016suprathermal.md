# Masters, Sulaiman, Sergis, Stawarz, Fujimoto, Coates & Dougherty (2016): Suprathermal electrons at Saturn's bow shock

- Ref: ApJ 826, 48; DOI 10.3847/0004-637X/826/1/48; arXiv:1606.01685. Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, full (arXiv v1); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Over all Cassini crossings of Saturn's bow shock, a suprathermal electron signature above 18 keV is rare
(31 of 508 crossings) and usually stays below 100 keV. The authors read the set as consistent with an
injection of thermal electrons by whistler waves at the shock front that becomes possible for all field
orientations at high Mach number.

## Method
- Cassini CAPS-ELS (0.5 eV-26 keV) and MIMI-LEMMS (above 18 keV) at 508 usable shock crossings.
- Upstream conditions from a model; the crossings are placed in a parameter plane with lines of constant
  M_A (Fig. 4, after Sulaiman et al. 2015).
- Magnetic connection to the magnetopause is checked for each event to judge leakage of magnetospheric
  electrons.

## Numbers to compare against
- LEMMS signature at 31 of 508 crossings. At 28 of them only the lowest channels (< 100 keV) are above
  background; at 3 all channels are (up to ~1 MeV).
- Leakage from the magnetosphere can be ruled out for 26 of the 31.
- The signatures tend to occur at high solar wind dynamic pressure; Fig. 4a "does not indicate any clear
  dependence" on M_A.
- The thermal electron distribution downstream is resolved by ELS at all crossings; its higher channels
  are at or near background.
- The IMF near Saturn drops to ~0.1 nT only rarely, and M_A then reaches order 100.

## Assessment
- Cassini meets mostly quasi-perpendicular shocks, so the set says little about the quasi-parallel side:
  the 2007-02-03 crossing of masters2013electron stays the only quasi-parallel case with relativistic
  electrons.
- The whistler-injection reading is a consistency argument (after amano2010critical; note on trunk); the
  waves are not measured here.
- The three ~1 MeV crossings are left to "a future dedicated study" (masters2017situ covers two crossings).
- Detection is set by the LEMMS background: "no signature" is an upper limit on intensity, not on
  acceleration.
