# Masuda, Kosugi, Hara, Tsuneta & Ogawara (1994): A loop-top hard X-ray source in a compact solar flare as evidence for magnetic reconnection

- Ref: Nature 371(6497), 495–497 (1994); DOI 10.1038/371495a0; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (publisher page, nature.com; OpenAlex and Crossref hold no abstract). The full text was not
  obtained. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Yohkoh hard and soft X-ray images "show a compact flare with a geometry similar to that of a two-ribbon
flare". "We identify the reconnection region as the site of particle acceleration, suggesting that the basic
physics of the reconnection process (which remains uncertain) may be common to both types of flare."

## Method
- Observation: Yohkoh Hard X-ray Telescope and Soft X-ray Telescope, one compact flare.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. The title names the result: a hard X-ray source at the loop top. Its height, energy
  band and spectrum are not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstract): an acceleration site in the corona above the flare loop, read as the
  reconnection region. The abstract gives no number.
- Later work (not read here; seen only as OpenAlex records): Alexander & Metcalf 1997 (ApJ 489, 442) and
  Petrosian, Donaghy & McTiernan 2002 (ApJ 569, 459) re-analyse the event and the statistics of loop-top
  sources.
- For this topic (our reading): the observational anchor for "energetic electrons concentrate at the top of
  a closed loop". The abstract attributes it to reconnection, not to a shock sweeping a closed field; a
  loop-top concentration in our runs would be a shape to compare, not a confirmation of the same mechanism.
