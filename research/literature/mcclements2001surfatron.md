## Claim
"Surfatron acceleration of electrons by waves excited by ions reflected from supernova remnant (SNR)
shocks is investigated using particle simulations. It is shown that surfatron and stochastic acceleration
could provide a seed population for the acceleration of cosmic ray electrons at SNR shocks."
