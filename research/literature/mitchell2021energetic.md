# Mitchell et al. (2021): Energetic Electron Observations by Parker Solar Probe/ISOIS during the First Widespread SEP Event of Solar Cycle 25 on 2020 November 29

- Ref: ApJ 919, 119; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ac110e (14 authors: Mitchell, De Nolfo, Hill, Christian,
  Richardson, McComas, McNutt, Mitchell, ...). Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
During the passage of two interplanetary coronal mass ejections at PSP (0.81 au), the near-relativistic
electron count rate peaks at the shock driven by the faster one, "implying that the shock parameters of
this ICME are conducive to the acceleration of electrons".

## Method
- PSP energetic particle suite, low-energy instrument (EPI-Lo): time profile and the first electron
  anisotropy measurements of that instrument; magnetometer for context.

## Numbers to compare against
- PSP at ~0.81 au; event starting 2020-11-29.
- Inside the magnetic clouds: electrons mainly parallel, antiparallel or bidirectional to the field.

## Assessment
- Abstract only: theta_Bn, Mach number and the electron energies are not in it.
- An electron peak at a shock at 0.81 au, i.e. a further "yes" candidate. Without the shock geometry
  it cannot be placed among the cases of this topic; the full text is needed before any use.
