# Morikawa, Ohira & Ohmura (2024): Particle acceleration and magnetic field amplification by relativistic shocks in inhomogeneous media

- Ref: Astrophys. J. Lett. 969, L1 (2024) (Crossref); DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ad50a2; arXiv:2405.15138; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
In 3D relativistic MHD with test particles, a relativistic shock hitting dense clumps amplifies the downstream field
to GRB-inferred levels; for large upstream density fluctuations, low-energy particles are first raised to the
injection energy by downstream turbulence and then rapidly accelerated by the shock.

## Method
- 3D relativistic MHD of a shock in a clumpy medium; test particles in the stored fields. Parameters not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Not kinetic; relativistic; density (not Alfvenic) inhomogeneity. Relevant only as a two-step injection picture
  (downstream turbulence, then shock) that competes with the at-front meandering picture.
