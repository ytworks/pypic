# Nakanotani, Zank & Zhao (2021): Interaction between multiple current sheets and a shock wave: 2D hybrid kinetic simulations

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 922, 219 (2021) (Crossref); DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ac2e06; no arXiv version found; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Upstream current sheets compressed by a shock become tearing-unstable downstream; the fields turn turbulent and
magnetic islands form well behind the shock, where protons are efficiently energized above 100 E0
(E0 = m_p V_A^2). A "post-cursor" region of decelerated flow (about 1 V_A, downstream frame) sits just behind the
shock.

## Method
- 2D hybrid (kinetic protons, fluid electrons), multiple current sheets imposed upstream. Parameters not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- Energetic protons above 100 E0 produced some distance downstream (abstract).
- Post-cursor flow about 1 V_A immediately behind the shock, then decelerating to 0 (abstract).

## Assessment
- Upstream structure is current sheets, not a turbulent spectrum; acceleration is downstream (islands), not at the
  front. Relevant as an alternative channel that a fully kinetic run with upstream structures must separate from
  front-related injection.
- Electrons fluid; no electron result.
