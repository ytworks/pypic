# Nersisyan & Osipyan (2009): Collisionless plasma expansion in the presence of a dipole magnetic field

- Ref: Contrib. Plasma Phys. 49(6), 351–361 (2009); DOI 10.1002/ctpp.200910032; arXiv:0905.0051;
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv record; OpenAlex holds the same text). The full text is open on arXiv but was not
  read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
"It is shown that the plasma cloud expands excluding the ambient magnetic field and the background plasma
to form a diamagnetic cavity which is accompanied by the generation of a collisionless shock wave."

## Method
- "2D3V hybrid (kinetic ions and massless fluid electrons) model", particle-in-cell, an "expanding
  high-energy plasma cloud" in a "magnetized background plasma in the presence of a dipole magnetic field",
  "high Alfvén–Mach numbers".
- The energy exchange between cloud and background is compared with "the analytical model suggested
  previously" ("qualitative agreement").

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstract): a cloud expanding in a dipole field forms a diamagnetic cavity and drives a
  collisionless shock; the retardation of the cloud is studied.
- Limit: hybrid with massless fluid electrons, so no electron kinetics and no electron acceleration; 2D.
- For this topic (our reading): the closest set-up found to ours (an explosion in a dipole with a
  background plasma), but at the ion and fluid-electron level. It can be a check of the cavity and shock
  formation; it cannot speak to electrons trapped between two crossings of the front.
