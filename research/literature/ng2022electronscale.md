# Ng, Chen, Bessho, Shuster, Burkholder & Yoo (2022): Electron-scale reconnection in three-dimensional shock turbulence

- Ref: Geophys. Res. Lett. 49, e2022GL099544 (2022) (volume and article number from the Crossref record of
  the DOI); DOI 10.1029/2022GL099544; arXiv:2205.01715 (v1); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In a 3-D PIC simulation of a quasi-parallel shock, "The shock transition region is turbulent, leading to the
formation of reconnecting current sheets with various orientations". For two sites, with weak and strong
guide fields, "reconnection is fast and transient". "In contrast to two-dimensional simulations, weak guide
field reconnection is realized", and "the current sheets in these events form in a direction almost
perpendicular to those found in two-dimensional simulations, where the reconnection geometry is constrained".

## Method
- 3-D particle-in-cell simulation of a quasi-parallel shock. Mach number, obliquity, mass ratio and box
  size: not in the abstract.
- Reconnection sites are characterized with "diagnostics including electron flows and magnetic flux
  transport".

- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It shows that the transition region of a quasi-parallel shock in 3-D has
  electron-scale reconnection sites that a 2-D run does not have in the same geometry. That is one more
  reason for 3-D at the polar regions of our sphere.
- The abstract says nothing on electron acceleration or heating by these sites. It names no specific shock;
  the arXiv subject class is Space Physics.
