# Niemiec, Pohl, Bret & Wieland (2012): Nonrelativistic parallel shocks in unmagnetized and weakly magnetized plasmas

- Ref: ApJ 759, 73 (2012) (volume and article number from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1088/0004-637X/759/1/73; arXiv:1209.3938 (v1); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Non-relativistic shocks "in both unmagnetized and magnetized plasmas can be mediated by Weibel-type
instabilities"; "the efficiency of shock-formation processes is higher when a large-scale magnetic field is
present". "We do not see any significant evidence of pre-acceleration, neither in the electron population
nor in the ion distribution."

## Method
- 2D3V PIC simulations of "non-relativistic plasma collisions with absent or parallel large-scale magnetic
  field for parameters applicable to the conditions at young supernova remnants".
- Collision of plasma slabs of different density, "leading to two different shocks and a contact
  discontinuity".

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.
- Downstream electron distributions "are generally isotropic, whereas that is not always the case for the
  ions".

## Assessment
- For this topic: a null result at the parallel end (theta_Bn = 0, the poles of our sphere), in a set-up
  that is close to ours in kind: two plasmas collide, and a forward shock, a reverse shock and a contact
  discontinuity form. A debris-driven front has the same three-part structure.
- It warns that a parallel shock in a PIC box of feasible size may show no pre-acceleration at all. A null
  at the poles of our runs would then not be new. Run times and box sizes are not in the abstract, so it
  cannot be said from it whether the null is one of duration.
