# Ohira & Takahara (2008): Oblique Ion Two-Stream Instability in the Foot Region of a Collisionless Shock

- Ref: ApJ 688, 320-326 (2008) (volume and pages from the Crossref record of the DOI); DOI 10.1086/592182;
  arXiv:0808.3195 (v1, "accepted for publication in ApJ"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In the foot of a high Mach number perpendicular shock, in two dimensions, the Buneman instability first heats
electrons; then a "highly oblique ion two-stream instability grows to heat mainly ions". This "is in contrast
to previous studies based on one-dimensional simulations, for which ion acoustic instability further heats
electrons". The result "implies that overheating problem of electrons for shocks in supernova remnants is
resolved".

## Method
- Two-dimensional linear analysis of a "relatively cold unmagnetized" plasma with a reflected proton beam.
- Electrostatic PIC simulations, "double periodic and taken as a proxy for the situation in the foot". The
  initial state satisfies the Buneman instability condition. No shock is simulated.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.
- Linear result: when the electron temperature is much higher than the proton temperature, the most unstable
  mode is the highly oblique ion two-stream instability, "rather than the ion acoustic instability that is
  dominant for parallel propagation".

## Assessment
- For this topic: it concerns electron heating in the foot at the perpendicular end, not acceleration. Its
  use is the warning that one-dimensional results overheat electrons there, and that "multi-dimensional
  analysis is crucial". Our runs are 3-D.
- Limits readable from the abstract: electrostatic, unmagnetized, periodic box. The predecessor is
  `ohira2007absence`.
