# Oka, Terasawa, Seki, Fujimoto, Kasaba, Kojima, Shinohara, Matsui, Matsumoto, Saito & Mukai (2006): Whistler critical Mach number and electron acceleration at the bow shock: Geotail observation

- Ref: Geophys. Res. Lett. 33, L24104 (2006); DOI 10.1029/2006GL028156; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract (Semantic Scholar record; full text not reached); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
At Earth's bow shock (Geotail), intense whistlers stand upstream in the sub-critical regime M_A < M_crit^w but
not in the super-critical regime. The whistler critical Mach number also appears to regulate electron
acceleration: the hardest electron spectra in the shock transition layer occur for M_A > M_crit^w.

## Method
- Geotail bow-shock crossings; electron energy spectra f(E) ∝ E^−Γ in the transition layer; classification
  by M_A relative to M_crit^w.

## Numbers to compare against
- Γ = 3.5–5.0 for M_A < M_crit^w; Γ = 3–3.5 (hardest) for M_A > M_crit^w (abstract).
- M_crit^w = 0.5 (mi/me)^½ |cosθBn| in the linear form used by Lindberg et al. (2024, Sect. 4); ≈ 21 |cosθBn|
  for real mass ratio (my arithmetic), e.g. ≈ 10.7 at θBn = 60°, ≈ 3.7 at 80°.

## Assessment
- The statistical baseline cited by Amano et al. (2024) and Lindberg et al. (2024): bow-shock electron spectra
  harden above M_crit^w, consistent with SSDA thresholds that fall with M_A/cosθBn.
- Bow shock only; typical IP shocks (M_A ≲ 3, Trotta et al. 2025) are below M_crit^w for θBn ≲ 75°, which is the
  regime where pre-existing turbulence could matter most for electrons.
- Number of crossings and statistics not read (abstract only).
- For PIC with reduced mass ratio: M_crit^w scales as (mi/me)^½, so the sub/super-critical boundary moves with
  the mass ratio and must be matched, not M_A alone.
