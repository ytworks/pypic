# Oka, Terasawa, Seki, Fujimoto, Kasaba, Kojima, Shinohara, Matsui et al. (2006): Whistler critical Mach number and electron acceleration at the bow shock: Geotail observation

- Ref: Geophys. Res. Lett. 33, L24104 (2006); DOI 10.1029/2006GL028156; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
The whistler critical Mach number M_crit^w "seems to regulate the electron acceleration efficiency" at the
Earth's bow shock: the hardest electron spectra are found in the super-critical regime, M_A > M_crit^w.

## Method
- Geotail data at the bow shock. Electron energy spectra at the shock transition layer are fitted with
  f(E) proportional to E^(-Gamma).
- Whistler waves are intense for M_A < M_crit^w and not seen in the super-critical regime.

## Numbers to compare against
- Sub-critical regime: Gamma between 3.5 and 5.0.
- Super-critical regime: "the hardest energy spectra with Gamma = 3–3.5".

## Assessment
- For this topic: an in-situ benchmark for the spectral index of electrons at the transition layer, and
  evidence that one threshold in Mach number orders the efficiency.
- The dependence of M_crit^w on the obliquity is not in the abstract, and the abstract only says "we
  discuss a possible relationship". The number of crossings is not in the abstract.
