# Oka, Wilson, Phan, Hull, Amano, Hoshino, Argall, Le Contel et al. (2017): Electron scattering by high-frequency whistler waves at Earth's bow shock

- Ref: Astrophys. J. Lett. 842, L11 (2017); DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/aa7759; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
"we report in situ evidence of pitch-angle scattering of non-thermal electrons by whistler waves at
Earth's bow shock."

## Method
- One crossing by the Magnetospheric Multiscale (MMS) mission on 2015 November 4.
- Electron velocity distributions at 30 ms cadence; the flux of 0.5 keV electrons at 1 ms cadence.

## Numbers to compare against
- Shock: Alfvén Mach number about 11, shock angle about 84 deg.
- In the ramp and overshoot: bursts of non-thermal (0.5–2 keV) electron flux, correlated with
  parallel-propagating whistler waves at 0.2–0.4 of the cyclotron frequency (the symbol is dropped in the
  record).
- The phase-space density gradient is enhanced where the parallel electron velocity matches the resonant
  energy of the waves.

## Assessment
- For this topic: observed pitch-angle scattering of non-thermal electrons in the ramp and overshoot of a
  nearly perpendicular shock. `katou2019theory` cites this work.
- Stated limit: "the precise role of the pitch-angle scattering ... in the electron acceleration process
  remains unclear." It is one local crossing; the curvature of the bow shock plays no part in the abstract.
