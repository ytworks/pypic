# Orlando, Bocchino, Reale, Peres & Petruk (2007): On the Origin of Asymmetries in Bilateral Supernova Remnants

- Ref: A&A 470, 927-939 (2007) (volume and pages from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1051/0004-6361:20066045; arXiv:0704.0890 (v1, "accepted for publication in A&A"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Bilateral remnants with two radio limbs of different brightness "can be explained if a gradient of ambient
density or, most likely, of ambient magnetic field strength is perpendicular to the radio limbs". Remnants
"with converging similar radio arcs can be explained if the gradient runs between the two arcs".

## Method
- 3-D MHD simulations "of a spherical SNR shock propagating through a magnetized ISM". Two cases: a gradient
  of ambient density with a uniform field; a homogeneous medium with a gradient of field strength.
- Synthetic synchrotron radio emission, "making different assumptions about the details of acceleration and
  injection of relativistic electrons".

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. It says that parameters quantifying the degree of asymmetry are derived; their values
  are not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: the fluid-scale counterpart of the geometry studied here, a spherical shock in a magnetized
  medium with theta_Bn varying over the front. Electron injection against obliquity is an assumption that is
  varied, not a result.
- The abstract says the asymmetry parameters "may provide a powerful diagnostic of the microphysics of strong
  shock waves". That is the place where a kinetic result on injection against theta_Bn would enter. The
  abstract does not say which injection assumption fits the observations.
