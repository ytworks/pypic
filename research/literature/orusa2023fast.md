# Orusa & Caprioli (2023): Fast particle acceleration in 3D hybrid simulations of quasiperpendicular shocks

- Ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 131, 095201 (2023); DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.131.095201; arXiv:2305.10511;
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
"Unlike 2D simulations, 3D runs show that protons develop a nonthermal tail spontaneously (i.e., from the
thermal bath and without preexisting magnetic turbulence). They are rapidly accelerated via shock drift
acceleration up to a maximum energy determined by their escape upstream."

## Method
- Hybrid simulations (kinetic ions, fluid electrons) of "nonrelativistic, weakly magnetized,
  quasiperpendicular shocks", in two and in three dimensions.
- The Mach number and the obliquity are not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a warning on dimension. For ions at a quasi-perpendicular shock the abstract says that
  three dimensions change the answer from no tail to a tail. Our run is three-dimensional, and its
  equator is the quasi-perpendicular case.
- It is about protons, with fluid electrons; electron acceleration is not in the abstract. The abstract
  also states that before this work "no self-consistent kinetic simulation has reported nonthermal tails
  at quasiperpendicular shocks".
