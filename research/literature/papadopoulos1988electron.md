# Papadopoulos (1988): Electron heating in superhigh Mach number shocks

- Ref: Astrophys. Space Sci. 144, 535–547 (1988); DOI 10.1007/BF00793203; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (NASA NTRS record 19880064458; the OpenAlex and Crossref records hold no abstract; the
  publisher page refused the request). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Fluid and MHD models, and the extrapolation of bow-shock data, predict a negligible downstream electron
pressure at very high Mach numbers. With "proper plasma physics considerations in the interaction of the
reflected and transmitted ions and the electrons at the 'foot' of the shock" the result is that "electron
heating can dominate" in the superhigh Mach number range.

## Method
- Theory, using plasma-physics experiments and magnetospheric observations as guides. "A stationary model
  of the shock structure is derived", an extrapolation of high Mach number shock physics "with
  incorporation of collective interactions at the foot".

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract (the Mach number that separates "high" from "superhigh" is not given).

## Assessment
- For this topic: a statement that collective interactions of the reflected ions, the transmitted ions
  and the electrons in the foot can make electron heating dominate at very high Mach numbers. The
  abstract does not name the instabilities.
- It is about heating, with a stationary model. Acceleration to non-thermal energies and the obliquity
  are not in the abstract. Background.
