# Petruk, Dubner, Castelletti, Bocchino, Iakubovskyi, Kirsch, Miceli, Orlando & Telezhinsky (2009): Aspect angle for interstellar magnetic field in SN 1006

- Ref: MNRAS 393, 1034-1040 (2009) (volume and pages from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2966.2008.14251.x; arXiv:0811.2319 (v1, "Accepted for publication in MNRAS");
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
The azimuthal profile of the radio surface brightness of a remnant gives the aspect angle between the ambient
field and the line of sight, for an assumed dependence of the electron injection efficiency on the shock
obliquity. For SN 1006 the quasi-parallel injection model needs a small aspect angle, for which the remnant
"is expected to have a centrally-peaked morphology contrary to what is observed". The analysis "provides some
indication against the quasi-parallel injection model".

## Method
- Comparison of observed and synthesized radio maps, "making different assumptions about the dependence of
  electron injection efficiency on the shock obliquity".
- The azimuthal brightness profile is used because "it is almost insensitive to the downstream distribution
  of magnetic field and emitting electrons".
- Data: a new radio image of SN 1006 from archival VLA and Parkes data, with angular scales "from few
  arcseconds to 15 arcmin".

## Numbers to compare against
- Best-fitting aspect angle of SN 1006: phi = 70 +/- 4.2 deg for isotropic injection; phi = 64 +/- 2.8 deg for
  quasi-perpendicular injection (equatorial belt in both cases); phi = 11 +/- 0.8 deg for quasi-parallel
  injection (polar caps).

## Assessment
- For this topic: it frames the observational question that a spherical front in a uniform field poses, namely
  at which theta_Bn electrons are injected. The injection law is assumed, not derived. The work constrains
  which assumed law fits the radio map of one remnant.
- Its conclusion is opposite to that of `schneiter20153d` (polarization, "strong support to the quasi-parallel
  model"). By the two abstracts the observational question is not settled.
