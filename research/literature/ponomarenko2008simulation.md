# Ponomarenko, Zakharov, Antonov et al. (2008): Simulation of strong magnetospheric disturbances in laser-produced plasma experiments

- Ref: Plasma Phys. Control. Fusion 50(7), 074015 (2008); DOI 10.1088/0741-3335/50/7/074015; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
"In a general perspective, the most important results have been obtained on the interaction of an expanding
plasma cloud with a uniform magnetized background, on the dynamics of an exploding plasma in a dipole
magnetic field and on the formation of an 'artificial magnetosphere' by a laser-produced plasma overflowing
magnetic dipole."

## Method
- Review of a series of laboratory experiments on the laser facility KI-1 (ILP SB RAS, Novosibirsk):
  laser-produced plasma clouds in a magnetised background and in a dipole field.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstract): the KI-1 programme includes an exploding plasma inside a dipole field, the
  laboratory analogue of our set-up. The abstract gives no result on particle acceleration.
- For this topic (our reading): the laboratory line of work to check for measured front speeds, cavity sizes
  and any energetic-electron diagnostic in the dipole case; to be read in full if one is needed. A related
  record from the same group: Shaikhislamov et al. 2014 (PPCF 56, 125007), a laser plasma with a frozen-in
  field compressing a dipole.
