# Raptis et al. (2025): Multi-Mission Observations of Relativistic Electrons and High-Speed Jets Linked to Shock Generated Transients

- Ref: ApJL 981, L10; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/adb154; arXiv:2411.12815 (22 authors: Raptis, Lindberg, Liu,
  Turner, Lalti, Zhou, Kajdic, Kouloumvakos, ...). Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusions (arXiv HTML); results skimmed;
  topics: qpar-electron-conditions
- Not to be confused with raptis2025revealing (Nature Communications; note on
  `research/spherical-shock-injection`), which holds the seed-electron comparison.

## Claim
Hot flow anomalies formed upstream of Earth's quasi-parallel bow shock are transmitted through it and keep
their structure downstream. The relativistic electrons they carry stay confined inside the transmitted
structures and gain further energy at the crossing, which the authors attribute to betatron acceleration.

## Method
- MMS upstream and Cluster downstream of the bow shock during the same interval; fields, plasma and
  energetic electrons.

## Numbers to compare against
- Electrons up to ~200 keV upstream and ~300 keV downstream, in association with the transients.
- Fast solar wind (~700 km/s) during the event, "consistent with the framework" of raptis2025revealing.
- Several transients in a row give nearly continuous fluxes above 100 keV in the magnetosheath for up to
  30 minutes; the fluxes are modulated by the transmitted structures.
- High-speed jets form at the compressive edges of the transmitted transients.

## Assessment
- One interval. The energetic electrons are tied to transients here too; the paper adds the downstream
  side, not a case without a transient.
- "Betatron" is inferred from the field compression across the shock; shares of processes are not separated.
- For this topic: downstream detections of > 100 keV electrons at Earth can be transmitted transient
  populations, so a downstream measurement alone does not show acceleration by the unperturbed shock.
