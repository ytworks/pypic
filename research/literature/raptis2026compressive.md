# Raptis et al. (2026): Compressive Structures in the Foreshock of Collisionless Shocks

- Ref: ApJL 1000, L55; DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ae53e5; arXiv:2603.17882 (14 authors: Raptis, Trotta, Turner,
  Blanco-Cano, Hietala, Karlsson, Jebaraj, Vasko, ...). Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, the event table, results summary and conclusions (arXiv HTML);
  appendices not read; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
A quasi-parallel interplanetary (IP) shock and Earth's quasi-parallel bow shock at similar Mach number both
start foreshock compressive structures (FCS) once the suprathermal ion density is high enough, but the IP
shock lacks mature SLAMS. The authors give two reasons: the region able to grow them is crossed in seconds,
and a planar shock has no lateral supply of energetic ions from other shock regions.

## Method
- Solar Orbiter at an IP shock (2022-08-31, 0.75 au) and MMS at the bow shock (2024-2025 "string of
  pearls" campaign); scales normalized to the ion inertial length, suprathermal density to the background.

## Numbers to compare against
- IP shock: theta_Bn 22 deg, M_A 5.4, V_sh 880 km/s, d_i 65 km. Bow shock: theta_Bn 39 deg, M_A 5.6,
  V_sh 50 km/s, d_i 90 km (event table).
- FCS start within <~ 50 d_i of the shock when the > 10 keV ion density exceeds ~1 % of the background.
- Region of the IP shock comparable with Earth's observations ~135 d_i, i.e. < 10 s of data.
- Curvature of the bow shock allows "cross-talk" between regions of different geometry; a planar IP shock
  does not.

## Assessment
- Ions and field structures only; electrons are not treated.
- For this topic: SLAMS are a feature of curved bow shocks, absent at this IP shock and at the PSP shock
  (jebaraj2026wave). A mechanism that needs SLAMS (masters2017situ, zekovic2025slams) cannot be the one
  acting at the PSP event; and a planar 1-D simulation has no lateral supply either (bears on H5).
- Two events; "lateral supply" is proposed, not measured.
