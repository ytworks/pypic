## Claim
"for shocks with high cosmic ray acceleration efficiency, the magnetic fields become highly disorganized,
resulting in near isotropic diffusion, independent of the initial orientation of the ambient magnetic
field."

## Method
- A three-dimensional hybrid MHD–kinetic code, with "a spherical harmonic expansion of the
  Vlasov–Fokker–Planck equation" for the cosmic rays, including their feedback on the field.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract. Qualitative results: sub-Bohm diffusion is possible for parallel shocks; "the
  diffusion coefficient approaches the Bohm limit from below for oblique shocks".

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It says that where cosmic-ray acceleration is efficient, the
  diffusion no longer depends on the initial field direction. If so, the ordering by obliquity on a
  sphere would weaken there; that is our reading, not a statement of the abstract.
- It is about cosmic rays in a precursor, not about electron injection. The scales are far beyond our
  run.
