# Reville & Bell (2013): Universal behaviour of shock precursors in the presence of efficient cosmic ray acceleration

- Ref: MNRAS 430, 2873-2884; DOI 10.1093/mnras/stt100. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
With efficient cosmic-ray acceleration the precursor field becomes disorganized, and the diffusion is nearly
isotropic and independent of the initial field orientation.

## Method
- 3-D simulation coupling MHD to a kinetic (Vlasov-Fokker-Planck, spherical harmonics) treatment of the
  cosmic rays.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- "The possibility of sub-Bohm diffusion is demonstrated for parallel shocks, while the diffusion
  coefficient approaches the Bohm limit from below for oblique shocks".
- The field is not self-similar, which gives "non-uniform energy-dependent behaviour of the energetic
  particle transport in the precursor".
- Cosmic-ray ions at high energy; the injection is not part of the model.
- For this topic: two points on what a measured diffusion coefficient can state. (1) Bohm is not a floor:
  a self-consistent model finds sub-Bohm values at parallel shocks, so our D̃_e may fall below 1 and the
  estimate "Bohm = fastest" in the plan (§5.5) is not a bound. (2) The transport depends on position and
  energy in a non-uniform way, which supports measuring D̃_e(x, p̃) as a function and not as one number.
  The neighbouring method (MHD + Vlasov-Fokker-Planck) starts above the injection, as MHD-PIC does.
