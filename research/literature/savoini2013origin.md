# Savoini, Lembège & Stienlet (2013): On the origin of the quasi-perpendicular ion foreshock: Full-particle simulations

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Phys. 118(3), 1132–1145 (2013); DOI 10.1002/jgra.50158; HAL hal-00793794
  (open copy read); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, set-up, conclusions); topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
A 2-D full-PIC curved shock forms the ion foreshock self-consistently, with field-aligned beam (FAB) and
gyro-phase-bunched (GPB) backstreaming ions. The paper has no electron-acceleration result.

## Method
- 2-1/2-D full PIC, cylindrical magnetic piston, theta_Bn from 90 to 45 deg, as Savoini et al. 2010 but
  larger: m_i/m_e = 84, v_A = 0.16, M_A about 3.8–4 at 90 deg; R_c >= 20 rho_ci after 0.3 tau_ci and
  about 140 rho_ci at the end; run about 5.3 tau_ci.

## Numbers to compare against
- Set-up values above; none for electrons.

## Assessment
- For this topic: shows that the authors' follow-up to Savoini et al. 2010 went to ions. The promised
  statistics of the three electron classes were not found in this or the later papers.
