# Savoini & Lembège (2015): Production of nongyrotropic and gyrotropic backstreaming ion distributions in the quasi-perpendicular ion foreshock region

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. Space Phys. 120(9), 7154–7171 (2015); DOI 10.1002/2015JA021018; HAL insu-01220125
  (open copy read); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, set-up, conclusions); topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
In the same 2-D full-PIC curved shock as Savoini et al. 2013, FAB and GPB ions differ by their interaction
time with the front. The paper has no electron-acceleration result.

## Method
- As Savoini et al. 2013 (m_i/m_e = 84, theta_Bn 90 to 45 deg, R_c about 140 rho_ci at the end, run about
  5.4 tau_ci).

## Numbers to compare against
- Expansion: "as R_c increases, the shock velocity slightly decreases and so, does the Mach Number from
  about 5 to about 3".
- FAB ions lose their phase coherence "after suffering several bounces along the curved front".

## Assessment
- For this topic: the only place found where the deceleration of a piston-driven curved PIC shock is
  stated with numbers (M_A 5 to 3). It is not related to electrons.
