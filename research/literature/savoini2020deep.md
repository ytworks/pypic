# Savoini & Lembège (2020): A deep insight into the ion foreshock with the help of test particle two-dimensional simulations

- Ref: Ann. Geophys. 38(6), 1217–1235 (2020); DOI 10.5194/angeo-38-1217-2020; HAL insu-02538095; open
  access (read); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, set-up, conclusions); topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
Test ions in the fields of the 2-D full-PIC curved shock separate the roles of the shock-front
nonstationarity and of the expansion for the FAB and GPB ion populations. No electron result.

## Method
- 2-D test ions in PIC fields; the fully consistent time-dependent expansion is compared with a
  "homothetic" expansion of a frozen front profile. Box 8192 x 8192 cells, about 150 rho_ci.

## Numbers to compare against
- None for electrons.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a method reference only (test particles in a frozen, self-similarly expanded front
  against the full time-dependent fields). The same comparison has not been published for electrons, as
  far as this search reached.
