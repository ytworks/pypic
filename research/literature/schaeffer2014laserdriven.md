## Claim
The platform of the Raptor laser and the Large Plasma Device lets one study the formation of magnetised
collisionless shocks, including the collisionless coupling of piston and ambient ions. A magnetosonic
pulse consistent with a low-Mach-number shock is seen.

## Method
- A laser-driven super-Alfvenic magnetic piston in a large pre-formed magnetised ambient plasma.
- Results are compared to theoretical and computational work.

## Numbers to compare against
- "a magnetosonic pulse consistent with a low-Mach number collisionless shock is observed in a
  quasi-perpendicular geometry in both experiments and simulations".
