# Schaeffer et al. (2017): High-Mach number, laser-driven magnetized collisionless shocks

## Claim
Experiments on OMEGA and OMEGA EP show the formation and evolution of high-Mach-number collisionless
shocks made by a laser-driven magnetic piston in a magnetised ambient plasma. Simulations show the shock
leaving the piston, and the data show it at late times.

## Method
- Time-resolved 2-D imaging of density and field; Thomson scattering of the ambient plasma.
- 2-D radiation-hydrodynamic simulations of the piston plasma initialise 2-D particle-in-cell simulations
  of the interaction of piston and ambient plasma.

## Numbers to compare against
- "large density and magnetic compressions that propagate at super-Alfvenic speeds and that occur over
  ion kinetic length scales".
- The simulations show "the separate dynamics of the carbon and hydrogen ions that constitute the ambient
  plasma and their effect on the shock structure".
- "The simulations also show the shock separating from the piston, which we observe in the data at late
  experimental times."
- The abstract gives no numbers. The Mach numbers and times are in the letter of the same year
  (Schaeffer et al. 2017, Phys. Rev. Lett. 119, 025001; note `schaeffer2017generation`).

## Assessment
- For this topic: the long version of the letter. It should hold the set-up of the 2-D PIC runs (box,
  mass ratio, piston profile), which the letter does not give. It is the first text to get if the piston
  profile of a strong laboratory shock is needed.
- By the abstract the piston comes from a rad-hydro ablation profile, not from a shell of fixed mass.
