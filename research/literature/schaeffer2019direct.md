# Schaeffer, Fox, Follett, Fiksel, Li, Matteucci, Bhattacharjee & Germaschewski (2019): Direct Observations of Particle Dynamics in Magnetized Collisionless Shock Precursors in Laser-Produced Plasmas

- Ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 122, 245001; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.245001. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
The first laboratory measurement of time-resolved electron and ion velocity distributions in the precursor
of a magnetized collisionless shock.

## Method
- Laser-driven piston in a magnetized plasma; Thomson scattering and proton radiography.

## Numbers to compare against
- From `orusa2025criteria` (its survey of this experiment): ~750 km/s, M_A 15, M_s 15, about 4 proton
  gyrations.

## Assessment
- "laboratory experiments can probe particle velocity distributions relevant to collisionless shocks, and
  can complement, and in some cases overcome, the limitations of similar measurements undertaken by
  spacecraft missions".
- Earlier work of the same group (Schaeffer et al. 2017, Phys. Rev. Lett. 119, 025001; DOI
  10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.025001; abstract only): a shock with magnetosonic Mach number ≈ 12 forms within
  about one gyroperiod; PIC simulations "constrained by experimental data".
- For this topic: the laboratory boundary area. The experiments are perpendicular and last a few gyro-
  periods, so they test shock formation and the precursor, not acceleration over the ages of our cases.
  What they offer is a measured distribution function at known conditions, against which a PIC code can be
  checked at the real parameters. No quasi-parallel experiment was found in what we read
  (`zhang2024kinetic`: planned).
