# Schaeffer, Fox, Follett, Fiksel, Li, Matteucci, Bhattacharjee & Germaschewski (2019): Direct Observations of Particle Dynamics in Magnetized Collisionless Shock Precursors in Laser-Produced Plasmas

- Ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 122, 245001 (2019); DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.245001; arXiv:1811.01528 (read);
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, full (arXiv version); topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
Thomson scattering resolves electron and ion velocity distributions while a piston-driven magnetised
shock forms. A strong compression of density and temperature exists already in the piston pile-up,
before a shock. The deformation of the piston flow marks the onset.

## Method
- OMEGA; laser-driven piston into a magnetised ambient plasma; Thomson scattering and proton radiography.
- Ambient: n_e0 = 0.9 +- 0.2 e18 cm^-3, T_e0 = 40 +- 10 eV. The field is 10 T near the target and falls
  off as 1/x.
- 1-D PIC (PSC) for comparison.

## Numbers to compare against
- In the pile-up region n_e/n_e0 ~ 10 and T_e/T_e0 ~ 10, "indicating that these effects are necessary but
  not sufficient signatures of shock formation".
- Peak field 35 +- 3 T; the upstream value, 6 +- 3 T, is not well constrained. Field structure speed
  790 +- 20 km/s.
- The ambient ions are swept up by the electric field of the pressure gradient at the piston edge.
- No pile-up without the field; no shock without the ambient plasma.
- The 1-D PIC shows a hydrogen shock just ahead of the piston pile-up.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a warning about diagnostics. A density or field jump at the debris edge is not a
  shock. A shock claim needs reflected ambient ions and a front that has left the piston.
- No formation threshold and no Mach number scaling is given here.
- Planar analysis; curvature and electron acceleration are not treated.
