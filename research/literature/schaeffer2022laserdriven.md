# Schaeffer, Cruz, Dorst et al. (2022): Laser-driven, ion-scale magnetospheres in laboratory plasmas. I. Experimental platform and first results

## Claim
"We find that distinct 2D kinetic-scale magnetopause and diamagnetic current structures are formed at higher
dipole moments, and their locations are consistent with predictions based on pressure balances and energy
conservation."

## Method
- Large Plasma Device (UCLA), high-repetition-rate laser driving "a fast plasma flow into a pulsed dipole
  magnetic field embedded in a uniform magnetized background plasma"; 2D maps of the magnetic field from
  flux probes; comparison with 2D particle-in-cell simulations.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstract): an ion-scale magnetosphere forms in the laboratory; magnetopause and
  diamagnetic-current positions follow pressure balance.
- The flow comes from outside the dipole (a solar-wind analogue), not from the dipole's centre.
- For this topic (our reading): a laboratory and PIC reference for a laser plasma meeting a dipole with a
  background plasma, but not an explosion from inside the closed field and no electron-acceleration result
  in the abstract.
