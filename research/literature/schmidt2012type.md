## Claim
Part 1: "An entirely analytical and more general formalism is developed, which includes kappa electron
velocity distribution functions for the plasma electrons and the shock-reflected electron beam."
Part 2: "The investigation reveals strong emission upstream of the flanks of the shock. A complicated
rippled shock geometry develops with embedded 'ripples' that stimulate short-lived 'bright spot' radio
sources".

## Method
- Analytic theory of "the acceleration of electrons at the shock, formation of electron beams, growth of
  Langmuir waves, and conversion of Langmuir energy into radiation" (extends Knock et al. 2001, 2003; Knock &
  Cairns 2005; Cairns & Knock 2006; Schmidt & Gopalswamy 2008), applied to MHD simulations of a CME-driven
  shock to give synthetic radio images and dynamic spectra.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstracts. Do not take numbers from this series (see Assessment).

## Assessment
- Shown (per the abstracts): a model chain from shock-reflected electrons to type II emission, with the
  emission strongest at the flanks and tied to ripples.
- The synthetic dynamic spectra are the same kind of product that the retraction notice of the 2014 paper
  calls likely fabricated; parts 1 and 2 are not retracted, but we treat their numerical results as
  unverified (our judgement).
- For this topic (our reading): the electron source in this model is single shock reflection (shock drift)
  on open or locally curved field; it has no trap between two shock crossings of a closed field. Useful only
  as background for "shock-reflected electrons make type II", not as prior work on closed fields.
