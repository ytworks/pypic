# Schneiter, Velázquez, Reynoso, Esquivel & De Colle (2015): 3D MHD simulation of polarized emission in SN 1006

- Ref: MNRAS 449, 88-93 (2015) (volume and pages from the Crossref record of the DOI);
  DOI 10.1093/mnras/stv279; arXiv:1502.03133 (v1, "accepted by MNRAS"); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
Synthetic maps of the Stokes parameter Q from a 3-D MHD model of SN 1006, compared with observations, are "a
valuable tool to discern unambiguously which mechanism is taking place in the remnant of SN 1006, giving
strong support to the quasi-parallel model".

## Method
- 3-D MHD simulation of the remnant.
- Synthetic maps of the polarized intensity, the Stokes parameter Q and the "polar-referenced angle".
- The maps are computed for "two possible particle acceleration mechanisms: quasi-parallel and
  quasi-perpendicular". The form of the obliquity dependence is not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: it bears on where a spherical front in a nearly uniform field is bright. By the abstract,
  the polarization of SN 1006 favours emission from the quasi-parallel parts (theta_Bn near 0, the polar
  caps). `petruk2009aspect` reaches "some indication against" that model from the radio azimuthal profile.
- The dependence of the acceleration on obliquity is an input of the model, not a result. The work does not
  say by which mechanism electrons are accelerated at either obliquity.
