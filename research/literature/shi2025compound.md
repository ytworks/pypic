# Shi, Artemyev, Angelopoulos, Liu & Wilson (2025): Compound electron acceleration at planetary foreshocks

- Ref: Nature Communications 16, 77; DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-55464-8. Peer reviewed; open access.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, introduction, the observation and model-result passages (journal HTML);
  methods skimmed; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
The acceleration of solar wind electrons by four orders of magnitude at Earth's foreshock is a compound
process: shock drift acceleration (SDA) at the bow shock, resonant scattering by several wave modes, and
adiabatic reflection from foreshock transients. A data-constrained model with all of them reproduces the
observed power law up to ~200 keV; without transients it stops near 20 keV.

## Method
- MMS and THEMIS observations of foreshock transients with electrons up to ~200 keV; the bow shock in the
  MMS event is quasi-perpendicular (normal at 80 +- 5 deg to the field).
- Model: electrons bounce between strong-field regions and are scattered in pitch angle by electrostatic
  waves, high-frequency whistlers and magnetosonic (low-frequency whistler) waves, with wave properties
  taken from the observations.

## Numbers to compare against
- Observed PSD tail ~E^-4 between ~1 keV and 200 keV in the core of the transient.
- Model without transients: with magnetosonic-wave scattering about 40 reflections from the bow shock,
  energy gain to 10-20 keV, starting from < 100 eV.
- Model with transients: up to and above 200 keV.

## Assessment
- This is an existing confrontation of an Earth event with a transport model built on measured wave
  properties. It differs from this topic's plan in three ways: the shock is quasi-perpendicular, the waves
  and structures are prescribed from data, and no self-consistent kinetic simulation supplies the
  scattering.
- Its "without transients" result (~20 keV) is a second, model-based statement of the Earth null case,
  next to the instrument limit of wilson2016relativistic.
- The model has many parts whose separate contributions are fixed by the chosen wave parameters; the
  paper shows sufficiency, not necessity, of the combination.
