# Shimada & Hoshino (2000): Strong electron acceleration at high Mach number shock waves: simulation study of electron dynamics

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 543, L67–L71 (2000); DOI 10.1086/318161; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not read. Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
In a perpendicular shock at high Mach number, two-stream instabilities in the transition layer heat and
accelerate electrons: "The nonlinear interaction of these waves leads to the strong electron heating as
well as the nonthermal high-energy electron acceleration in the shock transition layer."

## Method
- Particle-in-cell simulation of a perpendicular magnetosonic shock. The dimension of the run and its
  parameters are not in the abstract.
- Sequence in the abstract: (1) a two-stream instability "between the reflected ions and the incident
  electrons" excites "large-amplitude, coherent electrostatic waves with the electron holes in phase
  space"; (2) the electrons slow down, and a second two-stream instability grows between the incident ions
  and the decelerated electrons.

## Numbers to compare against
- Time scale of the instabilities: of the order of the inverse electron plasma frequency (the expression
  is garbled in the record).
- The Mach number and the energies are not in the abstract; it says only that the Mach number "greatly
  exceeds the critical Mach number".

## Assessment
- For this topic: PIC evidence that two-stream instabilities between ions and electrons in the transition
  layer produce non-thermal electrons (the survey lists this work under the Buneman instability). It is
  the perpendicular case, that is, the equator of the sphere.
- Shock surfing is not named in the abstract. `hoshino2002nonthermal` is the note on the later paper by
  the same two authors.
