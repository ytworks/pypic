# Talebpour Sheshvan, Dresing, Vainio, Afanasiev & Morosan (2023): On the Role of Interplanetary Shocks in Accelerating MeV Electrons

- Ref: A&A 674, A133; DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202345908; arXiv:2301.05587. Peer reviewed. Read in the arXiv
  version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract, part of the introduction, the event table and section 4; topics:
  qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
MeV electron increases at interplanetary shocks near 1 au are rare. A filtering method for the statistical
significance of intensity increases finds nine clear cases in the STEREO data of 2007-2019, all at shocks
driven by fast interplanetary coronal mass ejections.

## Method
- STEREO High Energy Telescope electrons around the shocks of the STEREO in situ shock list; 27 candidates
  by eye; a filter that tests the increase against the background fluctuation.

## Numbers to compare against
- 587 shocks; 9 clear MeV electron increases (~1.5 %).
- All nine: transit speed of the driver above 900 km/s.
- theta_Bn of the nine between ~45 and ~88 deg: none is quasi-parallel.
- The highest significance is usually in the highest electron energy channel.
- In several cases more than one shock passed within a day.

## Assessment
- For this topic: the MeV cases at 1 au are a different population from the quasi-parallel cases (PSP,
  Saturn). They cannot be used as quasi-parallel "yes" events.
- The method detects a local increase at the shock. As the authors say, it does not identify electrons
  accelerated earlier, closer to the Sun (H7).
- Multiple shocks within a day point to pre-conditioned upstream media (H4, H5); the paper does not test this.
