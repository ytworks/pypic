# Tsubouchi & Lembège (2004): Full particle simulations of short large-amplitude magnetic structures (SLAMS) in quasi-parallel shocks

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. 109 (A2), article 2003JA010014; DOI 10.1029/2003JA010014. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract (no open full text found); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
SLAMS form in a 1-D full particle simulation by the steepening of long-wavelength magnetosonic waves that
diffuse ions excite. Their life has five phases; a whistler precursor emitted from the steepened leading
edge "behaves as a new shock front".

## Method
- 1-D full particle (PIC) simulation of a quasi-parallel shock; the SLAMS are compared with Cluster and
  with earlier AMPTE measurements.

## Numbers to compare against
- Spiky electrostatic field of width ~0.5 ion inertial length, lifetime shorter than one inverse ion
  gyrofrequency.
- Phases: growth of the ULF waves; symmetric, asymmetric, spiky and late SLAMS; local self-reformation in the
  late phase.

## Assessment
- The scales are "in good agreement with results issued from previous hybrid simulations and with
  experimental measurements made by AMPTE UKS/IRM satellites".
- For this topic: evidence that a 1-D full particle run does produce SLAMS with realistic scales, including
  the electron-scale features (whistler precursor, electrostatic spike) that a hybrid run lacks. That bears
  on what our quasi-1-D runs can state about candidate H6 (SLAMS). Electron acceleration is not named in the abstract.
