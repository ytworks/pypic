# Tsurutani & Lin (1985): Acceleration of >47 keV Ions and >2 keV electrons by interplanetary shocks at 1 AU

- Ref: JGR 90(A1), 1-11; DOI 10.1029/JA090iA01p00001. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (it is long and holds the main numbers); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Interplanetary shocks at 1 au change the fluxes of > 47 keV ions and > 2 keV electrons only above a
threshold in shock speed along the upstream field. Every quasi-parallel shock above it gives a proton
event; electron spikes belong to quasi-perpendicular shocks.

## Method
- ISEE 3 particles and fields at 37 forward shocks (of 55), August 1978 to November 1979.

## Numbers to compare against
- Threshold: shock speed along the upstream field of ~250 km/s.
- Every quasi-parallel shock (theta_Bn <~ 50 deg) above the threshold: a proton energetic storm particle
  event.
- Electron spikes at most shocks with theta_Bn >~ 70 deg.
- Most common electron effect: a step-like increase after the shock by a factor of ~2, with no clear
  dependence on theta_Bn, at every shock faster than ~175 km/s.
- Electron effects limited to 2-10 keV, except three events reaching ~50 keV.
- Before every shock there were >= 2 keV electrons and >~ 47 keV ions in the ambient medium: possible seeds.

## Assessment
- The oldest statement of two things this topic uses: electron effects at 1 au stop near 10 keV, and a
  seed population is always present (H4).
- The speed threshold is expressed along the field, i.e. it favours quasi-parallel geometry for a given
  shock speed; the electron spikes nevertheless occur at quasi-perpendicular shocks.
- Instrument range of the time; "limited to 2-10 keV" is relative to that background.
