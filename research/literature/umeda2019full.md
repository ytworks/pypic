# Umeda et al. (2019): Full particle-in-cell simulation of the interaction between two plasmas for laboratory experiments on the generation of magnetized collisionless shocks with high-power lasers

- Ref: Phys. Plasmas 26(3), 032303 (2019); DOI 10.1063/1.5079906; arXiv:1902.03345 (read); peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, set-up, summary); topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
A 1-D full-PIC numerical experiment for laser experiments with a weak ambient field. A perpendicular
collisionless shock forms only when the piston plasma is magnetised. It forms inside the piston plasma,
while the ambient ions gyrate there.

## Method
- 1-D full PIC. An aluminium piston plasma with a drift speed "is continuously injected" from the left
  boundary into a nitrogen plasma at rest. Open boundaries.
- Four runs: each of the two plasmas magnetised or not.

## Numbers to compare against
- Summary: "a perpendicular collisionless shock is formed only when the piston plasma is magnetized".
- Summary: "The shock downstream is formed during the gyration of ambient ions in the piston plasma
  region on the timescale of quarter gyro period of ambient ions."
- A tangential discontinuity forms at the interface on the electron gyro-period when one of the two
  plasmas is magnetised.
- The authors contrast this with Schaeffer et al. 2017, "in which a strong ambient magnetic field was
  imposed and shock waves were formed around the interface between the piston plasma and the background
  plasma".

## Assessment
- For this topic: a different regime from ours. The ambient field is weak and the shock is made of
  ambient ions that gyrate in the field of the piston. It is not a shock that runs ahead of the piston
  into the ambient plasma.
- It still shows the role of a continuous piston: the injection lasts for the whole run, so the question
  of a finite debris mass does not arise.
- 1-D; no curvature; electron acceleration is not treated in the parts read. The drift speed, the fields
  and the mass ratios are in the set-up table, which was not copied here.
