# Vandas (1995a): Acceleration of electrons by a nearly perpendicular curved shock wave: 1. Zero shock thickness

## Claim
For a curved shock of zero thickness, acceleration by a spherical shock reduces to that by a cylindrical
shock; this does not hold for a general shape such as the bow shock. The tangent-plane approximation with
a variable normal field component is verified.

## Method
- Analytic / numerical electron orbits at a zero-thickness curved shock, in 2-D (cylinder) and 3-D
  (sphere); a method for a front of general shape.
