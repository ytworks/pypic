# Vandas (1995b): Acceleration of electrons by a nearly perpendicular curved shock wave: 2. Nonzero shock thickness

## Claim
A method for a curved shock of general shape with finite thickness, applied to the bow shock and to an
interplanetary shock: the theory gives the observed level of 2–3 keV fluxes at the interplanetary shock,
but bow-shock electrons above 10 keV have a harder spectrum than the theory.

## Method
- Analytic adiabatic theory of shock drift acceleration at a curved shock of general shape (extends the
  cylinder / sphere solution of Vandas 1994).

## Numbers to compare against
- 2–3 keV fluxes comparable with observation at an interplanetary shock; above 10 keV at the bow shock the
  observed spectrum is harder.

## Assessment
- Open, from the abstract: "some additional process (like pitch angle scattering) keeps the electrons
  longer in the shock layer and enables them to gain more energy".
