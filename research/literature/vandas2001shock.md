# Vandas (2001): Shock drift acceleration of electrons: A parametric study

## Claim
Spectra and angular distributions of electrons from shock drift acceleration at curved shocks are highly
anisotropic and are not power laws; comparison with observations shows that the process must be strongly
modified by scattering in the shock layer or by spatial / temporal changes of the front.

## Method
- Adiabatic theory of SDA at curved shocks (Vandas 1994, 1995a,b), scanned over shock parameters.

## Numbers to compare against
- "The highest fluxes can be found in the regions of the shock with theta_Bn about 86–88 deg."
- Reflected fluxes increase with decreasing curvature or decreasing thickness; transmitted fluxes hardly
  depend on them; the field profile shape matters little; higher upstream speed gives larger fluxes.

## Assessment
- For this topic: "reflected electrons come from just past the tangent point (86–88 deg), not from 90
  deg" is published here for a standing curved shock.
