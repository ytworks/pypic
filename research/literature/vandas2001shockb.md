# Vandas (2001): Shock drift electron acceleration and pitch angle scattering

- Ref: IAU Symp. 203 (Recent Insights into the Physics of the Sun and Heliosphere), 577–579 (2001);
  DOI 10.1017/S0074180900220044; conference proceedings (3 pages), light review.
- Read: 2026-11-13, full; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- Citekey note: `vandas2001shock` is the J. Geophys. Res. paper of the same year.

## Claim
Adding a smooth pitch-angle scattering to adiabatic shock drift acceleration at a curved shock gives harder
spectra to higher energy, raises the downstream fluxes and suppresses the upstream ones.
- Question: can scattering close the gap between SDA theory and the observed spectra (power laws with
  index about 3–4, fluxes above 10 keV, low anisotropy)?
- Idea: add scattering after Kocharov et al. 1999 to the adiabatic model of the earlier papers.

## Method
- Guiding-centre electrons in a prescribed curved shock (as Vandas 1995a,b, 2001); scattering frequency
  1 / (9 tau_g). Example at theta_Bn about 88 deg, alpha about 110 deg.

## Numbers to compare against
- Figs. 1, 2 only; no table. Without scattering the observations are not reproduced.

## Assessment
- Shown: the direction of the effect of scattering, in one example.
- Open: the source and rate of the scattering; a parametric study.
- For this topic: supports "single-encounter SDA at a curved shock is not enough"; it carries no result on
  the theta_Bn history of an electron.
