## Claim
When the shock of a fast CME runs into a slower CME (flux rope), the enhanced and helical field "may
favor acceleration of electrons at the shock by the fast-Fermi process since multiple encounters are
possible and nearly perpendicular parts of the shock are more abundant".

## Method
- Electron acceleration evaluated numerically on the fields of 2.5-D MHD simulations of two interacting
  flux ropes.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a source for multiple encounters made possible by curved (helical) field lines crossing
  a shock; again the field, not the convex front, closes the path.
