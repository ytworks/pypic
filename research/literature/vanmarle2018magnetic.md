## Claim
"In contrast with previous particle-in-cell-hybrid simulations, we find that particle acceleration and
magnetic field amplification also occur when the magnetic field is oblique to the normal to the shock but
on larger time-scales than in the parallel case."

## Method
- A grid-based magnetohydrodynamic thermal fluid combined with particle-in-cell suprathermal particles,
  with adaptive mesh refinement. Shocks "with various Alfvénic Mach numbers and magnetic field obliquity".
- How the suprathermal particles are injected is not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: a claim that oblique shocks do accelerate, through a chain given in the abstract: a
  pre-heating "similar to a shock drift acceleration leading to the corrugation of the shock front"; the
  corrugation helps particles enter the upstream region and start a non-resonant streaming instability.
- The thermal plasma is a fluid, so injection from the thermal pool and electron acceleration are not in
  the abstract. Background for the mechanism question.
