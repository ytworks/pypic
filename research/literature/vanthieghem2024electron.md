# Vanthieghem, Tsiolis, Spitkovsky, Todo, Sekiguchi & Fiuza (2024): Electron Heating in High Mach Number Collisionless Shocks

- Ref: Phys. Rev. Lett. 132, 265201; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.265201. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract; topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
A model of electron heating in high-Mach-number shocks mediated by the Weibel instability (an ambipolar-type
electric field together with diffusive transport of the electrons) agrees with fully kinetic simulations.

## Method
- Theory compared with fully kinetic (PIC) simulations.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- Heating, not acceleration; unmagnetized or weakly magnetized, Weibel-mediated shocks.
- For this topic: the downstream electron temperature fixes the thermal momentum against which any
  "injected" electron is counted (the reviewer's point on the injection-rate definition, p ≥ 5 p_th,e
  downstream). Saturn's case (M_A ~100) lies at high Mach number, where `bohdan2021magnetic` (S) reports
  Weibel-generated fields at Saturn's quasi-perpendicular crossings; runs S1-S3 can be compared with this
  heating model. Whether the model's range covers a magnetized
  quasi-parallel shock is not in the abstract.
