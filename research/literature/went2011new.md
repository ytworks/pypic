# Went, Hospodarsky, Masters, Hansen & Dougherty (2011): A new semiempirical model of Saturn's bow shock based on propagated solar wind parameters

- Ref: JGR 116, A7; DOI 10.1029/2010JA016349. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
A surface fitted to all available crossings gives the shape and the pressure dependence of Saturn's bow
shock.

## Method
- 574 crossings (Pioneer 11, Voyager 1 and 2, Cassini 2004-2010), covering latitudes up to ~45 deg.
- Dynamic pressure from Langmuir-wave densities and propagated solar wind velocities.
- An axisymmetric second-order surface.

## Numbers to compare against
- None used here.

## Assessment
- A mean surface: local normals at a rippled or moving shock can differ from the model normal.
- For this topic: the normal behind theta_Bn ~20 deg of the 2007-02-03 crossing (masters2013electron) comes
  from this model. The theta_Bn of the Saturn case is therefore a model value combined with the measured
  field.
