# West, Safi-Harb, Jaffe, Kothes, Landecker & Foster (2016): The Connection between Supernova Remnants and the Galactic Magnetic Field: A Global Radio Study of the Axisymmetric Sample

- Ref: A&A 587, A148 (2016); DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/201527001; arXiv:1510.08536 (v1, "accepted to A&A");
  peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (arXiv abstract). Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
A model of a remnant expanding into the ambient Galactic magnetic field (GMF) reproduces the observed radio
morphologies of many axisymmetric remnants when the GMF model of Jansson & Farrar (2012a) is used. "These
results strongly support the presence of an off-plane, vertical component to the GMF, and the importance of
the Galactic field on SNR morphology."

## Method
- Radio images of all known Galactic SNRs; a sample with "axisymmetric" morphology: "bilateral" or "barrel"
  shaped remnants and one-sided shells. The cleanest examples are modelled.
- Each is modelled at its Galactic position with two GMF models: Jansson & Farrar (2012a), with a vertical
  halo component, and Sun et al. (2008), parallel to the plane. Distances from 0.5 to 10 kpc.
- "a physically motivated model of a SNR expanding into the ambient GMF". Which dependence of the electron
  acceleration on obliquity it assumes is not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract beyond the distance range 0.5-10 kpc.

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It supports the premise that the ambient field direction sets the radio
  morphology of a remnant. It does not address the electron acceleration mechanism.
- The companion paper `west2017connection` compares quasi-parallel and quasi-perpendicular acceleration for
  the same sample.
