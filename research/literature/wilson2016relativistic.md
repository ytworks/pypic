# Wilson, Sibeck, Turner, Osmane, Caprioli & Angelopoulos (2016): Relativistic Electrons Produced by Foreshock Disturbances Observed Upstream of Earth's Bow Shock

- Ref: PRL 117, 215101; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.215101; arXiv:1607.02183. Peer reviewed. Read in the
  arXiv version.
- Read: 2026-11-14, full main text (arXiv HTML; extended-data figures not seen); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
Foreshock disturbances upstream of Earth's bow shock (hot flow anomalies, foreshock bubbles, short
large-amplitude magnetic structures) energize electrons from tens of eV to at least ~300 keV, locally. The
bow shock itself, a magnetospheric source and a solar source are argued not to be the origin.

## Method
- THEMIS electrostatic analyzers and solid state telescopes near foreshock disturbances in the ion
  foreshock; pitch-angle distributions inside and outside the enhancements.
- Solar origin excluded with Wind and STEREO radio and particle data; no interplanetary shocks nearby.

## Numbers to compare against
- 30 disturbances examined; 10 show energetic electron enhancements.
- During enhancements: nearly isotropic from ~0.05 to 300 keV (anisotropy rarely above a factor of 2); a
  single power law f(E) ~ E^-4 from ~0.25 keV to the highest energy. In two of the three examples the data
  above ~140 keV are noise.
- Outside the enhancements: "only noise > 12 keV".
- Nearly all disturbances show enhanced ion fluxes, often above 300 keV.
- Introduction: electrons beyond a few tens of keV were not expected at Earth's bow shock (M < 20).

## Assessment
- This is the primary source for "outside foreshock transients the quasi-parallel foreshock of Earth shows
  no electrons above ~12 keV" in the THEMIS data: an upper limit set by the instrument noise, not a zero.
- Exclusion of the bow shock as source is an argument from isotropy, the single power law and the
  location, not a measurement of where the energy is gained.
- 20 of 30 disturbances show no enhancement; the paper does not identify what separates the two groups
  (raptis2025revealing proposes a 1-5 keV seed in fast wind).
- Shock parameters of the bow shock at the time of each event (M_A, theta_Bn) are not the subject.
