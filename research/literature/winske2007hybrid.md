# Winske & Gary (2007): Hybrid simulations of debris-ambient ion interactions in astrophysical explosions

- Ref: J. Geophys. Res. 112, A10 (2007); DOI 10.1029/2007JA012276; peer-reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: spherical-shock-injection
- ABSTRACT ONLY (OpenAlex record). The full text was not obtained: no open copy was found in this search.
  Nothing below goes beyond the abstract.

## Claim
A dense debris plasma that expands faster than the Alfven speed into a magnetised ambient plasma makes a
diamagnetic cavity and stops after it has overrun about its own mass of ambient ions. Where the stopped
debris ends up depends on the debris gyroradius rho_d.

## Method
- Hybrid simulations (particle ions, massless fluid electrons) "run in a two-dimensional geometry".
- Studied: the stopping of the debris ions, the distribution of the stopped ions, and their penetration
  across the compressed ambient field.

## Numbers to compare against
- "The stopping distance of the debris is consistent with that determined from the debris ions
  overrunning an equivalent mass of ambient ions."
- The distribution of the stopped debris is "strongly dependent on the debris gyroradius based on the
  initial directed velocity rho_d, being highly peaked at the debris-magnetic field interface when rho_d
  is large".
- The distance that debris ions penetrate into the excluded field is "shown to increase linearly with
  rho_d".
- The abstract gives no shock criterion and no Mach number of a shock.

## Assessment
- For this topic: the source of the equal-mass radius R_m as the stopping scale of the debris, and of
  rho_d as the scale of the debris-ambient mixing layer. Both are the units of our design.
- By the abstract, a larger R_m/rho_d means a thinner mixing layer relative to the cavity. This supports
  a heavy piston, but the abstract does not say what shock results.
- Hybrid and 2-D: nothing on electrons. The parameters of the runs are in the full text, which was not
  read.
