# Wu (1984): A fast Fermi process: Energetic electrons accelerated by a nearly perpendicular bow shock

## Claim
The thin sheet of Anderson et al. 1979 is explained by a fast Fermi process: in the solar wind frame the
nearly perpendicular shock at the point of tangency is a fast-moving magnetic mirror that reflects
electrons of large pitch angle.

## Method
- Analytic theory (adiabatic reflection).

## Numbers to compare against
- Seed electrons of several hundred eV reach several keV.

## Assessment
- For this topic: with Leroy & Mangeney 1984, the origin of the mirror speed V_sh / cos theta_Bn for the
  reflected energy. Whether the paper discusses the motion of the tangent point in time is not in the
