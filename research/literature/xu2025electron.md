# Xu, Li & Yao (2025): Electron acceleration at shock ripples: role of pitch-angle diffusion

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 988(1), 67 (2025) (Crossref); DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ade23e; gold open access (CC BY) but
  the IOP copy is behind a bot check; no arXiv version found; peer-reviewed
- Read: 2026-11-13, abstract; topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
In a 2-D focused-transport model of a quasi-perpendicular shock with large-scale ripples, electrons reach
power-law spectra with a high-energy cutoff, softer than the DSA prediction; with a suitable pitch-angle diffusion
strength a small fraction is trapped magnetically in several ripples along the front and reaches ~MeV.

## Method
- Gyrophase-averaged electrons, focused transport equation; turbulence enters only as quasi-linear pitch-angle
  diffusion. Monte Carlo solution of the equivalent forward Ito stochastic differential equation in a periodic box.
- Geometry: the ripples are put into the magnetic field line; the shock itself is planar and piecewise (as the
  abstract states, "for tractability"). Parameters (ripple scale and amplitude, theta_Bn, shock speed,
  compression, diffusion coefficients, injection energy) not read.

## Numbers to compare against
- None beyond "~MeV" for the trapped fraction (abstract).

## Assessment
- Mechanism: magnetic trapping between ripples (mirror geometry), not field-line wandering across the front.
  Its control parameter is pitch-angle scattering strength, which is imposed, not produced by the plasma.
- Not kinetic: no shock structure, no self-consistent waves, no electron-scale physics; gyro-averaged, so
  shock-drift energization enters only through the guiding-centre equations.
- For our topic it gives a second, separate route by which large-scale front/field structure accelerates
  electrons (ripple trapping, cf. Guo & Giacalone 2010 Fig. 3) that a meandering test must tell apart from
  multiple connection.
