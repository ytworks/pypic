# Yang, Wang, Li, Wimmer-Schweingruber, He, Tu, Tian & Bale (2019): Electron Acceleration by ICME-driven Shocks at 1 au

- Ref: ApJ 875, 104; DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ab1133. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract only (full text not obtained); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
A survey of suprathermal electrons at 74 shocks driven by interplanetary coronal mass ejections: both
quasi-parallel and quasi-perpendicular shocks accelerate electrons in situ at 1 au, "mainly via shock drift
acceleration".

## Method
- Wind 3DP electrons, 1995-2014; downstream flux J_D against ambient flux J_A; spectra; pitch angles.

## Numbers to compare against
- 59 quasi-perpendicular and 15 quasi-parallel shocks.
- Share with J_D / J_A > 1.5, quasi-perpendicular (quasi-parallel): ~86 % (~60 %) at 0.43 keV, ~62 %
  (~36 %) at 1.95 keV, ~17 % (~7 %) at 40 keV.
- J_D correlates best with the magnetosonic Mach number, J_D / J_A with the magnetic compression.
- Downstream spectra ~0.4-100 keV: double power law, index 2-6 below a break near 2 keV, 2.0-3.2 above.
- J_D / J_A peaks perpendicular to the field at ~0.4-50 keV.

## Assessment
- Abstract only. ~7 % of 15 is one event: the quasi-parallel statistics at 40 keV rest on a single shock.
- Energies end at ~100 keV; nothing on relativistic electrons.
- For this topic: the 1-au quasi-parallel "mostly no" case, and evidence for drift acceleration rather
  than DSA at weak shocks (H6). Mach numbers of the 15 quasi-parallel shocks are not in the abstract.
- Related: Yang et al. 2018 (ApJ 853, 89), Qin, Kong & Wu 2023 (ApJ 942, 63); not read beyond what
  the plan records.
