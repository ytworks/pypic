# Yu, Xia & Fang (2024): Nonthermal Acceleration of Electrons, Positrons, and Protons at a Nonrelativistic Quasi-parallel Collisionless Shock

## Claim
In a 1-D PIC simulation of a quasi-parallel shock in an electron-positron-proton plasma, all three species
form power-law tails. Reflected positrons gain further energy from the parallel electric field.

## Method
- 1D3V PIC (Smilei); positron-to-proton density ratio 0.1.
- Flow 0.2c, shock speed 0.27c, theta_Bn 30 deg, mass ratio 30, M_s 17.1, M_A 16.8.

## Numbers to compare against
- None used by this topic.

## Assessment
- One condition, mass ratio 30 and v_sh 0.27c: far from the heliospheric events in mass ratio and speed.
- No diffusion coefficient and no injection rate are measured.
- For this topic: a further quasi-parallel PIC run at M_A ~17 with an electron tail, in line with
  gupta2024electron (tails for M_A >~ 10). Not a prior work for the measurement proposed here.
