# Zank, Hunana, Mostafavi, le Roux, Li, Webb, Khabarova, Cummings, Stone & Decker (2015): Diffusive shock acceleration and reconnection acceleration processes

- Ref: Astrophys. J. 814, 137 (23 pp) (2015) (Crossref); DOI 10.1088/0004-637X/814/2/137; no arXiv version found;
  peer-reviewed. Full text from the Caltech authors repository (record chzt2-ykn38).
- Read: 2026-11-13, skimmed (abstract, introduction, conclusions); topics: shock-alfvenic-turbulence

## Claim
Diffusive shock acceleration combined with acceleration by downstream magnetic islands (merging-induced
"anti-reconnection" electric field and island contraction) gives harder power laws in particle speed than DSA,
and, for contraction, intensities that peak downstream at a distance that grows with energy. Voyager 2 data behind
the heliospheric termination shock are offered as suggestive support.

## Method
- Analytic steady 1-D transport theory (Zank et al. 2014; le Roux et al. 2015) for particles diffusing in a region
  filled with contracting and merging plasmoids, solved with shock boundary conditions for three cases (merging
  only, contraction only, both). Averaging is long enough to neglect trapping in single islands; trapping matters
  only below a threshold speed.

## Numbers to compare against
- Spectral index depends on r and on U^2/V_E^2 (merging) or tau_diff/tau_c (contraction); the general solution
  converges to the merging-only spectrum as tau_diff/tau_c -> 0 (Sect. 3).
- Voyager 2 HTS crossing 2007 Aug 30, protons 1.8-22 MeV: downstream amplification above 1, ordered by energy
  (Fig. 13); called "suggestive only".

## Assessment
- The flux ropes here are downstream, generated by the shock, and enter only through transport coefficients. The
  upstream turbulence is not modelled, there is no kinetic shock and no electron injection from thermal energies.
- Relevance to our topic is marginal: a downstream energization channel that a kinetic run with flux-rope-like
  upstream structure could also show; it does not address field-line meandering at the front.
