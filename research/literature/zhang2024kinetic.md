# Zhang, Heuer, Davies, Schaeffer, Wen, García-Rubio & Ren (2024): Kinetic study of shock formation and particle acceleration in laser-driven quasi-parallel magnetized collisionless shocks

- Ref: Phys. Plasmas 31 (8), 082303; DOI 10.1063/5.0210050. Peer reviewed.
- Read: 2026-11-14, abstract (no open full text found); topics: qpar-electron-conditions

## Claim
2-D PIC simulations show that the early stage of quasi-parallel shock formation can be reached in
experiments planned for the National Ignition Facility, and that particles accelerated by a first-order
Fermi process should be observable there.

## Method
- 2-D PIC of a laser-driven piston plasma in a magnetized background, quasi-parallel geometry.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.

## Assessment
- "Compared to quasi-perpendicular shocks, quasi-parallel shocks are more difficult to form in the laboratory
  and to simulate because of their large spatial scales and long formation times".
- The abstract names repeated shock crossings of ions, the excitation of upstream waves by energetic ions,
  and a brief discussion of the energy partition.
- For this topic: the laboratory boundary area for our geometry. A quasi-parallel shock experiment is
  planned, not done, in what we read; the laboratory can at present test only the earliest stage, far from
  the ages of our cases (ω_ci t 35-10⁵). Whether electrons are treated is not in the abstract.
