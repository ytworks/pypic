# Zhou, Prokhorov, Ferrazzoli et al. (2023): Magnetic structures and turbulence in SN 1006 revealed with imaging X-ray polarimetry

- Ref: accepted for publication in ApJ according to the arXiv comment; volume and page are not in the metadata
  (journal reference not checked); no DOI in the metadata; arXiv:2309.01879v1.
- Read: 2026-11-12, introduction, results (Sects. 3.1-3.3), discussion and summary; topics: spherical-shock-injection

## Claim
X-ray polarisation of the north-eastern limb of SN 1006 shows that the field just behind the shock is close to the
shock normal (radial), as in the radio, and only partly ordered. The authors read this as support for efficient
quasi-parallel acceleration with turbulence driven by the cosmic rays themselves.

## Method
- Observation with IXPE (2-8 keV imaging polarimeter, resolution about 30 arcsec): 628-641 ks in August 2022 and 339
  ks in March 2023, north-eastern shell only (Sect. 2.1, Fig. 1).
- Analysis in 2-4 keV because the background dominates above about 4 keV; background is about 30 % of the shell
  photons (Sect. 2.4, Appendix).
- Polarisation degree (PD) and angle (PA) for the whole shell and regions A-D, by binned Stokes parameters and by
  joint fits of I, Q, U spectra (Sects. 3.2, 3.3). Events are also re-aligned to a circular geometry to test a radial
  field (Sect. 3.2).
- Comparison with 1.4 GHz ATCA polarisation (Reynoso et al. 2013), rotation measure 12 rad/m^2. Not a simulation.

## Numbers to compare against
- Shell: PD = 22.4 +/- 3.5 %, PA = -45.4 +/- 4.5 deg (north through east) (Table 1, Sect. 3.2).
- Re-aligned events: PD = 21.9 +/- 3.5 % at 6.2 sigma, PA = 0.9 +/- 4.6 deg, where 0 deg is radial and 90 deg
  tangential (Sect. 3.2).
- Magnetic position angle 44.6 +/- 4.5 deg; the shell region spans position angles 24-58 deg from the remnant centre
  (Sect. 4.1).
- Regions A-C: PD 21-23 %, PA -50 to -40 deg; outer and inner filaments have the same PD (Table 1). PD is 20-26 %
  whatever the X-ray brightness (Appendix, Table 3).
- Radio, same shell: PD = 14.5 +/- 0.2 %, PA = -36.3 +/- 0.4 deg (Table 1); the X-ray PD is higher at about 2 sigma
  (Sect. 4.4).
- Maximum possible PD = Gamma/(Gamma + 2/3) = 80 % for photon index 2.74 (Sect. 4.2).
- Random-to-ordered field ratio sigma_B/B = 1.2 (+0.2, -0.1) for SN 1006, about 1.8 for Tycho and 3.3 for Cas A, with
  the formula of Bandiera & Petruk (2016) (Sect. 4.3).
- Rim PD: Cas A 4.5 +/- 1.0 %, Tycho 12 +/- 2 %, SN 1006 NE 22.4 +/- 3.5 % (Table 2).
- Bell scale: about 2e17 cm for V_s = 5000 km/s, n_0 = 0.05 cm^-3, E_max = 100 TeV, B_0 = 3 microgauss (Eq. 1); rim
  widths 25 arcsec (8e17 cm) outer and 15 arcsec (5e17 cm) inner; the IXPE resolution is about 1e18 cm, so this
  turbulence is unresolved (Sect. 4.2).
- SN 1006: distance 2.18 +/- 0.08 kpc, diameter about 0.5 deg, about 560 pc above the plane (Sect. 1); V_s about 5000
  km/s, n_0 about 0.05-0.085 cm^-3 (Table 2, Sect. 4.2); shell proper motion about 0.48 arcsec/yr (Sect. 2.3); field
  amplification ratio 10-100 from filament profiles (Sect. 4.3).

## Assessment
- Shown by observation: the polarisation direction in the NE limb (field radial or parallel to the shock normal) and a
  PD far below the maximum, hence a turbulent field.
- Stated, not shown: that the ambient field runs north-east to south-west and survives the shock (taken from Reynoso
  et al. 2013); that the radial field reflects quasi-parallel acceleration; that the turbulence comes from cosmic-ray
  instabilities and not from density fluctuations (Sect. 4.2).
- Stated limits: only the NE limb was observed; a radial and a uniform field direction cannot be told apart there
  (Sect. 3.2); the origin of radial fields in young remnants is "not fully understood" (Sect. 4.1); it is unclear
  whether the Bell instability gives radial fields, and post-shock turbulence is not known (Sect. 4.2); the radio
  angle depends on an uncertain rotation measure (Sect. 4.4).
- The measurement constrains the downstream field where TeV electrons radiate, not electron injection itself.
- Discussed alternatives: a radial field from instabilities at density clumps (Inoue et al. 2013), judged unlikely in
  the smooth medium of SN 1006; a selection effect in a random field (West et al. 2017, ApJL 849, L22), judged unable
  to give the limb-brightened shape (Sect. 4.1).
- Small inconsistencies: detection significance "7 sigma" in Sect. 3.2 but 6.3 in Table 1; PA -45.4 deg in the
  abstract and Table 1 but -45.5 deg in the summary.
