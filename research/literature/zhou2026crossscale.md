## Claim
A global 2-D semi-implicit PIC simulation (FLEKS) of the dayside magnetosphere with kinetic ions and
electrons forms foreshock, bow shock and magnetosheath self-consistently. By particle tracking,
"backstreaming electrons are predominantly leakage particles from the thermalized magnetosheath".

## Method
- 2-D semi-implicit full PIC; solar wind against the terrestrial dipole. The mass ratio is given in the
  abstract but is lost in the record ("mass ratio )"); M_A and resolution are not in the abstract.

## Assessment
- For this topic: the only newer full-PIC run of a globally curved shock with a statement on the origin of
  backstreaming electrons that this search found. It points to leakage, the minority class of Savoini et
  al. 2010. A semi-implicit code that steps over electron scales may not resolve mirror reflection in the
  ramp; this cannot be judged from the abstract.
