## Claim
The decays of radioactive nuclei related to 44Ti and 56Ni, ejected in the explosion, "can provide a vast pool
of mildly relativistic positrons and electrons which are further accelerated to ultrarelativistic energies by
reverse and forward shocks". This "can be a clue for solution of the well known theoretical problem of
electron injection in supernova remnants".

## Method
- An argument, with an application to Cas A. The form of the calculation is not in the abstract.

## Numbers to compare against
- None in the abstract.
- For Cas A: radioactivity "can supply adequate number of energetic electrons and positrons for
  interpretation of observational data provided that they are stochastically pre-accelerated in the upstream
  regions of the forward and reverse shocks".

## Assessment
- For this topic: background only. It is an alternative seed population (decay positrons and electrons of the
  ejecta), not a mechanism at the front. Like `morlino2009cosmicray`, it is a competitor to kinetic
  injection of thermal electrons.
- By the abstract the route still needs a stochastic pre-acceleration upstream of the shocks. So it does not
  remove the pre-acceleration problem that the kinetic works address.
