## Fixed before the first run (docstring of `n12_n6_synthetic.py`)

- N12: every number (η_nt,e, φ_e, η_e above 3 √(m_e/m_i) m_i v_pt, q) of every block, region and variant equals
  the number of the electrons that the exclusion keeps, known from the construction, within 1e-9; x_0 and
  "loaded" from the id are exact; the dump reproduces the histogram above p_d,e within 1e-9; the verdict in D_fix is
  "no wall effect" without a wall population and "wall effect detected" with one.
- N6 (a): N and N⁺ equal the exact time integrals within 1 %; E_nt from the histogram equals the summed energy
  within 1 %; N − U_far ≤ E_esc ≤ N ≤ N⁺ with a slack of 1 % of N; the verdict on four constructed cases.

## Design

- V1's geometry, id layout, thresholds and cadences; 240 logarithmic momentum bins for both species.
- N12: 1.5 × 10⁶ thermal electrons (p_th = 0.03 m_i v_pt), 10⁵ with dN/dp ∝ p⁻² from 0.12 to 20, half of them
  loaded beyond 800 d_i and half injected. Case `wall` adds 3 × 10⁴ electrons with dN/dp ∝ p^−1.5 from 0.05,
  loaded at x_0 ≤ 200, 200–400 and 400–800 d_i (40, 30, 30 %); 70 % of them and 1 % of the p⁻² electrons are in a
  wall dump at a step drawn from the whole run (half of them before t = 102). 20 spectra outputs in t = 102–147.6,
  positions uniform in 0–900 d_i and drawn anew for every output.
- N6 (a), per species: 3 × 10⁵ particles with dN/dp ∝ p⁻², emitted at a constant mean rate 10 d_i before the slab
  S over t = 0–150, constant v_x between 0.2 and 1 of their speed; 30 % turn back at a place uniform between the
  inner edge of S and the face; 201 dumps every 25,000 steps (dump interval over L_S = 0.577).

## Readings and limits

- The sum over dumps gives the dump at the output its full weight. For a steady flux it lies above the integral
  to t by about half a dump interval over t (0.25–0.4 % here). Against the integral to half an interval later
  the difference is positive with seed 11 and about zero (electrons) or negative (ions) with seed 12, so that
  remainder is consistent with sampling noise (two seeds only).
- N12 here tests the bookkeeping: id → origin, wall tags by step, the five variants, the verdict rule. The numbers
  are exact because particle number and p_th come from the full histogram and every lowest momentum is above
  p_d,e. The rule for p_0,e < p_d,e and the recomputation of p_0,e when p_th,e changes by more than 2 % are not
  exercised.
- σ_pt here is counting noise of uniform positions. The size of a wall effect that V1 can detect depends on V1's
  own σ_pt.
- The dump and the histogram are built from the same particles, so their agreement tests the binning of
  `n12_n6.py`, not the engine's dump selection; that is checked on the dump pilot.
- N6 (a) here has ballistic particles and a steady emission. It does not test an intermittent flux, the motion in
  the fields of the absorbing layer, or particles that turn back more than once. U_far is taken from the dump at
  the step of the output.
- R with p_0/2 is not compared: p_0/2 is below p_d of both species here, as it is expected to be in V1; the dumps
  then miss the particles between p_0/2 and p_d, and `n12_n6.py` marks that row `above_p_d: false`.
