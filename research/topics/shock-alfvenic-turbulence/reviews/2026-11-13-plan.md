## Summary
The draft question asks two things about a nonrelativistic supercritical shock moving into pre-existing Alfvénic
fluctuations (δB/B0 = 0.3–1, L_c ≥ 10 d_i), with electrons that start in the thermal pool:
- how do the injection fraction, the spectrum and E_max depend on δB/B0, θ_Bn and L_c;
- is the gain due to field-line meandering across the front, or to SSDA, corrugation or the turbulence itself?

The claimed novelty is the first fully kinetic electron–ion run of such a shock in pre-existing magnetic or Alfvénic
turbulence. In those runs, a δB_x-vs-δB_z polarization control, test electrons, 3-D and a per-particle work split
would isolate the meandering channel.

The question and the novelty can be restated, but only as two questions. The physical core of the plan, the
polarization control, does not isolate meandering in its present form.

## Major issues

### 1. The δB_x/δB_z control does not switch off meandering "with everything else equal"
Why it matters: the certainty of the mechanism attribution, which is the main novelty. The geometry is
B0 = B0 ŷ in the plane, θ_Bn = 90°, k ∥ ŷ. Switching δB_x → δB_z changes at least five things besides field-line
crossings.

- **Local θ_Bn.** δB_x is a normal component.
  - The local shock becomes oblique: θ_loc = 90° − arctan(δB_x/B0), down to about 45° at δB/B0 = 1.
  - With δB_z, B_n = 0 everywhere and the shock stays exactly perpendicular.
  - Everything known to inject electrons at θ < 90° switches on with δB_x, even if each field line crosses the front
    only once:
    - adiabatic SDA reflection with gain ∝ U/cos θ (`katou2019theory`);
    - HTF-subluminal escape upstream;
    - the whistler channel of `riquelme2011electron`;
    - whistler self-confinement (`amano2010critical`);
    - the M_A/cos θ dependence of SSDA (`amano2022theory`, Eq. 14).
  - `trotta2019electron`: "what counts is the local θ_Bn, not the mean one". `guo2015acceleration` explains its
    result by the local angle.
  - So δB_x > δB_z is expected without any multiple connection.
- **2-D representation of waves.**
  - With δB_z, the local field leaves the plane, by 45° at δB/B0 = 1.
  - Out-of-plane B suppresses ion-scale ripples. In `burgess2006simulations`, out-of-plane B gives a gain of only
    2–3×, while in-plane B with ripples gives about 10× E_max.
  - It also changes the k ∥ B content needed by MTSI and whistlers.
  - It changes Buneman/surfing efficiency: `bohdan2017electron` finds about 10× difference in the non-thermal fraction
    between in-plane and out-of-plane B at M_A ≈ 32.
  - So δB_z systematically removes non-meandering channels: a 2-D artefact with a sign.
- **Front forcing and corrugation.**
  - The Alfvénic δv_x that goes with δB_x modulates the normal inflow, and so the local Mach number. It pushes the
    front directly.
  - δv_z does not, so corrugation and ripple driving differ (`jebaraj2025turbulence`, abstract only).
- **Downstream turbulence.**
  - B_n is continuous across the shock, while the tangential components are compressed by about r.
  - So the downstream δB/B differs between the two cases, and with it downstream stochastic or shear acceleration
    (`demidem2023relativistic`).
- **Motional E (physics, not engine).**
  - δB_x needs E_z(y) = (U − δv_x)B0.
  - δB_z needs E_y(y) = −U δB_z plus a space charge ρ = ∇·E/4π.
  - Both are non-uniform tangential fields at the x faces. E1 in the README mentions only the δB_x case.

**Consequence for the falsification criterion:** it fails both ways.
- A difference does not show meandering.
- Agreement within a two-seed spread does not exclude it, because there is no power estimate and the confounders can
  cancel.

**What resolves it:** keep polarization only as a secondary contrast, and add the following.
- **(a) A local-θ_Bn superposition null model.**
  - Laminar runs at uniform θ_Bn = 90, 80, 70, 60, 45° with the same M_A, β and m_i/m_e.
  - Weight them by the measured PDF of θ_loc at the front of the δB_x run.
  - Meandering (multiple connection) is the excess of the turbulent run over this weighted sum.
- **(b) An L_c scan at fixed δB/B0.**
  - The θ_loc PDF depends on δB/B0 only, while the excursion of a field line across the front grows with L_c.
  - At least three L_c values over more than a factor 2 (e.g. 5, 10, 20, 40 d_i).
- **(c) Direct field-line labelling.**
  - In 2-D with in-plane B, label field lines by A_z, using Jokipii's conserved p_z + qA_z/c
    (`jokipii1993perpendicular`).
  - For each tracked electron, count the front intersections of its A_z contour and the encounters actually made.
  - Bin the gain by encounter number over the whole energized population.

### 2. The polarization is not conserved upstream at the planned L_c
Why it matters: the control must still be a control when the waves reach the shock.

- A linearly polarized parallel wave is the sum of L and R modes. With Hall dispersion, ω_R − ω_L ≈ k² d_i v_A, so
  the plane of polarization rotates about B0 at about k² d_i v_A / 2.
- The reviewer's estimate (not computed by the authors; kinetic corrections at β = 1 not checked):
  - L_c = 10 d_i (k d_i ≈ 0.63): about 0.2 rad per Ω_ci⁻¹, so δB_x turns into δB_z in about 8 Ω_ci⁻¹;
  - L_c = 20 d_i: about 30 Ω_ci⁻¹;
  - both are below or near the 20 Ω_ci⁻¹ run and the transit time.
- With a loaded upstream (E1), the age of the turbulence also grows with the age of the shock.
- **What resolves it:**
  - compute the rotation for the actual spectrum and β (Hall MHD, then a kinetic dispersion solver);
  - add the polarization angle and the δB_x/δB_z ratio at the shock vs time to N6;
  - use a larger L_c (the rate scales as L_c⁻²), or inject at the face (E2) with a short fixed age.

### 3. There is no controlling dimensionless number, and the runs are not placed relative to any threshold
Why it matters: transfer of the physics, and the meaning of a null result.

- **Field-line excursion against shock thickness, Ξ = ξ_x / L_sh.**
  - ⟨ξ_x²⟩ = Σ |δB_x,k|² / (B0² k²), dominated by the lowest k. With the spectrum of `guo2010effect` it scales
    roughly as (δB/B0)² L_c L_y, so the box width L_y is a physical parameter of the channel.
  - Guo & Giacalone had L_c ≈ 1024 d_i, and energetic electrons reached about 100 d_i upstream.
  - At L_c = 10–20 d_i and L_y = 50 d_i, ξ_x is plausibly of order the foot width or smaller (reviewer's estimate).
  - A null result at Ξ ≲ 1 would not test the picture.
- **Electron access along the field, v_te cos θ_loc / U1.**
  - v_te/v_A = √(β_e m_i / 2m_e) is about 7 at m_i/m_e = 100 and β_e = 1, against about 30 at 1836.
  - With U1 = 3–10 v_A and cos θ_loc ≲ 0.3, most of the front is locally HTF-superluminal for thermal electrons at
    100, but not at the real mass ratio.
  - So m_i/m_e must be an explicit variable (100, 200, 400) at fixed Ξ.
- **Thresholds at simulated and real parameters:**
  - whistler critical number M_w = 0.5 √(m_i/m_e) cos θ_loc;
  - ripple onset M_A ≈ 3.5 (`trotta2019electron`);
  - Buneman/ESW trapping (`hoshino2002nonthermal`; `bohdan2017electron` Eq. 2: about 26 at m_i/m_e = 100);
  - M_A / √β_e (`amano2010critical`).
  - The M_A ≈ 30 runs at m_i/m_e = 100 sit above the surfing threshold, which is an artefact at the real mass ratio.
  - A table of where every run sits, at both mass ratios, must precede fixing the matrix.

### 4. There is no minimal analytic model, so there is no number to test
- A short model would give a falsifiable prediction of the injection fraction and the index as functions of Ξ and
  v_te cos θ_loc / U1. Its parts:
  - the gain per encounter (SDA at θ_loc, Δv ≈ 2U / cos θ);
  - the encounters per field line (from Ξ and the convection time);
  - the escape probability per encounter.
- Or adopt a published number to falsify. The cleanest is `guo2012particle`: about 10 % of thermal test electrons
  above 15 keV at M_A ≈ 2 with δB²/B0² ≈ 0.3.
- Name every fitted O(1) constant.

### 5. N3 ("test electrons in the same run") does not test the test-particle picture
- Test electrons pushed in the self-consistent PIC fields obey the same equation of motion as the kinetic electrons.
  Apart from their missing current, they are statistically identical, so agreement says nothing.
- What distinguishes Guo & Giacalone from a PIC run is:
  - the fields: no electron-scale structure, a fluid cross-shock potential;
  - the initial energy: a 100 eV shell, 5.7 v_te.
- **What resolves it:**
  - (i) test electrons in the PIC fields, injected as Guo's shell;
  - (ii) test electrons in the same run's fields low-pass filtered to ion scales (k d_e ≪ 1), offline from stored
    fields, or in a hybrid run with the identical upstream;
  - (iii) the kinetic thermal electrons.
  - (iii) − (ii) is what kinetic electron physics changes.

### 6. The research question must be one question
- The three-parameter dependence and the mechanism attribution are separate papers.
- The matrix of "Approach": 3 M_A × 4 δB/B0 × 2 θ_Bn × 3 polarizations × 2 L_c × ≥ 2 seeds ≈ 288 runs, about 130
  node-days at 11 h per run.
- **Suggested single question:** "At a supercritical perpendicular shock with kinetic electrons, does multiple
  connection of field lines to the front inject thermal electrons beyond what the local-θ_Bn distribution of a laminar
  shock gives, and how does that excess scale with Ξ?"
- **Suggested regime:** M_A ≈ 3–5 at θ_Bn = 90°.
  - The laminar baseline there is near zero (SDA ≲ 4× gain, no Buneman surfing).
  - `guo2012particle` gives a prediction in this regime, and U1/v_te is most favourable.
  - Drop M_A ≈ 30, 60° and out-of-plane B0 from this topic.

### 7. Definitions that can create the result
- The cut γ − 1 ≥ 3 (`fulat2026pic`, U ≈ 0.26c) is meaningless at U = 0.03–0.1c.
- In turbulence, the downstream T_e varies across the front.
  - A sum of local Maxwellians fitted with one Maxwellian makes a spurious tail.
  - A bulk δv inflates temperatures unless they are taken in the local bulk frame.
- **To fix before the runs:**
  - local thermal fits (patches of a few d_i) in the local bulk frame;
  - a physical injection threshold (a gyroradius comparable to the thermal ion gyroradius, or the SSDA → DSA
    threshold of `amano2022theory`);
  - a downstream region in coordinates relative to the corrugated front;
  - proof that the conclusion survives a second reasonable definition.

### 8. Survey gaps that bear on novelty and framing
- **Jokipii & Giacalone 2007:** the origin of the mechanism; it needs its own note.
- **Qin, Kong & Zhang 2018** (ApJ, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/aac26f, arXiv:1802.08367): test-particle electrons at
  perpendicular shocks, with efficiency rising as turbulence decreases. This is the opposite trend to
  `guo2010effect`/`guo2015acceleration`, so the "test-particle picture" is not one prediction.
- **Xu, Li & Yao 2025** (ApJ 988, DOI 10.3847/1538-4357/ade23e): focused transport of electrons trapped between
  large-scale ripples. A competing non-meandering channel; the reviewer read only an abstract summary.
- **Routes to search further:**
  - Kong et al. (coronal-shock test electrons);
  - Zank et al. 2015 and le Roux et al. (shock + small-scale flux ropes);
  - Xu, Spitkovsky & Caprioli 2020 (1-D PIC, repeated SDA cycles) as the laminar kinetic baseline.
  - These were not checked beyond titles and abstracts.
- **Notes to read in full before the question is fixed:** `jokipii1993perpendicular`, `giacalone2005efficient`,
  `giacalone2005particle` (abstract only), `barnes1974large` (not read).
- **Expert consultation:** not done. The Pohl group stated that Alfvénic turbulence is their next step; asking them
  is the cheapest novelty check.

### 9. 3-D as proposed is unlikely to be decisive
- At about 600³ cells and m_i/m_e = 25–50, the box cannot hold both the shock-normal length (≳ 100 d_i for
  20 Ω_ci⁻¹) and several L_c ≥ 10 d_i across.
- E.g. 2000 × 330 × 330 cells with d_e = 4 cells gives about 16 d_i across at m_i/m_e = 25.
- At m_i/m_e = 25, v_te/v_A ≈ 3.5, which suppresses access (Major 3).
- **What resolves it:** state the 3-D box in d_i, L_c and Ξ, or reframe 3-D as a check of one 2-D result at matched Ξ.

### 10. The turbulence families do not match the control
- The polarization control exists only for T1, a slab of k ∥ B0 waves. That is a set of waves, not turbulence: a
  few modes in the energy range, with a large realization spread.
- Relaxed 2-D turbulence (T2) with in-plane B0 mixes δB_x, δB_y and δB_z, so there is no clean contrast.
- At δB/B0 = 1, linear polarization is not an exact state.
  - |B| varies by up to √2, and slow-mode compression appears within about L_c / v_A.
  - Only circular states are exact for a parallel spectrum, and they mix both polarizations.
- 2-D decay depends on the angle between k⊥ and δB, so the two polarizations need not decay alike.
- **What resolves it:** run N2 separately for each polarization; record δn/n, the |B| variance, the polarization and
  the spectrum at arrival (N6), with a preset tolerance for "equal upstream".

## Minor issues
- **N4:** at least 4–5 realizations per case, and a power estimate.
- **N2 covers upstream acceleration only.**
  - Map where each tracked electron gains: upstream, foot/ramp, or downstream.
  - Consider a compressed downstream periodic null.
- **θ_Bn = 60° with in-plane B0:** k ∥ B0 is no longer along y, so the δB_x/δB_z definition must be restated, or 60°
  dropped.
- **Out-of-plane B0 (the geometry of Fulat):** it is a different meandering realization; do not pool it with the
  in-plane runs.
- **Run length:** 20 Ω_ci⁻¹ against about 50 (`guo2010effect`) and about 100 (`guo2012particle`). State whether E_max
  is limited by time or by the box.
- **Spectra:** state the one-count level and the particles per cell of the tail.
- **Background:** a δB/B0 statistic upstream of interplanetary shocks is still missing.
- **`fulat2026pic`:** the note still says "no DOI", while the survey records a DOI.
