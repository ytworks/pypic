## Summary

The protocol asks whether runs L, M and H leave a population of energetic electrons that one-step reflection (S0), the numerical floor and the regions without a shock cannot explain, and whether that residual carries a hypothesis. The structure is right, and the algebra of S0 is correct. Three things let a false "hypothesis obtained" through:

- S0 is the plane-shock, instantaneous version of a signal that the curved-shock literature already corrects.
- Two documented classes of backstreaming electrons are missing from the "known" side.
- Nothing in C1–C6 varies the particle number, so a numerical effect at 2 particles per cell would pass.

## Major issues

**1. S0 uses one V_HT per electron, but the foot point decelerates during the reflection. The resulting error has the signature of K1.**
- For a sphere, d ln V_HT/dt = −V_n tan²θ_Bn / R. With R = 100 d_e this is 0.0065 ω_pe at 60° and 0.016 ω_pe at 70°.
- A mirrored electron stays about 2.7 electron gyro-periods in the layer (`savoini2010origin` Sect. 3.2.1, at M_A = 3, V_sh/c ≈ 0.23, v_te/c = 0.1). That is 35–60/ω_pe here.
- So V_HT changes by 20–40 % during one reflection at 60°, and by order unity at 70°. It doubles at R = 50. These are my estimates; the layer width is not yet measured.
- The claim "the energies are a firm prediction" (Sect. 3.1) therefore does not hold in the 50–70° band, which is the only band where S0 reaches γ ≥ 1.5.
- This is published: `vandas1994acceleration` gives a mean gain of about 1/4 of the plane value for R_c/d = 10²–10³. These runs have R/d ≈ 10–40.
- B1 inverts the map with V_HT at the exit. An electron that entered at a higher V_HT then looks as if it "gained at a higher θ_Bn and exceeds the S0 maximum of its exit latitude". That is the definition of K1.
- B1 tolerates an error of roughly 0.15 c in V_HT through E_th (my estimate). That is marginal at 60° and insufficient at 65–75°.
- The error repeats in both hemispheres, all quadrants and a second seed, so C1 does not catch it.
- **Resolves it:**
  - replace S0 by S0′: guiding-centre orbits in the fitted, time-dependent front with the measured ramp profile;
  - or, at minimum, accept an electron in B1 if it is explained for any V_HT that its field line had during [t_r − τ_dwell, t_r];
  - report the number ε = V_n tan²θ_Bn τ_dwell / R per band;
  - take the foot point on the mid-ramp of |B| where b ≥ 1.5, not on the 1.5 n₀ density contour.

**2. Two published classes are missing from the known side and would land in the residual with positive signatures.**
- `savoini2010origin` is a full-PIC run at nearly the parameters of L. It finds 70 % mirrored, 20 % trapped in the parallel electrostatic well of the overshoot (μ not conserved, μ/μ₀ ≈ 4), and 10 % leaked from downstream (entry near 88°, exit near 48°, all exits below about 60°).
- Leaked electrons lie inside the loss cone, so they fail B1. They meet K1 and "magnetic moment broken".
- **Resolves it:** add two known classes with their own tests.
  - S1, leakage: inside the loss cone, with an HT-frame energy consistent with the measured downstream T_e; the electron was downstream in an earlier dump.
  - S2, overshoot trapping: dwell much longer than a few gyro-periods, bounces in E_∥.
  - The residual is what S0′, S1 and S2 leave.
- Also known, to be named in Sect. 3.4: relaxation of the reflected beam by Langmuir waves upstream. This spreads the S0 energies; test it as a spread that grows with distance from the front.
- Also known: a non-stationary HT potential (reformation above about 72° in `savoini2010origin`).
- On your other candidates:
  - A finite steady potential does not change the energies. That claim is correct.
  - Multiple reflections by free flight cannot occur on a convex front with straight upstream field lines. K2 therefore needs upstream scattering, physical or numerical.

**3. K1 is defined by the foot-point θ_Bn, which changes without any transport.**
- A field line first touches the front at 90° and its foot then moves poleward (sin ϑ = ρ/R). Going from 75° to 55° takes only an 18 % growth of R.
- This is published (`savoini2001twodimensional` Sect. 3.4, `kong2016electron`, `giacalone2017acceleration`).
- In axisymmetric fields the canonical angular momentum is conserved. An electron then returns upstream on its own flux surface: ρ of the guiding centre equals ρ at birth, within about a gyroradius.
- **Resolves it:** redefine K1 as transport across field lines.
  - Measure Δρ = ρ_gc(now) − ρ(birth cell, from `id // 2`) for every upstream electron of `hot_e`.
  - Compare it with the same distribution in the null run. This is a new null, N5: numerical cross-field diffusion.
  - It needs no tracks. It is the only version of K1 that the cited works have not shown.

**4. Nothing separates a 2-particle-per-cell effect from physics.**
- N3 measures ν_num on thermal electrons (v ≤ 0.2 c). The census electrons move at 0.75–0.94 c, and noise scattering falls steeply with speed.
- The null run holds no electron with γ ≥ 1.5, so N1 is "10 times zero" and N3 says nothing about B1 flight times.
- The null run is also at n₀, B₀ and T₀, not at the state of the layer.
- A second seed (C1) repeats the noise level. It does not test it.
- **Resolves it:**
  - a. measure pitch-angle and energy diffusion of energetic electrons in situ: match ids between successive dumps of `hot_e` during upstream free flight in L, in bins of speed;
  - b. compare the upstream fluctuation power of L and of the null run at the same place;
  - c. make "obtained" depend on a pair of smaller runs (384³, `rm` halved) at 2 and at 8 or more particles per cell, with the same residual fraction in both. This pair also gives a second R/L.
  - Without c the answer is at most "candidate".

**5. C1 has no term for the error rate of B1, and C3 does not exclude the driver along field lines.**
- With 3 × 10⁴–2 × 10⁵ S0 electrons, a 1 % failure rate of B1 gives 300–2000 "residual" electrons. That is above every threshold of C1.
- **Add to C1:** the residual exceeds 3 times (measured false-negative rate of B1) × (S0 count). Measure the rate on tracked single reflections with μ conserved.
- Define "upper bound of the reference" for upstream electrons. A Poisson 3σ is meaningless against a systematic factor.
- Condition (b) of Sect. 4 is radial, but a field line with foot at ϑ threads the interior down to r = ρ. Leaked or trapped electrons on lines with ρ < R_debris may come from the debris interface.
- **Add to C3 for every non-reflected electron:** ρ_gc > ρ_debris,max(t), and first appearance in `hot_e` at a time and place inside the valid domain.
- An electron whose back-traced crossing lies outside the fitted time range, or with v_∥ − V_HT < 0.1 c, is "undetermined", not residual.

**6. Valid domain: replacement numbers (Sect. 4).**
- **Density threshold 1.5 n₀:** keep it. The bin noise is 6–13 %. Repeat with 1.3 and 1.7; use a band only if R moves by less than 1 d_i and V_HT by less than 10 %.
- **(b) lead over the debris:** replace "2 d_i" by two conditions.
  - The debris edge lies behind the position of B_max.
  - The lead is at least 0.7 V_n/Ω_ci, the foot length: 2 d_i in L, 3.4 d_i in M, 4.9 d_i in H at V_n = 0.2 c.
  - A fixed 2 d_i puts debris inside the interval where b is measured (3 d_i behind the front).
  - Use `n:de` as well as `n:di`, and report the edge for 0.03 and 0.3 n₀.
- **(c) factor 1.5:** keep it as a conservative cut.
  - Add V_n − 2σ_V > 1.1 v_f. I estimate σ_V ≈ 0.01–0.017 c from five outputs with a position error of 0.5–1 d_e.
  - Compute v_f with the measured, heated upstream temperatures.
  - Add a consistency check: the normal velocity of the ambient ions behind the front is within 30 % of V_n (1 − n₀/n).
- **(d) five outputs** are 1.65 / 0.96 / 0.66 Ω_ci⁻¹ in L / M / H. Replace by 2 Ω_ci⁻¹: 6, 11 and 15 outputs.
- **(e) window:**
  - Replace the lower bound 0.3 R_B by an age of 2 Ω_ci⁻¹. Nominally t ≥ 112, 192, 278 and R ≈ 45, 57, 72 d_e.
  - Replace the upper bound by R ≤ 192 − 8 − V_n/Ω_ci: about 173, 165, 156 d_e.
- **Front fit:** a bound on the rms of R does not bound R_ϑ. A 10 % distortion per radian changes V_HT by 27 % at 70°. Use a band for B1 only if V_HT from the fit and from local differences agree within 15 %, and the four quadrants agree within 15 %.

## Minor issues

- **b_max as a global maximum** makes the loss-cone test nearly empty. That is conservative. Say so, and report B1 also with the local b(ϑ, t).
- **E_th = 20 kT** hides a pre-heated seed population. Make the comparison of the pre-reflection energies with the flux-weighted Maxwell–Jüttner a criterion, not only a check.
- **N1 with a zero floor:** use max(count, 3).
- **N3 on thermal electrons** is still useful for B2. If ν_num τ ≈ 0.04–0.4 for thermal electrons in the layer (my rough estimate), the loss cone is filled numerically and the reflected count changes. Report it per speed bin.
- **N4:** five samples per gyration in the compressed field of L, and a noise field that decorrelates between samples, may fail the 10 % closure for sampling reasons. The fallback (μ and encounters) is right.
- **C3 "origin is ambient":** say whether electrons injected at the faces count.
- **C4:** require the hypothesis in dimensionless form (ε or R/L, V_HT/v_te, V_sh/c, m_i/m_e) with its value for a remnant. At V_sh/c ≈ 0.01 the 50–70° band carries no such energies.
- **L-s2:** commit the frozen definition of the residual (band, energy bin, class, expected count) before it starts.
- **Novelty (C5):** "the energy differs from the plane S0 value on a curved front" is published in adiabatic theory (`vandas1994acceleration`). What remains open is narrower than Sect. 10 suggests.

## Verdict

- **Baseline S0 as the known signal:** needs rethinking in one respect (finite dwell on a curved, expanding front) and needs two classes added. The kinematics as written is correct.
- **Shock definition and valid domain:** needs the replacement numbers above. The approach is sound.
- **Numerical floor N1–N4:** needs evidence. It does not constrain the energetic electrons and has no particle-number control.
- **Criteria C1–C6:** not yet sufficient. With changes 6–8 the three-way verdict is honest.
- **The owner's question:** with the present set, "obtained" should not be reachable. The realistic best outcome is "candidate", with K1 by Δρ as the most promising test because it is exact in axisymmetry and cheap. The null run of L can start once changes 1–5 are in the configs.
